## Introduction

Hey there! May's birthstone is **Emerald** (the main one!) and **Jade** (the classic alternative). Emerald is one of the **"Big Four Gemstones"** alongside diamonds, rubies, and sapphires, and its deep green color symbolizes **"good fortune, love, and eternal youth."** It's a vibrant green stone, full of life, just perfect for anyone born in May when the new greenery is so beautiful!

## What are May's Birthstones?

For May's birthstone, **Emerald** is the main star, and **Jade** is recognized as the classic alternative. In the **2021 birthstone revision**, **Jade** was officially added to the list. (Though in Japan, it's been considered a parallel birthstone for May for a long, long time!)

## Meanings and Stone Meanings

Emerald's stone meanings are **"good fortune, love, eternal youth, and prosperity."** It's super famous as the **gemstone Cleopatra adored**, and the emerald mines in ancient Egypt were even called **"Cleopatra's Mines."** How cool is that?

> **Heads up!** The effects attributed to power stones are cultural and symbolic. They aren't medically or scientifically guaranteed, okay?

Since ancient times, it's been called the **"Stone of Truth,"** and there's even a legend that if you look at an emerald in front of someone telling a lie, its color will **change**! In the Bible, it appears as one of the **12 gemstones adorning the walls of the New Jerusalem.** **Jade**, known as "gyoku" in the East, has been revered in China and Japan for over 5,000 years as a symbol of **"virtue, prosperity, and longevity."**

## Fun Facts and Origins

-   **Etymology**: From the Greek word *smaragdos* (meaning "green stone").
-   **Mohs Hardness**: 7.5 to 8, but it can be brittle due to inclusions.
-   **Big Four Gemstones**: One of diamond, ruby, sapphire, and emerald.
-   **Beryl Mineral**: Part of the beryl family, just like aquamarine and morganite! The **chromium** content gives it its beautiful green color.
-   **Cleopatra's Beloved Stone**: In the 1st century BC, she owned mines named after herself!
-   **Colombian Origin**: Today, the highest quality emeralds come from Colombia's Muzo mine.
-   **Jade**: A stone cherished by Japanese people since the Jomon period. Jade from Itoigawa, Niigata, is a primary source of Japanese jade and was designated as the **National Stone** in 2016.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Why is Emerald said to be "fragile"?**

A. Emeralds often contain many **natural inclusions**, which create tiny fissures, making their structure vulnerable to impact. Most emeralds on the market undergo **oil treatment** to make these inclusions less visible, so you should avoid ultrasonic cleaning, strong detergents, and hot water, as they can degrade this treatment.

**Q2. What's the difference between Jade and Jadeite/Nephrite?**

A. Jade is the **general term for '翡翠'**, and mineralogically, it's divided into two types: **Jadeite ('hard jade')** and **Nephrite ('soft jade')**. Most Japanese jade is **Jadeite**, while much of ancient Chinese jade was **Nephrite**. For more info, check out [The Meanings and Effects of Jade](/blog/jade/).

**Q3. What stones pair well with Emerald?**

A. Popular pairings include **[Clear Quartz](/blog/clear-quartz/)** (for amplification and purification), **[Rose Quartz](/blog/rose-quartz-meaning/)** (for harmony and love), **[Aquamarine](/blog/aquamarine/)** (its sister stone!), and **[Aventurine](/blog/aventurine/)** (another green healing stone).

**Q4. Does it look strange for men to wear green stones?**

A. Not at all! In the East, Jade has a long history of being cherished by men, from Chinese emperors to samurai, as a **symbol of male virtue**, making it a very suitable stone for men. Emeralds can also add a touch of elegance when incorporated into **tie pins or cufflinks**.

**Q5. Is a May birthstone a good Mother's Day gift?**

A. Absolutely, especially if your **mom was born in May**! Jade, as a symbol of **"longevity and prosperity,"** is one of the most traditional and thoughtful choices for a gift to an elder.
