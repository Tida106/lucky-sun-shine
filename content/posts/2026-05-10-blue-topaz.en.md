Blue Topaz is a stone that holds the clear blue of the sky. In ancient Greece and Rome, topaz was called the "**Stone of Strength and Truth**" and was even a gem dedicated to the sun god Apollo. ## <span id="meaning"></span>Gemstone Language and Historical/Cultural Background

Its gemstone language speaks of "**Sincerity, Friendship, and Communication**."

In ancient Greece, the name "Topaz" is said to originate from "**Topazios Island**" (a legendary island in the Red Sea). During the Roman era, yellow topaz was highly prized as the color of the sun god Apollo. While natural blue topaz was extremely rare until the late 19th century, **radiation treatment technology** for coloring was established in the latter half of the 20th century, leading to its widespread popularity as a blue gemstone. In Japan, it is a November birthstone, a representative gem for those born in autumn, alongside Citrine.

## <span id="faq"></span>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Is radiation treatment safe?**

A. Yes, it is. The radiation treatment for blue topaz is a **standard and recognized process in the industry**, and the stones are stored until any residual radioactivity falls below safety standards before entering the jewelry market. It is safe to wear daily.

**Q2. Does natural blue topaz exist?**

A. It exists very rarely, but the color is usually faint, and it's hardly available in the market. It's practical to understand that **vibrant blue topaz is almost always treated** and enjoy it with that knowledge.

**Q3. Is London Blue the most expensive?**

A. Generally, Swiss Blue and London Blue are popular, and depending on size and clarity, they can be more expensive than Sky Blue. **It's perfectly fine to choose based on your preference and budget.**

**Q4. As a November birthstone, how does it differ from Citrine?**

A. Citrine is a **warm-toned (yellow to orange) gemstone symbolizing wealth and confidence**, while Blue Topaz is a **cool-toned gemstone symbolizing sincerity and intellect**. People often choose between them based on their personality or the occasion, and some even own both.

**Q5. Are there other stones for communication?**
