## Meaning and Effects of Each Stone

### Main Stone: Tiger's Eye (Golden Brown Stripes)

**Tiger's Eye**, with its tiger-like striped pattern, is a stone of **"decision and insight."** It supports you through all phases of a job change, including **decision-making, interviews, and salary negotiations.**

- **Activating Decision-Making Power**: Choosing one option from many
- **Insight**: Understanding the hiring side's intentions
- **Focus at Critical Moments**: During interviews and final decisions

For more details, please refer to [Tiger's Eye Meaning, Stone Language, and Effects](/blog/tigers-eye/).

### Iolite (Blue-Violet)

Known as the **"Viking's Compass,"** this stone helps guide your path. It was used to determine the sun's position at sea, making it a symbol of **"showing the way forward."** It's a great charm for those **uncertain about their industry or profession.**

### Carnelian (Vermillion Red)

A stone of **"action and achievement."** It encourages **courage to take a step,** helping you move from just **"thinking about" to "acting on"** your job change. For more details, please refer to [Carnelian Meaning, Stone Language, and Effects](/blog/carnelian/).

### Lapis Lazuli (Deep Blue with Gold Flecks)

The **"stone of discerning truth."** It empowers you when you confront the fundamental question: **"What work do I truly want to do?"** For more details, please refer to [Lapis Lazuli Meaning and Effects](/blog/lapis-lazuli/).

### Prehnite (Pale Green)

A cleansing stone that **"releases the unnecessary and invites new flows."** It's a charm for the transition period, helping you **let go of attachments to your previous job** and **open up to a new environment.**

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Is it okay to wear a bracelet to an interview?**

A. It's fine if it's **small and subtly colored.** The brown of Tiger's Eye **blends well with business suits.** If you're concerned, **wrap a tumbled stone in a handkerchief and put it in your pocket.**

**Q2. What if my job search is dragging on and I feel tired?**

A. It's important to take time to rest with **Amethyst (for mental stability) plus Rose Quartz (for self-love).** It's said that by using **Prehnite** to **"release attachments to your previous job,"** new opportunities will begin to flow.

**Q3. What if I want to give one as a gift?**

A. For a friend actively job hunting, a **Tiger's Eye bracelet** is a classic. For an independence celebration, a **Lapis Lazuli pendant**, and for a new beginning, an **Iolite tumbled stone** are popular choices.

**Q4. What if I'm thinking about going independent or starting a business?**

A. The three-stone set of **Tiger's Eye (decision) plus Pyrite (entrepreneurial shield) plus Citrine (business prosperity)** is a charm for entrepreneurs. On the day of company establishment, add **Morion** for dispelling negative energy.

**Q5. How often should I cleanse my stones?**

A. **Once every two weeks**, and always the day before an interview. **On clear quartz chips, moonlight bathing, or white sage** are safe methods. Avoid prolonged direct sunlight for Iolite and Prehnite.
