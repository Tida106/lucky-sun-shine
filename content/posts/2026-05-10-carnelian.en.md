Carnelian is a stone that holds a vivid scarlet red, much like a setting sun. In ancient Egypt, it was extensively used as **royal burial adornments**, embedded in Tutankhamun's pectoral and rings. The anecdote of **Napoleon carrying a carnelian with his seal** on the battlefield is also famous, and it has been passed down through generations worldwide as "the **stone that encourages action and achievement**." ## <span id="basic"></span>Basic Information and Mineral Data

| Item | Content |

| Stone Name | Carnelian |
| Japanese Name | 紅玉髄 (Benigyokuzui - Red Chalcedony) |
| Mineral Species | A variety of Chalcedony |
| Chemical Composition | SiO₂ (trace amounts of iron) |
| Crystal System | Trigonal (Cryptocrystalline) |
| Main Producing Regions | Brazil, India, Uruguay, Madagascar |
| Mohs Hardness | 6.5 to 7 |
| Color | Orange to Reddish-brown |
| Corresponding Chakras | Second (Sacral) / First (Root) |

Its orange to red coloration is a natural hue caused by **trace amounts of iron oxide**. Many carnelian stones on the market are standard treated items, where the rough stones are heat-treated to unify their color. Its etymology comes from the Latin word *cornum* (the fruit of the Cornelian cherry).

## <span id="meaning"></span>Stone Properties, History, and Cultural Background

Its stone properties are "**action, victory, and achievement**."

In ancient Egypt, it was a symbol of regeneration and resurrection, known as the "**stone of the setting sun**." A large number of carnelian adornments were found in Tutankhamun's tomb, where they were buried as amulets for his journey to the afterlife. In ancient Rome, it was widely used as a **signet stone**, symbolizing authority as the stone used to seal emperors' decrees. It is said that Napoleon also wore a carnelian with his seal on the battlefield.

> Source: GIA Carnelian Reference, various archaeological documents.

## <span id="faq"></span>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. What's the difference between Carnelian and Sardonyx?**

A. Both are varieties of chalcedony. **Carnelian is characterized by its uniform scarlet red, while Sardonyx features red and white striped patterns**. Sardonyx is an ancient gemstone that even appears in the Old Testament and is known as the birthstone for August.

**Q2. I've heard that heat-treated items are common.**

A. Yes, that's correct. The majority of items on the market are **heat-treated to unify their color**, which is a standard treatment accepted by the industry. Completely untreated natural carnelian has a slightly darker hue and is rarely circulated in today's market.

**Q3. Is it effective for continuing diets and exercise?**

**Q4. Can it be used by both men and women?**
