## <span id="basic"></span>Basic Information and Mineral Data

| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| Stone Name | Citrine (Yellow Quartz) |
| Japanese Name | 黄水晶 (Yellow Quartz) |
| Mineral Species | Quartz |
| Chemical Composition | SiO₂ (contains trace amounts of iron) |
| Crystal System | Trigonal |
| Main Sources | Brazil, Bolivia, Madagascar, Russia |
| Mohs Hardness | 7 |
| Color | Pale Yellow to Deep Orange |
| Associated Chakra | Third (Solar Plexus) |

Its etymology comes from the French word **"citron" (lemon)**. Trace amounts of iron within the stone react to natural radiation underground, causing it to display its yellow coloration.

## <span id="meaning"></span>Stone Keywords and Historical and Cultural Background

Its stone keywords are "**Prosperity, Hope, and Confidence**." In ancient Greece and Rome, it was cherished as an amulet, and in medieval Europe, merchants invariably carried it in their wallets as a "talisman for business prosperity." In Chinese Feng Shui, there is a traditional practice of placing a rough citrine stone in the southwest corner of the living room as a stone of financial luck.

## <span id="natural"></span>Natural Citrine versus Heat-Treated Citrine

| Type | Color | Price | Availability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Natural Citrine | Pale Yellow to Lemon Yellow | Higher | Limited |
| Heat-Treated Citrine | Deep Orange to Reddish-Brown | Affordable | Widespread |

Heat-treated citrine is an **industry-standard treated product** created by heating inexpensive amethyst from Brazil at 350-560°C. A guideline for distinguishing them by color is: "pale and natural = natural / deep and uniform orange = heat-treated."

## <span id="faq"></span>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Is there a difference in effect between natural and heat-treated citrine?**

A. Culturally and symbolically, they are considered equivalent. The choice depends on personal preference for color and rarity, or price. For daily use, an affordable heat-treated piece is often sufficient.

**Q2. When should I replace the citrine in my wallet?**

A. There's no strict deadline, but many people make it a habit to **replace it with gratitude** around the New Year or during a New Moon.

**Q3. Won't a yellow bracelet look too flashy?**

A. If you choose a pale-colored citrine, it's easy for daily wear and suits both men and women. Pairing it with a leather strap or wood beads can also give it a casual touch.

**Q4. Is ametrine different from citrine?**

A. Ametrine is a natural stone that is a blend of amethyst and citrine, often in roughly equal parts. It symbolizes the balance between "spirituality and materiality" and is typically more expensive than citrine alone.

**Q5. Are there other stones for financial luck?**

A. Rutilated Quartz, Tiger's Eye, Pyrite, and Jade are common choices. For more details, please refer to the financial luck section of our [Comprehensive Guide to Power Stones by Purpose](/blog/luck-powerstones-complete-guide/).
