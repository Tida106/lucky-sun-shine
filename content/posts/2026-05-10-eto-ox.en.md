## Introduction

The "Ox" (Ushi) is the second sign of the Chinese zodiac, deeply intertwined with agricultural culture. From the image of an ox silently pulling heavy loads, it has become a symbol of honesty, perseverance, and steadfastness. It has also been cherished since ancient times as the messenger of **Sugawara no Michizane**, the deity of scholarship. The guardian stone said to accompany those born in the Year of the Ox is **Agate (Menou)**, with its layered, striped patterns reminiscent of the earth itself. It is said to be the stone that most resonates with those who cherish family and connections, and who nurture things over long periods.

## Personality and Fortune of Those Born in the Year of the Ox

Those born in the Year of the Ox are often referred to as the "unsung heroes" among the twelve zodiac signs. They are said to possess a tenacious strength to see things through once decided, earning them deep trust from those around them.

In their careers, it's not flashy bursts of energy but rather their **talent for long-term accumulation** that shines. They are often described as types who truly excel in fields where "time is on their side," such as craftsmanship, research, inheriting a family business, or supporting local communities.

On the other hand, they can be prone to stubbornness and resistance to change, sometimes lagging behind new trends. They also have a habit of suppressing their emotions, viewing "patience" as too much of a virtue, which means mental and physical fatigue can often go unnoticed in those born in the Year of the Ox.

Among the twelve zodiac signs, the Ox carries a strong "earth" energy and governs the transitional hours from night to morning, as represented by Ushimitsu-doki (2-3 AM). It is believed that the true flow of luck for those born in the Year of the Ox follows a rhythm of slow germination, like a **seed growing underground**, before gradually sprouting.

## Why Agate is a Good Match

Agate is a type of chalcedony that grows in layers over thousands, sometimes tens of thousands of years, and its striped patterns are often called the "annual rings of the earth." In ancient Mesopotamia and Egypt, it was used as a stone symbolizing the protection of homes and family bonds. In Japan, it has been unearthed in many ancient burial mounds as magatama (curved beads), showing its long history of accompanying human life.

The "earth" energy governed by those born in the Year of the Ox and the "time spent layering" symbolized by Agate are said to deeply resonate. It's not about making big changes all at once, but rather becoming stronger by gradually adding thin layers – this very growth pattern has been described as a microcosm of the life journey of those born in the Year of the Ox.

Furthermore, Agate is also known as a stone that governs "family love, matchmaking, and bonds." It is the stone that most naturally aligns with the true nature of those born in the Year of the Ox, who cherish family and friends and cultivate connections spanning generations. It is also considered a presence that can transform the hardships of perseverance into the 'joy of nurturing.'

## Compatible Stones to Carry Together

-   **Jade**: Known in the East as the "stone of virtue," it is said to bring prosperity to modest and honest individuals. It is believed to support those born in the Year of the Ox in connecting their honesty to social recognition.
-   **Hematite**: A heavy, dark stone containing iron, it symbolizes the power to move reality and tenacity. It is believed to further support the steadfast efforts of the Ox when grounded action is needed.
-   **Smoky Quartz**: A brown quartz that governs earthy grounding. It is said to help bring someone back to their roots when they feel detached due to overthinking or excessive patience.

## Good Luck Actions for Those Born in the Year of the Ox

-   **Clothing Colors**: Earth tones like brown, beige, and khaki as a base, with white as an accent color. This color scheme is traditionally favored to harmonize earth energy and add a touch of purity.
-   **Wallet**: Durable genuine leather (cowhide). Those born in the Year of the Ox have a good compatibility with "nurturing and using" items, and it's said that a longer replacement cycle helps to establish financial luck.
-   **Pilgrimage Sites**: Tenmangu shrines, such as Dazaifu Tenmangu and Kitano Tenmangu, which enshrine Sugawara no Michizane. The custom of stroking the "**Nade-Ushi**" (stroking ox statues) within the shrine grounds is believed to strengthen the protection for those born in the Year of the Ox.
-   **Auspicious Days**: Days of "**Kichu (Tsuchinoe-Ushi)**" or Ox days in general are considered days when the foundation for those born in the Year of the Ox is stabilized. Some people choose these days to start studying for a new qualification.
-   **Habits**: A glass of plain hot water immediately after waking up, and a gratitude note before bed. These routines, which restore the 'earth's calm' at the beginning and end of the day, are said to enhance the luck of the Ox.

## Unlucky Years, Milestone Years, and How to Use Stones

For those born in the Year of the Ox, the "**Toshi Otoko / Toshi Onna**" years, which occur every 12 years (their own zodiac year), are considered the "eve of harvest" years when what they've built up is likely to take shape. Rather than rushing to change something, it's said to be good to focus on carefully finishing what they are currently cultivating and to renew their awareness of "layering" by wearing Agate.

In the main unlucky years (Hon-yaku, such as 42 for men and 33 for women by traditional Japanese reckoning), the tendency to over-exert oneself is said to often lead to negative outcomes. Many people use Hematite as an aid to maintain balance. It is said that spending this period by "**shifting to a supportive role**" rather than "pushing harder" is key to passing these unlucky years peacefully.

Conversely, the **Year of the Goat/Sheep (Hitsuji-doshi)**, which is opposite to one's own zodiac sign, is a year when relationships can be turbulent. It is believed that adding Rose Quartz and Crystal alongside Agate can supplement the energy of harmony and bring stability.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q. Should I buy a new stone in my zodiac year?**
A. It's not mandatory, but the custom of marking this once-every-12-years milestone with a stone has been passed down since ancient times. It's common to acquire a new Agate and keep the old stone close with gratitude.

**Q. Should I only have one zodiac stone?**
A. It's preferred to have one main Agate, accompanied by two to three compatible stones. Since those born in the Year of the Ox have a compatibility with "nurturing things for a long time," it is said that the longer you continue to use a stone you've welcomed, the deeper your connection with it becomes.

**Q. Is it bad to own stones of other zodiac signs?**
A. It is not considered an issue. It has long been recommended to carry stones of other zodiac signs to complement qualities you might lack, such as using Tiger's Eye (for the Year of the Tiger) to enhance decisiveness.

**Q. Agate comes in many colors, but which one is best for the Year of the Ox?**
A. Earthy colors like brown and gray are said to resonate most naturally with those born in the Year of the Ox. Some people also use different colors depending on the situation, for example, Blue Agate to enhance intellect or Red Agate to boost motivation.
