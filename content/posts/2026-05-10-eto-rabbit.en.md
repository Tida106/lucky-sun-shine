## Introduction

The "Rabbit" (卯) is the fourth sign in the Chinese zodiac. Traditionally seen as a sacred creature dwelling on the moon, it symbolizes leaps of progress, harmony, gentleness, and the fostering of connections. In Japan, the Rabbit appears in myths like "The White Rabbit of Inaba," often playing a role in creating bonds. For those born in the Year of the Rabbit, the most harmonizing guardian stone is **Rose Quartz**, its pale pink glow a crystal embodiment of gentleness itself. Sharing the common traits of moonlight and love, it's said to be a stone that quietly supports the harmonious nature inherent in Rabbit-born individuals.

## Personality and Fortune of Those Born in the Year of the Rabbit

Rabbit-born individuals are considered the most gentle and soft-spoken of all the zodiac signs, known for their approachable demeanor and high empathy. With a knack for listening and caring for everyone, they naturally become the "center of connections," where people gather around them.

In their careers, Rabbit-born individuals excel in roles where interpersonal relationships directly impact outcomes, such as counseling, education, customer service, healthcare, and creative fields. And just like a rabbit gracefully leaping towards the moon, they possess the **innate ability to seize opportunities**, making their lives prone to years of not just stability, but also significant leaps forward.

However, their excessive gentleness can sometimes lead them to suppress their own opinions or become exhausted by carrying others' emotions. Tendencies like "being unable to refuse" or "comparing themselves to others and feeling down" are patterns that Rabbit-born individuals are encouraged to consciously manage.

Among the twelve zodiac signs, the Rabbit carries the "Wood" element, governing the East, spring, and the dawn (5 AM to 7 AM). Like **tender new sprouts**, it symbolizes a season where gentleness and forward momentum coexist, and this very balance is said to be the core of the Rabbit's fortune.

## Why Rose Quartz is a Perfect Match

Rose Quartz has been known since ancient times as the "stone of love" and the "stone of beauty." In Greek mythology, it's associated with Aphrodite, symbolizing love and harmony. In Japan, it's cherished as a representative stone for matchmaking and improving interpersonal luck, believed to fill people with a quiet energy, much like the light of the moon.

What deeply resonates with Rabbit-born individuals is Rose Quartz's sensitivity towards "**gentleness for oneself and others**." It's often said that while Rabbit-born individuals possess kindness for others, they tend to put their own well-being last. Rose Quartz, as a symbol of "**love that first fulfills oneself**," is believed to help balance the Rabbit's tendency towards self-sacrifice.

Furthermore, the combination of the moon and the rabbit is a widespread tradition throughout East Asia, beautifully aligning with the fact that Rose Quartz has historically been called the "stone that holds moonlight." The practice of purifying the stone according to the moon's phases is said to be a particularly compatible maintenance method for those born in the Year of the Rabbit.

## Companion Stones to Carry

-   **Moonstone**: This milky-white feldspar is considered the stone of the moon goddess. Believed to harmonize with the moon's cycles and gently stabilize emotional fluctuations, it's said to be a supportive companion for the delicate heart of Rabbit-born individuals.
-   **Amethyst**: A purple quartz. It governs intuition and mental stability, purifying a mind weary from human relationships. For Rabbit-born individuals, it's known as the "deep breath" stone that helps them regain their sense of self when feeling "swallowed by others."
-   **Kunzite**: A pale pink "stone of unconditional love." Said to possess an even finer frequency than rose quartz, it fosters both self-love and love for others simultaneously, offering a deeper lesson in love for Rabbit-born individuals.

## Lucky Charms and Actions for Rabbit-Born Individuals

-   **Clothing Colors**: Pastel pink, white, and sprout green. Rabbit-born individuals are highly compatible with "spring colors," and soft, bright palettes are said to harmonize the flow of their interpersonal relationships.
-   **Wallets**: Pink or white tones. Lighter, mini wallets are also a good match for Rabbits, promoting agility while attracting good connections.
-   **Shrine Visits**: Kyoto's Okazaki Shrine, Tokyo's Omiya Hachimangu, and Nara's Tsuki Shrine (which features guardian rabbits instead of lions) are all excellent choices, as they honor rabbits as divine messengers. **Visiting during a full moon night** is believed to strengthen the protection for Rabbit-born individuals.
-   **Auspicious Rhythms**: The "Day of the Rabbit" (卯の日) coinciding with a full moon is considered the strongest day for matchmaking and making wishes. It's ideal for writing letters, contacting people, or taking steps in dating or marriage endeavors.
-   **Habit**: The "Moon Purification" habit of warming a rose quartz in your palm before sleep and reflecting on your day. This practice is said to be particularly beneficial for Rabbit-born individuals in organizing their emotions.

## Navigating Unlucky Years and Milestones with Stones

For Rabbit-born individuals, the "lucky year" (年男・年女) that comes once every 12 years is sometimes referred to as the "**Year of the Moon**." This period often marks a milestone where internal balance and external leaps forward can occur simultaneously. It's a time when "renewals of connections" frequently happen—be it new relationships, a new home, or marriage—and many choose to acquire a new rose quartz to wear.

During unlucky years (本厄, e.g., age 42 for men, 33 for women, in traditional Japanese counting), Rabbit-born individuals are prone to exhaustion from bearing others' emotions. Traditionally, moonstone is used alongside rose quartz to gently maintain emotional equilibrium. "**Learning to say no**" is often highlighted as a theme for these years.

The zodiac sign directly opposite the Rabbit is the **Rooster (酉)**. The Year of the Rooster is often one where Rabbit-born individuals can get overwhelmed by busyness and lose their sense of calm. During this year, keeping amethyst and rose quartz close, and consciously taking time to gaze at the moon, is said to bring stability.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q. Should I buy a new stone in my lucky zodiac year?**
A. The Year of the Rabbit is often a time for "renewing connections," so many people choose to welcome a new rose quartz. Old stones can be purified with gratitude and traditionally passed on to family or close friends.

**Q. Should I only carry one zodiac stone?**
A. Not at all! It's popular to combine your main rose quartz with moon-related or purple stones like moonstone and amethyst. Rabbit-born individuals have a good affinity for "layering stones to gently find balance."

**Q. Is it bad to carry stones from other zodiac signs?**
A. Not at all! For situations where gentleness struggles to translate into action, it's traditionally recommended to carry stones that complement your temperament, such as a Tiger's Eye from the Year of the Tiger to enhance decisiveness.

**Q. I heard rose quartz can fade. Is that true?**
A. Yes, rose quartz can sometimes lose its color if exposed to direct sunlight for extended periods. Purification by moonlight is traditionally preferred for this reason, which perfectly aligns with the Rabbit's affinity for the moon!
