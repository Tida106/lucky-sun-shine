## Introduction

The "Snake" (巳, Hebi) is the sixth of the twelve Chinese zodiac signs, and its ability to repeatedly shed its skin and be reborn has made it a symbol of **regeneration, transformation, wisdom, and financial fortune**. Here in Japan, the snake is considered a messenger of **Benzaiten**, and white snakes, in particular, are revered as incarnations of the deity herself. They are worshipped in many shrines even today as guardians of money and the arts. And guess what, my dear Snake friends? The stone that resonates most deeply with those born in the Year of the Snake is **amethyst**, known for its purple glow that calms the spirit. Since ancient times, it's been called the "stone of spirituality" and the "stone to prevent intoxication," believed to support Snake-born individuals who, like snakes, quietly discern the essence of things.

## Why Amethyst is a Perfect Match

Amethyst, affectionately known as the "purple quartz," is said to have been carved into banquet cups in ancient Greece as the "stone that prevents intoxication." Its name comes from "a-" (without) plus "methystos" (intoxicated), symbolizing the ability to stay calm and not be overwhelmed by emotions. In medieval Europe, it adorned bishops' rings and has long been cherished as a gem of spirituality. It's super cool how this stone has such a long history!

The primary reason amethyst resonates with Snake-born individuals is that it's considered the **"stone that harmonizes intuition."** It's said that those born in the Year of the Snake can sometimes be swayed by their overly sharp senses, and amethyst is believed to help refine that intuition into "**clear judgment**." Amethyst helps you make super clear decisions, even when your intuition is buzzing! The spiritual wavelengths governed by both the serpent deity's symbol and this sacred purple stone are said to deeply intertwine.

Furthermore, the Year of the Snake is also the zodiac sign of "transformation and regeneration," meaning those born under it often experience significant shedding at life's turning points. Amethyst has been known since ancient times as a stone that supports transformation, acting as a mirror to help you discern "**what to keep and what to shed**" during life's transitions. It's like having a wise friend helping you decide what's next!

-   **Clothing Colors**: Purple, white, and black. Since white snakes are a symbol of Benzaiten, traditionally, incorporating a white handkerchief or small accessory is considered a good luck practice for the Year of the Snake. Adding a little white touch is a secret power-up!

## Unlucky Years, Turning Points, and How to Use Your Stones

For those born in the Year of the Snake, the year of their own zodiac sign (known as "Toshio-Otoko" or "Toshionna"), which occurs once every 12 years, is sometimes called the "**Year of Shedding**." It's considered a turning point where old relationships, old habits, and old titles naturally fall away, allowing for rebirth into a new self. Many are said to acquire a new amethyst during this time. It's your special year to become an even brighter you!

During your main unlucky year (yakudoshi, for example, 42 for men and 33 for women by traditional Japanese count), intuition is said to work strongly as a "warning." Traditionally, it's advised to combine amethyst with obsidian during this period, listening carefully not just to the visible reality but also to your inner voice. Listen extra carefully to your feelings during those tricky years!

The zodiac sign directly opposite the Snake is the **Boar (亥, I)**, and the Year of the Boar is said to be one where Snake-born individuals might be easily swayed by straightforward energy. During this year, it's traditionally recommended to use amethyst as your base stone, complemented by labradorite, to maintain flexibility. Labradorite will help you stay graceful and adaptable when things get a bit too direct!

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q. Should I buy a new stone in my zodiac year?**
**A.** The Year of the Snake is considered a "Year of Shedding," so many people welcome a new amethyst. Traditionally, old stones are cleansed with gratitude and carefully kept as a memento of the milestone. It's like a fresh start for your spiritual journey!

**A.** It's preferred to carry one main amethyst, complemented by transformation-oriented stones like labradorite or charoite. For Snake-born individuals, it's said to be beneficial to gradually add more stones at each life turning point. Feel free to mix and match to boost your energy!

**Q. I heard amethyst can fade. Is that true?**
**A.** Yes, it's said that prolonged direct sunlight can cause its color to fade. Cleansing with moonlight, sage, or a crystal cluster is recommended, and a nightly cleansing ritual is particularly compatible with those born in the Year of the Snake. So give your amethyst a lovely moon bath instead of a sunbath!
