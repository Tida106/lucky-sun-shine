## Introduction

The Tiger (Tora) is the third sign in the Chinese zodiac, and just like the fierce and brave tiger it represents, it symbolizes courage, decisive action, and leadership. In Buddhism, the Tiger is considered a messenger of **Bishamonten**, the deity of both war and wealth. As a sacred beast attending to this god, it has been deeply revered by warriors and business leaders since ancient times. For those born in the Year of the Tiger, there's a guardian stone that perfectly matches their inherent nature and even their name: **Tiger's Eye**, also known as "Tiger Stone." Its golden, shimmering, eye-like stripes are said to be the very crystallization of the Tiger's decisive power and ability to ward off evil.

## Personality and Fortune for Those Born in the Year of the Tiger

Those born in the Year of the Tiger possess a distinct leadership quality among the twelve zodiac signs. They are said to readily take the lead in groups and possess the courage to promptly raise their hand and say, "I'll do it!" in times of crisis.

In their professional lives, they are often drawn to roles where their **own judgment makes all the difference**, such as entrepreneurship, independent work, and sales. Their speed in making decisions and commitment to results are lauded as among the best in the zodiac, and they tend to grow into individuals who can break through stagnation in organizations.

However, it's also true that if their strong sense of justice manifests as stubbornness, it can easily lead to conflicts with others. It's also said that Tigers can sometimes lose momentum by alienating valuable allies in their pursuit of what they believe is right, or by being overly aggressive in crucial moments.

Among the twelve zodiac signs, the Tiger is associated with the element of "Wood" and presides over the hours just before dawn (3 AM to 5 AM, known as the "Hour of the Tiger"). They carry the energy of the **moment of transition from darkness to light**, and are said to achieve their greatest successes when their actions and intuition align.

## Why Tiger's Eye is a Perfect Match

Tiger's Eye is a stone where fibrous minerals create a silk-like band of light. It gets its name from the visual phenomenon called "chatoyancy" (or "cat's eye effect"), which resembles a tiger's eye. In ancient Egypt, it was believed to represent the eyes of the gods, and Roman soldiers reportedly carried it into battle as an amulet.

The biggest reason for its resonance with those born in the Year of the Tiger is said to be its combination of **"insightful vision" and "power to ward off evil."** Tiger's Eye supports the fast-deciding Tigers, helping them to grasp the essence of a situation before acting, rather than rushing forward on instinct alone. It has been described as an amulet of "power wielded righteously," much like the jeweled staff Bishamonten holds.

Moreover, Tiger's Eye has been known since ancient times as a stone of financial luck, a rare gem that simultaneously governs both decisiveness and wealth. The fact that "victory" and "prosperity" are intertwined with this stone also aligns with Bishamonten's world view, who presides over both war and riches, making it the perfect match for the grand scale of life for those born in the Year of the Tiger.

## Compatible Stones to Carry Along

-   **Garnet**: This deep-red "pomegranate stone" is known as the stone of victory. It's said to supplement the Tiger's inherent tenacity, helping them win long battles without giving up, and transforming their initial bursts of energy into sustained endurance.
-   **Carnelian**: A stone of action and passion. This orange-hued stone, reportedly favored by Napoleon, is said to give you the push you need to take that crucial first step when the stakes are high.
-   **Onyx**: A black stone that severs negativity. It's said to act as a shield, protecting Tigers from accumulating unnecessary adversaries, especially in situations where their strong sense of justice might unintentionally create conflict.

## Auspicious Actions for Those Born in the Year of the Tiger

-   **Clothing Colors**: Gold, black, deep green. Traditionally, incorporating a single tiger-print accessory is also favored. On presentation days or when facing a challenge, use gold accessories as an accent.
-   **Wallet**: A long wallet in yellow or gold tones. Because Tigers are associated with "acting decisively and making big moves," using a long wallet that opens wide to keep energy flowing is preferred.
-   **Places of Worship**: Temples and shrines that enshrine Bishamonten, such as Chogosonshi-ji Temple on Mt. Shigi and Kurama-dera Temple. It's said that visiting on a day when the **Day of the Tiger, Month of the Tiger, and Hour of the Tiger** align will strengthen your protection.
-   **Auspicious Rhythm**: The "**Day of the Tiger**" is considered the most auspicious day for departures, investments, and opening new businesses, stemming from the saying, "A tiger travels a thousand li and returns a thousand li."
-   **Habit**: Drink warm water and practice deep breathing every morning. Since Tigers tend to be proactive, taking time in the morning to "center themselves" is said to stabilize their judgment throughout the day.

## Unlucky Years, Milestones, and Stone Usage

For those born in the Year of the Tiger, their own zodiac year (the "Year of the Tiger," occurring once every 12 years) is literally considered the strongest period of energy, where "the tiger moves in the year of the tiger." It's an auspicious time for major decisions like new businesses, independence, moving, and marriage, and many people are said to acquire a new Tiger's Eye to be their companion for the year.

However, it's also said to be a year where momentum can be so strong that it leads to recklessness, so particular caution is needed if your major unlucky year (yakudoshi, e.g., 42 for men and 33 for women in traditional counting) coincides with a Tiger year. During such a year, it's traditionally recommended to focus on being a "**protecting Tiger**" rather than an "attacking Tiger," and to use Onyx in conjunction to avoid making enemies.

The zodiac sign directly opposite the Tiger is the **Monkey (Saru)**, and the Year of the Monkey is said to be a year when Tigers can easily get caught up in schemes or tricks. During this year, it's said that adding Carnelian alongside Tiger's Eye can help stabilize them by reinforcing a clear and consistent course of action.
