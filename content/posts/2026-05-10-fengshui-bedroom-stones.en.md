## Introduction

We spend **one-third** of our day in the bedroom. Your bedroom is a "charging station for good fortune" — by keeping it tidy and harmonious, you can transform everything from your next day's performance to your relationships. In this article, I'll introduce 7 power stones perfect for your bedroom and share tips on where to place them.

## 3 Key Objectives for Your Bedroom

1.  Improve **sleep quality**
2.  Enhance **love luck and marital harmony**
3.  Prevent **nightmares and spiritual unease**

## 7 Power Stones Perfect for Your Bedroom

### 1. Amethyst (Sleep Quality and Mental Stability)

-   **Role**: Soothes irritation, insomnia, and anxiety.
-   **Placement**: Bedside table next to your pillow.
-   **Size**: A raw stone or cluster about 5-8 cm is ideal.
-   **Caution**: Avoid direct sunlight, so keep it away from curtain rails.

## Tips for Placement in the Bedroom

### 1. Ideal Placement Next to Your Pillow

-   **Left Side**: Receiving side (intuition and love).
-   **Right Side**: Releasing side (unwanted energy).

Example: Rose Quartz on the left, Smoky Quartz on the right → Receive love and release stress.

### 2. Placing Under the Bed is Also Okay

-   Keeps things visually neat.
-   Cleanse a bit more frequently than others (dust tends to accumulate).

### 3. On Your Dresser or Vanity

-   Boost feminine energy: Rose Quartz and Moonstone.
-   Affects your entire energy field when you look into the mirror.

### 4. Morion in the Four Corners of Your Bedroom

An application of warding off negative energy from the entire house. By placing **four raw Morion stones in the corners**, you create a protective barrier for the entire room.

## Feng Shui Don'ts in the Bedroom

-   **Foot of the bed directly facing the door**: Avoided as it's called "the dead man's pillow."
-   **Large mirror above the bed**: Reflecting yourself while sleeping can disrupt your energy.
-   **Too many houseplants or dried flowers**: Stones and fabrics are generally preferred over plants in the bedroom.
-   **Too many electronics**: Electromagnetic waves can disrupt energy; keep them as far away as possible.
-   **Stuffing items under the bed**: Blocks the flow of energy.

## A Daily Cycle for Good Sleep

### Morning

-   Open the window to ventilate.
-   Briefly charge your bedside stones with sunlight (only for sun-safe stones, for a short duration).

### Evening (Before Bed)

-   Express gratitude for the day to your stones.
-   Organize your next day's plans to lighten your mind.
-   Keep your smartphone away from the bed.

### Moonlight Bath Days

-   On a full moon night, place your bedside stones by the window.
-   Always bring them back inside by the next morning.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. I've heard that placing stones near the pillow can make it "too energetically strong to sleep." Is that true?**

A. It is said that some **energetically strong stones (like Morion and Lapis Lazuli)** can indeed hinder sleep for sensitive individuals. If you feel any discomfort, it's safer to either **place them more than 30 cm away from the bed** or **stick to Amethyst and Rose Quartz near your pillow.**

**Q2. How many stones can I place in the bedroom?**

A. **3 to 5 stones** is a practical number. One to two by the pillow, one on the dresser, plus four Morion stones in the corners would total 5 to 7. If there are too many, the energy theme can become diluted, so the trick is to **combine stones with different roles.**

**Q3. My partner dislikes stones. Can I place them discreetly?**

A. Placing them **under the bed (near the pillow)** or **inside a dresser drawer** is said to still be effective. However, dust tends to accumulate, so don't forget to **cleanse them once a month.**

**Q4. Can I place stones in a child's bedroom?**

A. For a child's bedroom, **stones with gentle energy (Rose Quartz, Amethyst, and Clear Quartz)** are recommended. Avoid strong stones like Morion, and choose stones **large enough that a child cannot put them in their mouth** (5 cm or larger).

**Q5. How often should I give my stones a moonlight bath?**

A. Traditionally, **once or twice a month (especially on a full moon night)**. Place them by the window and always bring them back inside by the next morning. For more details on moonlight baths, please refer to [Moon Phases and Power Stones](/blog/moon-phases-and-stones/).
