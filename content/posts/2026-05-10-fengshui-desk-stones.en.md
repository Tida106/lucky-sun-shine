## Introduction

With remote and work-from-home becoming the norm, the **"energy" around your desk** directly impacts your work performance. In this article, I'll introduce 7 crystals you should place on your desk and explain how to arrange them to enhance focus, career luck, and improve your relationships.

## Four Key Objectives for Your Desk

1.  Maintain **focus**
2.  Attract **career success and promotions**
3.  Improve **interpersonal relationships** (quality of emails and meetings)
4.  Relieve **fatigue and stress**

## 7 Crystals You'll Want on Your Desk

### 1. Fluorite (Stone of Focus and Genius)

-   **Role**: Enhance focus and clarify thoughts
-   **Placement**: Next to your computer, slightly visible within your line of sight
-   **Shape**: Octahedral crystal or tumbled stone

### 2. Tiger's Eye (Stone of Decisiveness and Career Success)

-   **Role**: Decision-making, insight, and career success
-   **Placement**: Upper right of your desk (the direction of status and southeast)
-   **Best for**: Managers, sales professionals, and freelancers

### 3. Citrine (Stone of Financial Luck and Self-Esteem)

-   **Role**: Business prosperity and confidence
-   **Placement**: To the right of your computer, on top of documents
-   **Best for**: Entrepreneurs and those in commission-based roles

### 4. Lapis Lazuli (Stone of Leadership and Wisdom)

-   **Role**: Discernment to see the true essence of things
-   **Placement**: North side of your desk (the direction for career success)
-   **Best for**: Leaders, planners, and researchers

### 5. Amethyst (Stone of Stress Relief)

-   **Role**: Calm irritability and anxiety
-   **Placement**: Left side of your desk (the receiving side)
-   **Best for**: Those prone to stress and individuals in emotionally demanding jobs

### 6. Aquamarine (Stone of Communication)

-   **Role**: Enhance the quality of meetings, emails, and phone calls
-   **Placement**: Next to your computer, near your phone
-   **Best for**: Those with frequent external communications

### 7. Pyrite (Stone of Financial Luck and Willpower)

-   **Role**: Attract financial luck and strengthen willpower to resist temptation
-   **Placement**: Lower right of your desk
-   **Best for**: Those with side businesses or managing multiple projects

## 8 Principles of Placement

### 1. One Stone Next to Your Computer

This is the most visible spot. Place it where you can easily hold it when you need to focus.

### 2. Be Mindful of the Desk's 'Three Divisions'

-   **Left 1/3**: Receiving Area (information and emails) → Amethyst and Quartz
-   **Middle 1/3**: Working Area → Fluorite
-   **Right 1/3**: Outgoing Area (phone calls and meetings) → Tiger's Eye and Aquamarine

### 3. Don't Place Stones Directly on Your Computer

There's a possibility that electromagnetic waves and heat could degrade the stone. Plus, covering the screen isn't ideal from a feng shui perspective either.

### 4. Combine with Houseplants

Scatter small tumbled stones in the plant pot. Houseplants activate positive energy.

### 5. North Side of Your Desk = Career Success

Place Lapis Lazuli on the **north side of your desk** (facing forward from your perspective), not the room's north.

### 6. Southeast of Your Desk = Financial Luck

Place Citrine, Tiger's Eye, and Pyrite in the back right of your desk.

### 7. Purify Once a Month

The stones absorb work-related stress. Make sure to purify them once a month.

### 8. Before and After Business Trips or Outings

Hold your stone before a trip and say, "**Please take care of me today!**" Then purify it when you return.

## Things to Avoid

-   **A cluttered desk**: This can disturb the stones' effects. Tidy up first!
-   **Working while eating**: The energy of food and work can mix.
-   **Leaving old documents around**: "Past energy" can stagnate.
-   **Three coffee cups**: Indicates stagnant water. Clear them away!
-   **A dying houseplant**: This brings "stagnant energy." Let it go immediately!

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Is it okay to place stones on my office desk?**
A. **One or two small tumbled stones** can be placed without looking out of place. It's best to choose a **discreet size and color** so you can explain it as an "amulet" if asked. **Citrine, Tiger's Eye, and Fluorite** are recommended for their elegant appearance.

**Q2. Will placing them near my computer cause electromagnetic interference?**
A. It's generally very unlikely that stone materials will **degrade due to heat or electromagnetic waves**. However, avoid placing them directly **on top of or right next to a laptop** where heat can accumulate. Placing them **slightly away** will give you peace of mind. Remember to purify them once a month for refreshment.

**Q3. If I work both at home and in the office, where should I place my stones?**
A. It's recommended to place your main stones at your **primary base (home or office)** and carry **one small amulet stone** with you. Simply putting a tumbled stone in your pocket allows you to carry its energy with you wherever you go.

**Q4. How long does it take to feel the effects?**
A. Many people experience enhanced **focus and calmness** on the very day they place the stone. However, external changes like **career success and financial luck** realistically take **several weeks to several months**. The essential mechanism is believed to be that "**the stone helps align your consciousness, which in turn changes your actions**".

**Q5. Which is more important: houseplants or stones?**
A. **Both are ideal**. Houseplants activate energy, and stones help direct that energy. The combination of **scattering small tumbled stones in a houseplant's pot** is a traditional arrangement that enhances the effects of both.
