## Introduction

In Feng Shui, the **most important place** is actually not the living room or the bedroom, but rather the **entrance**. Since all energy entering your home passes through the entrance, harmonizing this space directly impacts your overall luck. In this article, I'll explain 8 power stones you'll want to place in your entrance and tips on how to arrange them!

## Why the Entrance is the Most Important Place in Feng Shui

- **The Gateway for All Ki**: Both good and bad energy first pass through here.
- **First Impression of Your Luck**: It's the first space you encounter when you return home.
- **Visitors' Impression**: The energy of others is also brought into your home.
- **Overall Home Energy Balance**: The state of your entrance affects the entire house.

## 8 Power Stones You'll Want to Place in Your Entrance

### 1. Quartz (Most Basic and Versatile)

- **Role**: Purification of energy and overall upliftment.
- **Placement**: Avoid placing directly on the `tataki` (the sunken entrance floor). Instead, place it on a small dish on top of the shoe cabinet.
- **Size**: For rough stones, 5-10cm is ideal. A cluster is also excellent.

### 2. Morion (Strongest Ward Against Evil)

- **Role**: Powerful protection against negative energies.
- **Placement**: In a corner of the entrance, or on top of the shoe cabinet.
- **Suitable for Homes**: Facing a road, with high foot traffic, or for people who are spiritually sensitive.

### 3. Citrine (Attracts Financial Luck)

- **Role**: Attracts energy of abundance and money.
- **Placement**: In a bright spot, or at the base of a houseplant.
- **Recommended Shape**: Rough stone or cluster.

### 4. Tiger's Eye (Decision-Making and Financial Fortune)

- **Role**: Harmonizes the entry point for career luck and financial fortune.
- **Placement**: On the front side of the top of the shoe cabinet.
- **Suitable for**: Business people and entrepreneurs.

### 5. Rose Quartz (The Stone of Love that Invites People)

- **Role**: Brings warm energy and harmony to relationships.
- **Placement**: On the right side of the entrance (the feminine energy side).
- **Suitable for Homes**: Where you want to improve family relationships.

### 6. Amethyst (Spiritual Purification)

- **Role**: Promotes calm energy and spirituality.
- **Placement**: On top of the shoe cabinet, or near a houseplant.
- **Suitable for**: Highly sensitive individuals and those who practice meditation.

### 7. Turquoise (Protection for Travel and Movement)

- **Role**: Safeguards the safety of those who go out.
- **Placement**: Near the entrance door, or on a small dish on the `tataki` (entrance floor).
- **Suitable for**: Those who travel frequently for business or leisure, and those who drive.

### 8. Pyrite (Warding Off Evil and Financial Luck)

- **Role**: Combines financial luck with protection against negative energies.
- **Placement**: On top of the shoe cabinet, slightly below eye level.
- **Recommended Shape**: Cubic crystal cluster.

## Tips for Placing Stones in Your Entrance

### 1. Slightly Below Eye Level

Placing them directly on the `tataki` is a no-go (they might get kicked or stepped on). The **top of your shoe cabinet** is the classic spot.

### 2. In a Spot Where Light Hits

The brighter your entrance, the more vibrant the stone's energy will be. A spot where **natural light enters** is best. For stones that don't like direct sunlight, avoid placing them right by the window.

### 3. Keep It Clean

Regularly wipe dust off the stones. Wipe down the shoe cabinet itself once a week.

### 4. Combine with Houseplants

Houseplants activate energy in Feng Shui. It's also recommended to **scatter small stones in the plant pots**.

### 5. Use a Small Dish or Cloth

Instead of placing them directly on a cold floor, placing them on a **small dish, box, or cloth** conveys a feeling of "handling them with care" to the stones.

## Things You Should NOT Do in Your Entrance

- **Leaving Shoes Scattered**: This disrupts the flow of energy (put them in the shoe cabinet after taking them off).
- **Leaving Old Umbrellas Standing**: Moisture equals stagnant energy.
- **Dried Flowers**: These are considered "dead energy" in Feng Shui.
- **A Mirror Directly Facing the Entrance Door**: This repels incoming energy.
- **A Dark Entrance**: Turn on the lights at least once a day to keep it bright.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. How many stones can I place?**

A. There's no strict upper limit, but **2 to 4 stones** is practical. The classic approach is to start with the versatile **Quartz and the protective Morion**, then add one or two more based on your specific goals. Too many stones are believed to dilute the focus of the energy.

**Q2. What if the top of my shoe cabinet is cluttered and I can't place stones?**

A. First, **start by tidying up the top of your shoe cabinet**. In Feng Shui, excessive clutter itself is believed to block the flow of energy. After organizing, securing even just **enough space for one small dish** for your stone will be plenty!

**Q3. Do they work even in rental homes or small entrances?**

A. Yes! Even just **one small quartz point** is believed to change the energy flow. A tiny space next to the `tataki` or a corner on top of your shoe cabinet is perfectly fine.

**Q4. What if the stones get dirty?**

A. Once a month, **wipe them with a soft cloth**, or if it's not quartz, **rinse them briefly under running water** (excluding water-sensitive stones). Leaving dirt is believed to reduce their purification power, so regular care is important. For more details, please refer to the [Complete Guide to Power Stone Purification Methods](/blog/purification-complete-guide/).

**Q5. Should I match the entrance direction with the stones?**

A. It's not mandatory, but being mindful of the combinations listed under **"Recommended Stone for Enhancement by Direction"** is believed to help balance the overall energy of your home. If you're unsure which direction your entrance faces, you can check with a **compass app on your smartphone**.
