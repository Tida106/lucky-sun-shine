## The Relationship Between Fertility and Safe Childbirth and Crystals

Many fertility stones often feature colors that symbolize "**motherhood**," such as **milky white, light green, and pink**. In ancient Rome, **Moonstone** was considered the stone of Diana, the goddess of the moon, and its connection to "**the moon's rhythm equaling a woman's cycle**" led to its use for fertility prayers. Native Americans in North America regarded **Unakite** as a symbol of "**Mother Earth**," and in Hawaiian goddess worship, **Chrysocolla** has been cherished under the name "**Mother Earth**."

> **Important Note**: Crystals are not a substitute for medical treatment. **If you experience any discomfort during fertility treatment, pregnancy, or postpartum, please consult a medical institution immediately.** This article explains cultural and symbolic meanings only.

## Meanings and Effects of Each Stone

### Main Stone: Moonstone (Milky White to Blue Iridescence)

**Moonstone**, aligning with the moon's rhythm, is a prominent stone known for enhancing **femininity**. It has been used for **fertility prayers** since ancient times, with the growth from new moon to full moon likened to "**the process of nurturing a child**."

-   **Regulating Moon's Rhythm**: Resonates with a woman's cycle.
-   **Welcoming a New Life**: A talisman for the fertility journey.
-   **Evoking Motherhood**: Supports the resolve of motherhood.

For more details, refer to [The Meaning and Effects of Moonstone](/blog/moonstone/).

### Unakite (Green and Pink Mosaic)

This stone, with its blend of green (epidote) and pink (feldspar), is known as the stone of "**Mother Earth**." It accompanies you through all phases of **pregnancy, childbirth, and child-rearing**, making it a hidden gem and a classic choice among fertility stones.

### Chrysocolla (Blue-Green)

Known by the epithet "**Mother Earth**," this is a stone of motherhood. It is believed to bring **deep relaxation** and **harmony to femininity**, making it a calming talisman during stressful fertility journeys.

### Rose Quartz (Pale Pink)

The **essence of motherhood and love**. As a symbol of nurturing "**love for the baby in the womb**," there is a tradition of placing it by the bedside or around the abdomen. For more details, refer to [The Meaning and Effects of Rose Quartz](/blog/rose-quartz-meaning/).

### Malachite (Deep Green Swirl Patterns)

A traditional stone historically used in Europe as a "**stone to protect children**." Also known as the "**all-seeing eye**," it acts as a protective charm for mother and child, with its ability to detect danger. For children, small **tumbled stones** are recommended.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Is it safe to wear them during pregnancy?**

A. **Generally, yes**, but avoid heavy or pointed stones that might press against your abdomen. Placing them in a **soft fabric amulet pouch** is safer. If you feel any discomfort, remove them.

**Q2. What is the meaning of holding them during fertility treatment?**

A. Stones are **"talismans to help you compose your mind"** and are not a substitute for treatment. While **prioritizing medical care**, many people incorporate them as a form of mental support on anxious days.

**Q3. What if I want to give them as a gift?**

A. For a friend on their fertility journey, a **Moonstone bracelet**; for someone pregnant, a **Rose Quartz amulet pouch**; and for safe childbirth prayers, a **Unakite tumbled stone** are often appreciated.

**Q4. What about talismans for children?**

A. **Malachite tumbled stones** are a classic choice for school bags and satchels. To prevent choking, opt for **larger tumbles (5cm or more)** or **secure amulet pouches**.

**Q5. How often should I cleanse them?**

A. **Once a month**, especially according to pregnancy weeks or on the new moon. Placing them on **clear quartz chips** or **through moonlight cleansing** are safe methods. Moonstone and Malachite are **sensitive to water**, so avoid cleansing them with running water.
