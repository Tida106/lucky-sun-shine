## Introduction

Kunzite is a relatively new mineral, discovered in 1902 in a mine in California, USA, and formally described by **Dr. George Frederick Kunz**, then the leading gemologist and chief gem expert for Tiffany and Company. It was named "**Kunzite**" in his honor. As a mineral, it is a type of lithium aluminum silicate called "**Spodumene**," and its delicate color, shifting from pale pink to purplish-pink, evokes a gentle, motherly quality.

It was cherished by prominent 20th-century jewelry designers and actresses alike. J.P. Morgan was a collector, and it is famously known as an "**engagement stone**" given by J.F. Kennedy to Jacqueline Kennedy Onassis. Despite its relatively short history, Kunzite has rapidly gained popularity among healers and women as the "**Mother of Love**," a stone of unconditional love and healing for emotional wounds.

## Stone Words and Meaning

Kunzite's stone words are "**unconditional love, healing, and motherhood**." It has been cherished worldwide as a stone symbolizing the gentle motherly love of the "Mother of Love."

Pink gemstones have historically been treated as "**symbols of love**." While [Rose Quartz](/blog/rose-quartz-meaning/) symbolizes "**gentle love in general**" and [Morganite](/blog/morganite/) represents "**compassion and prayer**," Kunzite is spoken of with the nuance of "**motherly love that asks for nothing in return**" and "**love that embraces oneself**."

The soft coloration characteristic of lithium-containing Spodumene has solidified its popularity among modern psychological therapists and healers as a "**stone for healing past wounds**." Its susceptibility to breakage if dropped and its tendency to fade in strong light also reinforce the image of a "**stone that empathizes with sensitive hearts**."

## Color Variations

Kunzite is a stone whose impression can change dramatically with slight differences in color.

-   **Pale Pink**: The most delicate hue, described as "**girlish gentleness**."
-   **Rose Pink**: A classic shade, the representative color of the Mother of Love.
-   **Violet Pink**: A type with a higher proportion of purple, linked to themes of intuition and spirituality.
-   **Green Spodumene (Hiddenite)**: The green variety of the same mineral, considered a sister stone for themes of "**emotional healing and growth**."
-   **Confusion with Morganite**: [Morganite](/blog/morganite/) is also pink and similar, but it belongs to the beryl family. The presence of columnar vertical striations is characteristic of Kunzite.

## How to Spot Fakes and Artificial Stones

-   Natural Kunzite typically exhibits **fine columnar vertical striations (cleavage)** internally. If it appears completely clear, it might be glass or resin.
-   Because it is prone to fading, stones that have been exposed to bright display lighting for long periods might have a lighter color.
-   To ensure it is not mistaken for another variety like Hiddenite, check for the mineral name "**Spodumene**" in the description.
-   Purchasing from a reputable specialty store with certification provides peace of mind. Please also refer to our [Guide to Spotting Fakes and Artificial Stones](/blog/spot-fake-stones/).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q. What is the difference between Kunzite and Rose Quartz?**
A. Both are pink stones of love, but [Rose Quartz](/blog/rose-quartz-meaning/) is broadly associated with "**gentle love in general, romance, friendship, and self-love**," while Kunzite is said to specialize in deeper themes of "**unconditional motherly love and the healing of emotional wounds**."

**Q. What bead size is recommended for a bracelet?**
A. **6 to 8mm** is standard. Larger beads tend to have more beautiful color, but as it's a stone prone to breaking, smaller beads might be easier for daily wear.

**Q. Does it fade?**
A. Yes. Kunzite is sometimes called the "**Evening Stone**" because it is sensitive to light, and prolonged direct sunlight can cause its color to fade. It's ideal to wear it during the day and store it in a light-blocking place at night.

**Q. Is it okay to hold after a breakup?**
A. On the contrary, it is said to be a suitable stone for such times. Kunzite is believed to support the healing of "**past wounds of love**" while helping to restore the "**ability to love oneself again**."

**Q. Is it unusual for men to wear it?**
A. While it's often associated with femininity due to its "motherly" image, it is also a suitable stone for men's self-care and for confronting past traumas. Tumbled stones or pendants can be incorporated naturally.
