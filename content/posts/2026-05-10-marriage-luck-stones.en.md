## Meaning and Effects of Each Stone

### Main Stone: Morganite (Pale Pink to Salmon)

**Morganite** is a Beryl family gemstone, belonging to the same mineral group as Emerald and Aquamarine. Named in 1911 after **J.P. Morgan**, it has been chosen for engagements and weddings as a symbol of "**sincere and enduring love**."

- **Long-term partnership**: Gentle and lasting love
- **Unconditional kindness**: The power to forgive others
- **Family bonds**: Extending to relationships with parents and siblings

### Aquamarine (Light Blue)

A traditional stone, given to fiancées since ancient times as the "**stone of happy marriage**." In Roman mythology, it was dedicated to the goddess of the sea and is said to bring harmony to marital communication, like a "**calm sea**." For more details, refer to [Aquamarine Meaning and Effects](/blog/aquamarine/). If you were born in March, also check out [March Birthstone](/blog/birthstone-march/).

### Peridot (Bright Green)

Known as the stone of "**marital harmony and calming jealousy**." Because it shines even at night, it's also called "**the sun stone**" or "**evening emerald**," and is believed to play a role in **brightly illuminating** and resolving misunderstandings between couples.

### Rose Quartz (Pale Pink)

The **foundation of love and gentleness**. It serves as a charm to maintain compassion for your partner in the daily rhythm of married life. For more details, refer to [Rose Quartz Meaning and Effects](/blog/rose-quartz-meaning/).

### Jade

In the East, **Jade** is a symbol of "**family, prosperity, and longevity**." Often passed down through generations as a wedding gift, it's perfect for those who value the "**bond across three generations**."

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Can Morganite replace a wedding ring?**

A. **Morganite engagement rings** have seen a surge in popularity in recent years. They are more affordable than diamonds and offer a **softer impression**, but most importantly, their symbolism of "**long-term partnership**" makes them perfect for marriage. However, with a hardness of 7.5 to 8, we recommend choosing a **secure setting with robust prongs** for daily wear.

**Q2. Should I choose Aquamarine or Morganite?**

A. For the **engagement and marriage-hunting stages**, Aquamarine (traditionally associated with "happy marriage") is ideal. For **after marriage and long-term partnership**, Morganite (for sincere continuity) is wonderful. Owning both is also a classic approach! If you were born in March, remember it's also your birthstone, so check out [March Birthstone: Aquamarine](/blog/birthstone-march/).

**Q3. What if I want to give one as a gift?**

A. For a fiancée, a **Morganite pendant** (10,000-50,000 yen) is a common gift. For a couple's anniversary, an **Aquamarine ring** is a classic choice, and for parents' wedding anniversary, a **Jade bracelet** is a traditional pick.

**Q4. What stones are good for men to wear?**

A. Since **Morganite** has a soft color, men might consider **Garnet (for sincere bonds)** or **Tiger's Eye (for decision-making)** as a charm before proposing. After marriage, a **Jade bracelet** offers a more subdued and sophisticated impression.

**Q5. How often should I cleanse my stones?**

A. **Once a month** is a good guideline. Making it a habit to cleanse your stones around your wedding anniversary or birthday helps you remember. **On clear quartz chips**, **moonlight bathing**, and **white sage** are safe methods. Please avoid exposing Morganite to direct sunlight for extended periods.
