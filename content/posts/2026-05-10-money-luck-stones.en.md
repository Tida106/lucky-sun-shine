## Meanings and Effects of Each Stone

### Main Stone: Citrine (Yellow Quartz)

**Citrine** is a classic for money luck, known as the "**Stone of Prosperity**" and "**Sun Stone**." Its bright yellow color is said to resonate with the **third chakra (solar plexus)**, fostering confidence and a positive outlook.

-   **Attracts income flow**: An amulet for merchants and business owners
-   **Confidence and self-esteem**: Useful for price negotiations and personal branding
-   **Brightness and positivity**: A reset for gloomy days

For more details, refer to [Citrine Meaning, Stone Language, and Effects](/blog/citrine/).

### Rutilated Quartz (Quartz with Golden Needles)

With golden needles (rutiles) running through the crystal, Rutilated Quartz is a **"top-tier financial luck stone."** It's said to attract sudden wealth and is popular as an amulet for "**short-term battles**" such as stock investments, gambling, and lotteries.

### Tiger's Eye (Golden-Brown Striped)

A stone of **decisiveness and insight**. Rather than solely for money luck, it acts as a "**bridge, connecting work decisions to financial fortune**." An amulet for important investment decisions, contracts, and career changes. For more details, refer to [Tiger's Eye Meaning and Effects](/blog/tigers-eye/).

### Pyrite (Iron Pyrite)

With its metallic luster, Pyrite is a "**two-in-one**" stone for **warding off negative energy and attracting money luck.** Beyond increasing income, it's also believed to offer protection against excessive spending and wastefulness.

### Jade

The most revered stone of "**prosperity and longevity**" in the East. It symbolizes "**long-term savings and sustained abundance**" rather than short-term, flashy gains. Ideal for those who focus on family business and household finances.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Which stone is best to put in my wallet?**

A. **Citrine tumbled stones** are a classic and easy single choice. Placing a single **Rutilated Quartz tumble stone** is also recommended. Choose small pieces, around **5-10mm**, to avoid inconvenience.

**Q2. Do money luck stones work for lotteries?**

A. If you're aiming for "**sudden wealth**," **Rutilated Quartz** is the classic choice. However, stones are purely "**amulets for aligning your mindset**." Please understand they do not guarantee winning.

**Q3. What if I want to give one as a gift?**

A. For businessmen, a **Tiger's Eye bracelet**; for women, a **Citrine pendant**; and for a business opening, a **Rutilated Quartz tumbled stone set** are classic choices.

**Q4. What if I can't stop overspending?**

A. Combine **Pyrite and Morion** for "preventing overspending and warding off negative energy." Along with stones, it's effective to tidy your wallet, remove unnecessary loyalty cards, and not accumulate receipts—addressing **behavioral habits** too.

**Q5. How often should I cleanse my stones?**

A. Every **2 weeks to 1 month** is a guideline. Placing them on **clear quartz tumbled stones**, **moonlight bathing**, or **white sage smoke** are safe methods. Citrine and Tiger's Eye tolerate sunlight relatively well, but avoid prolonged direct sunlight for Rutilated Quartz.

## Editorial Team's Perspective: Before Choosing a Money Luck Stone, Ask Yourself Just One Question About Your Relationship with Money

When asked for "**money luck stones**," the editorial team always starts by posing one question: "**Do you want more money, or do you want your relationship with money to change?**" These two seem similar, but they are entirely different themes.

| Your True Desire | Suitable Stone |
|---|---|
| Simply want to **increase income** | Rutilated Quartz and Citrine (flow and amplification) |

Don't lump everything under "money luck." Deciding whether you truly want to change "**the numbers**" or "**the relationship**" first will quickly narrow down your stone choice.

### Common Misconceptions

-   ❌ "**Holding a money luck stone guarantees an unexpected windfall**" → ✅ It's a fundamental premise that nothing happens without action. **Stones are a trigger for "developing money-conscious habits."**
-   ❌ "**The more needles in Rutilated Quartz, the stronger the effect**" → ✅ The quantity of needles is irrelevant to the effect. **The correct choice is "the one you find beautiful."**
-   ❌ "**Heat-treated Citrine has a weaker effect**" → ✅ Culturally and symbolically, they are considered equivalent. The majority of the market is heat-treated.
-   ❌ "**Jade is a Chinese stone, so it doesn't suit Japanese people**" → ✅ Japanese **magatama** (curved beads) were also made of jade. It's a **stone with a deep connection to Japanese people since the Jomon period.**
