## Introduction

Since ancient times, moon phases have been believed to influence **tides, plant growth, and even the human mind and body**. In the world of power stones, aligning your crystals with the moon's rhythm can help their energy flow more naturally. In this article, I'll explain how to make the most of each of the four moon phases!

## The Four Moon Phases

| Moon Phase | Energy | Duration (once a month) |
|---|---|---|
| **New Moon** | Beginnings, Wishing | 1 day (strictly speaking, a few hours) |
| **First Quarter Moon** | Growth, Action | Approx. 7 days later |
| **Full Moon** | Completion, Cleansing, Gratitude | Approx. 14 days later |
| **Last Quarter Moon** | Releasing, Decluttering | Approx. 21 days later |

## 1. How to Use the New Moon: Start Your Intentions

The **New Moon** is when the energy of "**new beginnings**" is strongest. It's the perfect time to set intentions with your stones for what you want to start or attract!

### What to Do on a New Moon

-   **Welcome new stones**: Traditionally, it's best to buy new power stones within a few days of the New Moon.
-   **Set intentions**: Cup your stone in both hands and verbalize your new goals or wishes.
-   **New Moon affirmations**: Affirm your wishes in a positive statement, like "I attract..."
-   **First use of a new bracelet**: Establish a "contract" with yourself and your new crystal bracelet.

### Stones That Resonate Well with the New Moon

-   **Moonstone**: Most in tune with the moon's rhythm.
-   **Labradorite**: For the spirituality of new beginnings.
-   **Chrysoprase**: The stone of blossoming.

## 2. The First Quarter Moon: Empowering Action

The **First Quarter Moon** is when the moon is waxing (growing). This is a time for **taking action** to manifest the intentions you set during the New Moon.

### What to Do During the First Quarter Moon

-   **Take action**: Translate your New Moon wishes into concrete steps.
-   **Wear your stones when you go out**: Utilize stones that boost action and courage.
-   **Start new habits**: Like waking up early or exercising.

### Stones That Resonate Well with the First Quarter Moon

-   **Carnelian**: For action and the courage to step forward.
-   **Citrine**: For confidence and positivity.
-   **Tiger's Eye**: For determination.

## 3. The Full Moon: Cleansing and Gratitude

The **Full Moon** is when moonlight energy is at its maximum. This is the most traditional and important time for cleansing your power stones.

### What to Do on a Full Moon

-   **Cleanse stones with moonlight bathing**: Place your stones by a window overnight.
-   **Gratitude practice**: Express "thank you" for the events of the past month.
-   **Full Moon release**: Meditate to let go of attachments under the full moon's light.
-   **Take photos**: Photographing your stones with the Full Moon is also known as "**cleansing by photography**."

### How to Do Moonlight Bathing

1.  The 3 days surrounding the Full Moon are your cleansing window.
2.  Place your stones by a window or on a balcony where the moonlight directly shines (be careful of theft if outdoors).
3.  Leave them overnight. Be sure to bring them inside the next morning.
4.  Even if it's rainy or cloudy, the moon's energy is still said to reach them.

### Stones That Resonate Well with the Full Moon

-   **Moonstone**: Activated by the full moon's power.
-   **Clear Quartz**: Greatly cleansed by moonlight bathing.
-   **Larimar**: For the harmony of the sea and moon.
-   **Selenite** (a variety): A stone that holds the moon's energy.

## 4. The Last Quarter Moon: Releasing and Decluttering

The **Last Quarter Moon** is when the moon is waning. It's the perfect time to **let go of what you no longer need and declutter**.

### What to Do During the Last Quarter Moon

-   **Declutter**: Organize items, relationships, and habits.
-   **Review your stones**: Cleanse and thank unused stones, returning them to the earth if possible.
-   **Past release work**: Meditate to let go of attachments and trauma.
-   **Release old thought patterns**.

### Stones That Resonate Well with the Last Quarter Moon

-   **Prehnite**: The stone for letting go of unnecessary things.
-   **Obsidian**: For releasing the past.
-   **Charoite**: For life transformation.
-   **Morion**: For strong protection against negative energy.

## Caution: Stones Not Suitable for Moonlight Bathing

-   **Onyx, Morion, Smoky Quartz**: These colored quartz types are generally okay for short periods, but may not absorb moonlight effectively for cleansing due to their dark color.
-   **Amber**: Tends to lose moisture, so indoor cleansing is safer.
-   **Malachite, Lapis Lazuli**: Sensitive to water and humidity. Be careful of rain if placed outdoors.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Does moonlight bathing work on cloudy or rainy days?**

A. **Yes, it is believed to.** Moonlight is thought to penetrate clouds, and traditionally, "the date (Full Moon day) is prioritized over weather conditions." However, letting water-sensitive stones get directly wet in the rain by an open window can damage them, so **through a closed window** is safer.

**Q2. Does moonlight bathing *have* to be on the exact Full Moon day?**

A. **The 3 days surrounding the Full Moon** are considered the prime time, but **any night outside of the New Moon** is said to have some charging effect. It's more practical to choose a day that fits your lifestyle and aim for once or twice a month.

**Q3. What if I don't have a balcony or moonlight doesn't enter my window?**

A. **The moon's energy is still said to reach indoors.** Simply place your stones on a table by a window or in a spot facing the moon. Direct moonlight exposure isn't strictly necessary.

**Q4. How should I write my New Moon intentions?**

A. Traditionally, it's recommended to write them **within 48 hours of the New Moon** in **completed or present tense**, such as "I will become..." or "This will manifest...". Many people practice writing around 10 intentions as a bulleted list on an A4 sheet of paper.

**Q5. Is there a real connection between moon phases and physical well-being?**

A. Many people report experiencing **lighter sleep or heightened emotions** around the Full Moon, and medical research shows a weak correlation between sleep and moon phases. **Avoiding strenuous decisions and taking it easy** around the Full Moon is a traditional way to navigate this period.

**Q6. Can I combine this with other cleansing methods for power stones?**

A. Yes! Moonlight bathing is commonly combined with methods like **white sage, crystal chips, and running water**. For more detailed combinations, please refer to our [Complete Guide to Power Stone Cleansing Methods](/blog/purification-complete-guide/).
