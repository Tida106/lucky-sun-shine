## <span id="basic"></span>Basic Information and Mineral Data

| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| Stone Name | Onyx |
| Japanese Name | Kuro-menou (Black Agate) |
| Mineral Species | A type of Chalcedony |
| Chemical Composition | SiO₂ |
| Crystal System | Trigonal (Cryptocrystalline) |
| Main Sources | Brazil, India, Uruguay, USA |
| Mohs Hardness | 6.5 to 7 |
| Color | Jet black (Striped varieties are called Sardonyx or Banded Onyx) |
| Corresponding Chakra | First (Root) |

The name 'Onyx' comes from the Greek word **"onyx" (nail or claw)**. Originally, it referred to banded agate with black and white stripes, but in today's jewelry market, it's generally used to describe **solid black chalcedony**.

## <span id="meaning"></span>Stone Meanings and Historical and Cultural Background

Its stone meanings are "**Protection, Willpower, and Self-Control**."

In ancient Rome, soldiers carried it as an amulet on the battlefield to ground their energy and maintain composure. In medieval Europe, it was known as a **bedside stone to ward off nightmares** and is also one of the twelve gemstones mentioned in the Bible. In Japan, it has long been cherished as a material for Buddhist rosaries (juzu), a serene stone that can be worn appropriately at funerals and memorial services.

## <span id="faq"></span>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Is Onyx dyed?**

A. Most of the jet-black onyx circulated in the market is natural grayish chalcedony that has been **dyed to achieve a uniform black color**. This is a widely accepted standard treatment in the industry, and it's believed not to alter its perceived effects.

**Q2. I heard that wearing Onyx can make you feel depressed.**

A. Due to its strong grounding effects, sensitive individuals might feel a "heaviness." If you're feeling down, it's recommended to combine it with lighter stones like Rose Quartz or Clear Quartz, or to give it a break for a while.

**Q3. What is the difference between Black Onyx and Morion?**

A. **Onyx** is chalcedony (cryptocrystalline quartz), while **Morion** is black quartz (transparent crystalline quartz). Morion sometimes shows internal flashes when held up to light and is often spoken of as a stone for even stronger warding off evil.

**Q4. Is it okay to wear it at funerals or Buddhist services?**

A. Its serene color and use in traditional Japanese juzu make it a stone that feels appropriate. Choose a simple design to suit the occasion.

**Q5. Are there other protective stones?**

A. Morion, Hematite, Jet, Smoky Quartz, and others are classic choices. For more details, refer to the "Warding off Evil and Protection" section of the [Complete Guide to Power Stones by Purpose](/blog/luck-powerstones-complete-guide/).
