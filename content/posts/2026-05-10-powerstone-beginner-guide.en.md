## Introduction

So, you're interested in power stones, but you might be thinking, "**Do they really work?**" "**Which one should I choose?**" "**What's cleansing?**" — and that's totally normal for anyone starting out! This article is your go-to beginner's guide, packed with all the knowledge you'll need to **start today**.

## What Are Power Stones?

Power stones are **natural stones believed to hold spiritual or symbolic meaning**. While they became widely recognized in Japan in the 1990s, their origins stretch back thousands, even tens of thousands of years.

- **Lapis Lazuli** and Turquoise in ancient Egypt
- **Moonstone** in ancient India
- **Jade** in China
- **Magatama** in Japan's Jomon period

Since the dawn of civilization, humans have endowed specific stones with meaning and worn them. You can find more detailed explanations of each stone's history and meaning in individual articles like [Lapis Lazuli](/blog/lapis-lazuli/), [Moonstone](/blog/moonstone/), and [Jade](/blog/jade/).

> **Important Note**: The "effects" of power stones are **not medically or scientifically guaranteed**. It's crucial to approach them from a cultural and symbolic perspective. This article is written with that understanding in mind.

## It's Realistic to See Power Stones as "Amulets"

It's not about "wearing one and your financial luck increases," but rather, "**your actions change because you wear it and focus your awareness on it**" — this is the healthiest way to understand power stones.

- In the morning, wear [Citrine](/blog/citrine/) and resolve, "I won't miss any opportunities today."
- Before an important business meeting, hold [Tiger's Eye](/blog/tigers-eye/) and take a deep breath.
- On a restless night, place [Amethyst](/blog/amethyst/) by your bedside to calm your mind.

These are acts of using stones as a **mental switch**. While it's sometimes criticized as a placebo effect, if it leads to positive changes in your behavior, then it holds enough value.

## Checkpoints Before Buying

- Purchase from a reliable store (mineral specialty store or power stone specialty store).
- A **certification** provides extra peace of mind (especially for expensive stones).
- Check for origin and grade information.
- Overly cheap items might be dyed, artificial, or glass fakes.

## What to Do Once You Have Your Stone

### 1. Initial Cleansing

A stone bought directly from a store has absorbed various energies during its journey and display. Place it **on clear quartz chips overnight** or pass it through **white sage smoke** to reset it.

### 2. Making a "Commitment" with Yourself

After cleansing, cup the stone in both hands and mentally say, "**Please take care of me from now on**" (or a similar sentiment). The meaning isn't in the ritual itself, but in **your own commitment**.

### 3. Where to Wear Your Stone

- Wear your **bracelet on your left wrist**: corresponds to "receiving" energy.
- A **pendant on your chest**: directly connected to the 4th Chakra (heart).
- In your **pocket or wallet**: keep it close by at all times.

### 4. Regular Cleansing

It's said that stones can get "tired" if worn continuously. Cleanse them **once a week to once a month** with clear quartz chips or sage.

## What Not to Do

- Blindly believe in their effects and **entrust medical or financial decisions** to them.
- Unknowingly performing salt baths or water cleansing on **stones sensitive to salt or water** (e.g., [Malachite](/blog/malachite/), [Lapis Lazuli](/blog/lapis-lazuli/), [Turquoise](/blog/turquoise/)).
- **Prolonged direct sunlight** (many stones will fade).
- Leaving a cracked or broken stone as is (cracks are said to be bad for both the stone and yourself).

## Editorial Team's Perspective: Tips to Avoid Disappointment When Starting Out

The biggest reason beginners give up on power stones is probably because they feel "**nothing has changed after a few days**" and stop. This is a common scenario the editorial team has observed repeatedly.

To be honest, it's generally **not the case that your situation will change overnight just by holding a stone**. If you expect dramatic changes, you'll almost certainly be disappointed. On the other hand, when you look back after six months and realize, "Come to think of it, ever since I started carrying that stone, my mindset has gradually shifted" — that's the most realistic way power stones work.

### Adjusting Expectations: Reflecting on a Monthly Basis

- **Day 1 to 1 Week**: A period to get used to having the stone. Observe "if you remembered to wear it" rather than its effects.
- **2 Weeks to 1 Month**: Become aware of "what you were thinking when you looked at the stone." It starts to function as a **mindfulness reminder**.
- **3 Months**: Check if your "initial wish" is still central to you. If it's changed, it's OK to switch to a different stone.
- **6 Months to 1 Year**: Look back and write down "one thing that has changed about yourself during this period."

Rather than having the stone change you, it's about changing *with* the stone — if you can embrace this feeling, you'll enjoy them for a long time.

### Common Misconceptions

- ❌ "**Expensive stones work better initially**" → ✅ Clear Quartz or Rose Quartz priced at ¥1,500–¥3,000 is perfectly sufficient. **Attachment matters more than price** in determining results.
- ❌ "**It has to be a bracelet to be effective**" → ✅ Putting a tumbled stone in your pocket or placing it on your desk is absolutely fine. **Any form is acceptable as long as it's close to you**.
- ❌ "**Stones received from others are less effective**" → ✅ Gifts are traditionally considered to have strong "connections" (*en*) and are often welcomed. There's no need to worry.
