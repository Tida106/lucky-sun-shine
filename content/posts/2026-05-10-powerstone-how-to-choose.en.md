## Introduction

With hundreds of types of crystals out there, finding **the one that truly resonates with you** might seem a bit overwhelming. But don't worry! This article will introduce you to four easy-peasy approaches, making it super simple for beginners to pick their perfect stone without getting lost.

## Approach 1: Choose by Intuition (The Most Traditional Way)

> **"The stone your eyes meet is calling out to you."**

In traditional crystal shops, this is considered the most authentic way to choose your stone.

### Tips for Choosing by Intuition

- When you're browsing in a store or looking at photos, take note of **the first stone that catches your eye**.
- When you touch it, does it give you a **sense of comfort in your hand**?
- Pick the stone that makes you feel "this might be it" after seeing it a few times.
- **Don't worry too much about the price**; prioritize the stone that speaks to your intuition.

Choosing with your feelings, not just logic – this isn't some mystical trick! It's an ancient wisdom that helps us align **our own energy with that of the stone**.

## Combining Multiple Approaches Makes You an Expert!

Actually, the most ideal way is to **combine all four approaches**!

For example: You chose a stone by intuition → it also matched your birthstone → it suits your concern (e.g., love luck) → and its color aligns with the relevant chakra.

When a stone aligns on multiple layers like this, there's a good chance it's truly a **"called stone"** for you!

## Tips for Making a Smart Purchase

- **Start with stones under ¥3,000 (approximately $20-25 USD)**: Save the pricier ones for when you have more experience.
- **Compare at multiple stores**: This helps you get a feel for prices and quality.
- **Don't decide based solely on online listings**: If possible, touch the actual stone.
- **Get a certificate of authenticity**: For expensive stones, this is practically a must.
- Stones that are too cheap might carry the risk of being **dyed, artificial, or fake**.

## Summary

- The four main approaches to choosing are **intuition, birthstone, specific concerns, and color**.
- For beginners, it's realistic to start by combining **intuition and specific concerns**.
- For expensive stones, choose ones **with a certificate of authenticity** from a reliable store.
- If you find a stone that aligns on multiple layers, it's likely a stone of great destiny for you.

Your very first crystal will be a long-term partner in your life. So, take your time, have fun, and enjoy the process of choosing it!
