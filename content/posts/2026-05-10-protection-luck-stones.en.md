Many stones used for protection and warding off evil often come in **black, dark brown, or metallic hues**, and have traditionally served the role of **"absorbing and reflecting negative energies."** In ancient Rome, warriors embedded **Hematite** in their shields, in Tibet, **Black Quartz (Morion)** was used by high monks for their spiritual training, and in Japan, **black crystal spheres** have historically been used in shrine amulets.

## Meanings and Effects of Each Stone

### Main Stone: Morion (Jet Black Quartz)

**Morion** is a natural jet black quartz, often called the **"strongest warding-off stone"** and is a prime choice for protection. Tibetan monks used it for **purifying negative energies**, and even today, it's popular for protection from **"malice, spiritual anxieties, and negative places."**

-   **Powerful Evil Banishment**: Absorbs and purifies all kinds of negativity
-   **Spiritual Protection**: Creates a barrier to protect you from places and people
-   **Release from Past Trauma**: Helps let go of emotional burdens

### Obsidian (Volcanic Glass)

Born from volcanic glass, it's known as the **"mirror reflecting truth."** Used as ceremonial mirrors by the Maya and Aztecs, it's said to grant the power to **face your true self**. An amulet for **letting go of past attachments**.

### Onyx (Jet Black Banded Agate)

A stone of self-control that **helps resist temptation and protects you from bad relationships**. An amulet for when you want to break **smartphone addiction, cravings for sweets, or unhealthy social ties**.

### Hematite (Dull Metallic Luster)

Embedded in the shields of ancient Roman warriors, it's a stone of **"victory and protection."** Its **strong grounding effect** helps to bring scattered thoughts and emotions back down to earth.

### Smoky Quartz (Brownish Quartz)

A more gentle **everyday guardian stone** than Morion. It balances **stress reduction** with **quiet protection**, making it ideal for beginners seeking warding-off stones.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Is it a good gift for someone in an unlucky year?**

A. A **Morion bracelet** is a classic choice. It's good etiquette to ask for the person's permission before gifting, as protection stones often have a strong personal meaning. **Smoky Quartz** is gentle and reassuring for beginners.

**Q2. Which should I choose, Morion or Obsidian?**

A. **Morion** is more about **"protecting against external negative energies,"** focusing on guardianship, while **Obsidian** is more about **"facing your inner darkness,"** leaning towards introspection. The classic way to distinguish their use is: **external troubles → Morion**, **past traumas → Obsidian**.

**Q3. What about protection for children?**

A. A **Smoky Quartz tumbled stone** is gentle and safe. To prevent accidental ingestion, choose a **larger size (over 5cm)** or place it in a **sturdy amulet bag**. Many parents attach them to their children's school bags or backpacks.

**Q4. Will holding too many black stones make me feel depressed?**

A. Adding one stone like **Citrine (for brightness)** or **Rose Quartz (for gentleness)** can help balance things out. The combination of "**protection and light**" is a classic set for warding off evil.

**Q5. How often should I cleanse them?**

A. **Once every one to two weeks**, or sooner if something unpleasant has happened. Placing them **on clear quartz chips** or cleansing with **white sage smoke** are safe methods. **Morion can also be cleansed with salt** (for a few hours to half a day), but some Obsidian varieties are sensitive to salt and water, so it's best to check when purchasing.

## Editorial Team's Perspective: Don't Be Swayed by the Catchphrase "Strongest Warding-Off Stone"

Articles introducing protection stones often feature strong words like **"strongest," "absolute," and "complete defense,"** but our editorial team **deliberately avoids** such expressions. The reason is that these words can easily foster **over-reliance on stones**.

Even if Morion and Obsidian are called "the strongest," it refers to their **"psychological strength as an amulet."** In situations involving truly malicious individuals or actual harm, **practical action, not just the stone,** must be the top priority.

### 3 Basics to Address Before Relying on Protection Stones

1.  **Physical Environment**: Ventilation, cleaning, and tidying your room (cleanliness is fundamental, even before Feng Shui).
2.  **Lifestyle Rhythm**: Sleep, diet, and exercise (fatigue invites negativity).
3.  **Relationships**: First, distance yourself from people you can interact with less frequently.

If these basics are not in order, you might feel like **"nothing changes even though I have the stone."** Conversely, when your basics are in place, you'll feel a stronger sense of **"I am making choices,"** and the stone will truly show its power.

### When to Seek Approaches Other Than Stones

| Situation | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Harassment causing actual harm | **Consult a specialized institution** (social labor and insurance consultant, lawyer, and labor standards office) rather than a stone. |
| Persistent severe anxiety or insomnia | **Seek medical attention** rather than relying on a stone. |
| Financial troubles | **Consult financial and legal experts** rather than a stone. |
| Serious family issues | **Seek counseling or mediation** rather than a stone. |

Protection stones truly shine as amulets for **"small daily worries" and "fluctuations in mood."** It's important to make a realistic distinction that they are not the primary solution for serious problems.

### Common Misconceptions

-   ❌ "**Holding Morion completely protects against spiritual influences.**" → ✅ An amulet is ultimately a psychological support. Its meaning is diminished without **practical action** alongside it.
-   ❌ "**Black stones are gloomy and make me feel depressed.**" → ✅ While black represents the "yin" in yin and yang, combining it with "light" stones like Citrine or Clear Quartz creates balance.
-   ❌ "**I only need to hold it during my unlucky year.**" → ✅ It's valuable to hold it not only during unlucky years but also during turning points or anxious periods.
-   ❌ "**The more strong stones you layer, the stronger the protection.**" → ✅ Combining three strong stones like Morion, Obsidian, and Hematite can feel **too intense and heavy** for some people. Starting with **Smoky Quartz alone** is a gentler approach.
