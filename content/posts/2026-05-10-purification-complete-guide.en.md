## Introduction

When you wear power stones continuously, they're said to "**get tired**." Since they absorb negative energy for us, it's essential to regularly **cleanse** them to help them regain their original power. This article will introduce all 10 cleansing methods and provide a complete guide to their **suitability for different stones**.

## 3 Key Times When Cleansing is Needed

1.  **Right after purchase**: They've absorbed various energies in stores and during circulation.
2.  **After a day you felt tired or unwell**: There's a chance they've absorbed your negative energies.
3.  **Regularly (weekly to monthly)**: As part of routine maintenance.

## 10 Cleansing Methods

### 1. Crystal Clusters or Clear Quartz Chips (Universal and Most Recommended)

**Clear quartz** is the **safest and most versatile cleansing item**, suitable for almost all stones. Just place your stone on a crystal cluster or some clear quartz chips overnight.

-   Suitable for: **Almost all stones**
-   Frequency: Can be used daily
-   What you need: A crystal cluster (around 500g, ¥3,000~) or clear quartz chips (100g, ¥500~)

### 2. White Sage Smoke

A traditional Native American cleansing method. Pass your stone through the **smoke from burning sage leaves** (for 1 to 2 minutes).

-   Suitable for: **Almost all stones**
-   Frequency: When strong cleansing is needed
-   Caution: Be careful with fire. Ensure good ventilation if using indoors with smoke detectors.

### 3. Moonlight Bathing

Place your stone by a window where moonlight falls on a full moon night (overnight). This is a traditional method to **absorb lunar energy**.

-   Suitable for: **Almost all stones**
-   Frequency: Optimal on a full moon; also suitable during new moon and waxing crescent
-   Caution: Always bring it indoors by morning (many stones are sensitive to direct sunlight, as explained in the next section).

### 4. Running Water

Rinse briefly under clear river water or tap water. This method washes away impurities **both physically and energetically**.

-   Suitable for: **Quartz-based and [Chalcedony (Agate)](/blog/agate/)-based stones** (Mohs hardness around 7)
-   Not suitable for: **Water-soluble, copper-containing, or organic stones** (explained later)
-   Frequency: When strong cleansing is needed

### 5. Sunlight Bathing (Short Duration)

Charge your stone with vitality by exposing it to the morning sun.

-   Suitable for: **[Clear Quartz](/blog/clear-quartz/) (but only for a short time), [Tiger's Eye](/blog/tigers-eye/), [Carnelian](/blog/carnelian/), etc.**
-   Not suitable for: **Colored stones may fade** ([Amethyst](/blog/amethyst/), [Rose Quartz](/blog/rose-quartz-meaning/), etc.)
-   Frequency: About once a month, for 10 to 30 minutes at most

### 6. Salt

Place on natural salt, or immerse in saltwater (briefly).

-   Suitable for: **Clear Quartz**
-   Not suitable for: **Stones containing metal or water-soluble stones** (explained later)
-   Frequency: When strong cleansing is needed

### 7. Earth

Bury it in the earth to cleanse with natural energy (for a few hours to half a day).

-   Suitable for: Almost all stones (but they might get dirty)
-   Frequency: When strong cleansing is needed
-   Caution: Risk of loss, wrap it in a cloth before burying.

### 8. Sound (Crystal Singing Bowl or Tuning Fork)

Cleanse using the sound waves from a crystal singing bowl or a tuning fork.

-   Suitable for: **All stones; the gentlest cleansing method**
-   Frequency: Can be used daily
-   What you need: A tuning fork (¥3,000~) or a crystal singing bowl

### 9. Fragrance (Sandalwood or Agarwood)

Pass it through the smoke of incense. Similar principle to sage.

-   Suitable for: Almost all stones
-   Frequency: Can be used daily
-   Caution: Natural incense is better than essential oil-based ones.

### 10. Intention (Your Own Energy)

Hold it in both hands and mentally say, "**Thank you. Please reset.**"

-   Suitable for: All stones; the most personal method
-   Frequency: Anytime
-   Caution: Perform this when you are feeling calm.

### Stones Sensitive to Direct Sunlight Only (Many)

Almost all colored stones are not suitable. **They will fade with prolonged direct sunlight**.
Examples: [Amethyst](/blog/amethyst/), [Rose Quartz](/blog/rose-quartz-meaning/), [Citrine](/blog/citrine/), [Carnelian](/blog/carnelian/), [Garnet](/blog/garnet/), [Peridot](/blog/peridot/), [Aquamarine](/blog/aquamarine/), etc.

### Stones Suitable for Water, but Sensitive to Direct Sunlight

For **colored quartz-based stones** like [Rose Quartz](/blog/rose-quartz-meaning/), [Amethyst](/blog/amethyst/), and [Clear Quartz](/blog/clear-quartz/), water is generally fine, but limit direct sunlight to short periods.

### Stones Suitable for Water, Salt, and Sunlight (Few)

[Clear Quartz](/blog/clear-quartz/) (transparent quartz). Even so, it's safer to avoid prolonged direct sunlight.

## Cleansing Frequency Guidelines

-   **If you wear it daily**: Clear quartz chips once a week, sage once a month
-   **If you use it only on weekends**: Clear quartz chips once a month
-   **If you use it for important situations**: Always cleanse it before use

## Cleansing Actions to Avoid (NG)

-   Exposing to **water, salt, or direct sunlight** without knowing the proper cleansing method (leads to degradation or fading)
-   Soaking **bracelets with metal components** in salt or water (causes the metal fittings to rust)
-   Returning to a **negative environment** immediately after cleansing (can cause a rebound effect)
-   Cleansing while feeling **anger or anxiety** (your energy might transfer to the stone)

## Editorial Team's View: Cleansing is More Sustainable as a "Ritual" Than a "Means"

While we've listed 10 methods, the honest opinion of our editorial team is that **it's less about the "correctness of the method" and more about "whether you can put your heart into the act itself."**

The reason is simple: cleansing isn't a task that can be "optimized for efficiency." Instead of seeking a method that takes 10 seconds, spending three minutes to arrange your stones, place your palms over them, and mentally say, "Thank you, you've worked hard"—this kind of ritual will deepen your connection with the stones over time. Perhaps the true essence of cleansing isn't about "whether the stone gets cleansed," but rather **"whether you find peace and calm."**

### Common Misconceptions

-   ❌ "**If I don't cleanse my stone, its effects will disappear.**" → ✅ It's said the effects might "diminish," but they won't vanish completely. **Cleansing is maintenance**, not an absolute requirement.
-   ❌ "**Stronger cleansing is always better.**" → ✅ Excessive cleansing can put a strain on the stone. **Leaving it on clear quartz chips overnight** is the gentlest method and safe for frequent use.
-   ❌ "**One cleansing lasts for half a year.**" → ✅ If you wear it daily, once a week; if you use it for important situations, right before then—**timing is more important than frequency.**
-   ❌ "**Moonlight bathing can only be done on a full moon.**" → ✅ The waxing and waning crescent moons are also sufficient. A new moon has a different meaning as a "reset" type of cleansing.
