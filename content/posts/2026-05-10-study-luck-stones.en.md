## Meaning and Effects of Each Stone

### Main Stone: Fluorite (Green to Purple Gradient)

**Fluorite** is known as the "**Genius Stone**" from medieval Europe, a great helper for concentration. Its translucent green to purple hues are said to resonate with the **6th Chakra (Ajna)**, helping to **clear your thoughts**.

-   **Activate Concentration**: Direct scattered thoughts to a single point.
-   **Clear Thinking**: Organize jumbled information.
-   **Memory Retention**: Arrange and tidy up learned content.

### Sodalite (Deep Blue with White Veins)

Known as the "**Scholar's Stone**," Sodalite symbolizes logical thinking and calmness. It's helpful when you need to **think systematically without being swayed by emotions**, especially for writing papers, mathematics, or programming studies.

### Apatite (Blue to Green)

The stone of "**Goal Achievement and Knowledge Acquisition**." It boosts **persistence for long-term goals** and is perfect for "steadily continuing" learning pursuits like language study or certification exams.

### Lapis Lazuli (Deep Blue with Gold Specks)

Revered in ancient Egypt and Mesopotamia as the "**Stone of Discerning Truth**." It's believed to support the ability to **grasp the essence** of things, rather than just superficial memorization. Great for philosophy, history, and religious studies. For more details, see [Meaning and Effects of Lapis Lazuli](/blog/lapis-lazuli/).

### Garnet (Deep Red)

The "**Stone of Fruition**." With energy to **turn effort into results**, it's a classic charm for students taking exams. It provides the "never give up" power needed to sustain a long-term battle over a year. For more details, see [Meaning, Stone Words, and Effects of Garnet](/blog/garnet/).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Can I bring them into the exam venue?**

A. **Yes, you can**, but be mindful of exam regulations regarding conspicuous accessories. It's safest to **wrap a tumbled stone in a handkerchief and place it in your pocket**. Just carrying it as a charm is enough.

**Q2. What if I want to give one as a gift to a student taking an exam?**

A. A **Garnet bracelet** (symbolizing "effort bearing fruit") is a classic choice. A **Fluorite tumbled stone** (for placing on a desk) and an **Apatite keychain** (to attach to a pencil case) are also popular.

**Q3. Is it okay for children to have them?**

A. A **tumbled stone in a charm pouch** is safe. Due to choking hazards, please choose **stones larger than 5cm** for small children.

**Q4. Which stones are good for people who struggle with memorization?**

A. The classic three-stone set is **Apatite (memory retention) plus Lapis Lazuli (understanding) and Garnet (persistence)**. There's also an old tradition of **talking to your stone about what you've learned before bed**.

**Q5. How often should I cleanse them?**

A. **Once every two weeks**, and also the day before an exam if it's approaching. **Placing on clear quartz chips, moonlight bathing, or white sage** are safe methods. Since Fluorite has low hardness, be careful not to bump it.

-   ❌ "**Holding a stone makes memorization easier**" → ✅ Stones are for **concentration rituals**, memorization itself requires personal effort.

-   ❌ "**A stone bought by parents with good intentions will still work**" → ✅ If the person themselves doesn't feel "**I want to have this**," it's hard to ritualize.
