## Introduction

Takachiho Gorge, located in Takachiho Town, northern Miyazaki Prefecture, is a valley with **columnar joint cliffs** carved by the **Gokase River** through pyroclastic flow deposits from Mount Aso. Here, clear streams flow between vertical rock walls reaching 80 to 100 meters high, and **Manai Falls (Manai-no-taki)** plunges 17 meters directly into the gorge. This spectacular sight is designated as a **National Place of Scenic Beauty and Natural Monument**, making it one of Japan's most prominent scenic spots. It's located at the heart of **Takachiho**, the mythical stage of the **Tenson Kōrin (Descent of the Heavenly Grandson)**. This mystical power spot, where nature and myth converge, attracts pilgrims and tourists from all over Japan. This article covers its **geology, myths, Manai Falls, surrounding sacred sites, FAQs, and an editor's note** – all on one page.

## <span id="geology"></span>Formation and Geology of Takachiho Gorge

Takachiho Gorge is a valley formed by the erosion of pyroclastic flows by the Gokase River. These flows originated from the **massive eruptions of the Aso Caldera approximately 120,000 and 90,000 years ago**.

The cliffs feature **columnar joints** (fissures resembling bundles of hexagonal pillars) that formed as the pyroclastic flows cooled and solidified, extending for about 7 kilometers.

The surface of the rock walls bears traces of rapidly cooled magma, making it a geologically very valuable landscape. It was designated as a **National Place of Scenic Beauty and Natural Monument in 1934**. It is not only a mythical stage but also a place where you can witness the Earth's activity firsthand.

## <span id="myth"></span>The Tenson Kōrin Myth and Takachiho

The **Tenson Kōrin (Descent of the Heavenly Grandson)**, described in the *Kojiki* and *Nihon Shoki*, recounts how **Ninigi-no-Mikoto**, the grandson of Amaterasu Omikami, descended to earth with the Three Sacred Treasures. The location is recorded as **"Takachiho"**.

While there are **two theories** regarding the exact location—Takachiho Town in Miyazaki Prefecture and Mt. Takachiho-no-Mine in the Kirishima mountain range—Takachiho Town in Miyazaki Prefecture has long been a deeply revered **land of descent**.

Furthermore, this area is also the stage for the **"Amano Iwato Myth,"** where Amaterasu Omikami hid in **Amano Iwato (the Heavenly Rock Cave)**, plunging the world into darkness, until **Ame-no-Uzume-no-Mikoto** danced to coax her out and reopen the cave. Many fundamental scenes from Japanese mythology are passed down throughout this region.

## <span id="manai"></span>Manai Falls and Rental Boats

The symbol of Takachiho Gorge, **Manai Falls**, is a rare waterfall with a **17-meter drop** directly into the gorge.

It is said to be sourced from the sacred water known as **"Ama-no-Manai (Heavenly True Well) Water,"** which Ninigi-no-Mikoto is believed to have brought from the heavens during the Tenson Kōrin.

**Rental boats** that allow you to get right up close to the falls are extremely popular. A 3-person boat costs **2,000 to 4,000 yen (for 30 minutes, prices vary during peak seasons)**. On weekends and holidays, you might face **several hours of waiting** or need an **online numbered ticket**. It's recommended to queue up **around 8 AM** or check the online reservation site in advance.

## <span id="shrine"></span>Takachiho Shrine and Yokagura (Night Rituals)

**Takachiho Shrine**, located about a 15-minute walk from Takachiho Gorge, is the head shrine of the eighty-eight shrines in the Takachiho region.

It is an ancient shrine, said to have been **founded about 1,900 years ago**, and is known for its blessings of **matchmaking and marital harmony**.

Every evening from **8:00 PM, Yokagura (night rituals)** are performed. You can enjoy a one-hour excerpt of **"Iwato Kagura (Takachiho Kagura),"** a ritual dance based on myths (admission fee: 1,000 yen). The authentic Yokagura in local communities is a traditional event performed in the homes of parishioners **annually from mid-November to February**.

## <span id="amanoiwato"></span>Amano Iwato Shrine and Amanoyasukawara

**Amano Iwato Shrine**, about a 15-minute drive from Takachiho Gorge, enshrines **Amano Iwato (the Heavenly Rock Cave)**, where Amaterasu Omikami is said to have hidden, as its sacred object.

If you inquire at the shrine office, you can receive a **free guided remote worship of Amano Iwato**.

Another 10-minute walk leads to **Amanoyasukawara**, a **massive cave** said to be the place where countless deities gathered to discuss a plan during the Amano Iwato incident. Inside the cave, **innumerable stone stacks** built by visitors create a deeply mystical atmosphere.

## <span id="access"></span>Access

Train: From JR Nippo Main Line "**Nobeoka Station**," take a local bus for about 1 hour and 20 minutes to "Takachiho Bus Center," then a taxi for about 5 minutes.

Car: Approximately 2 hours from "**Kumamoto IC**" on the Kyushu Expressway, or about 60 minutes from "**Nobeoka IC**" on the Higashi-Kyushu Expressway.

**Parking**: Oshihoi Parking (paid), Ohashi Parking (free), Araragi Parking (free). During long holidays and summer, parking lots tend to fill up, so early morning arrival is recommended.

## <span id="season"></span>Best Times to Visit

**New foliage in May and autumn leaves in November** are particularly beautiful. **Early mornings (until 9 AM) or weekdays** see fewer people, and boat rentals are smoother. **Summer** is crowded with tourists seeking cool relief, but it's also when the waterfall's volume is at its fullest. During **early mornings in autumn and winter**, a sea of clouds can sometimes be seen at Kunimigaoka, allowing you to experience the world of mythology.

## <span id="manner"></span>Basic Visiting Etiquette

1. Bow once before passing through the torii gate (Takachiho Shrine and Amano Iwato Shrine).
2. The center of the approach path is for the deities; walk along the sides.
3. At the temizuya (water purification pavilion), cleanse your left hand, then your right, rinse your mouth, cleanse your left hand again, and finally clean the dipper.
4. **Two bows, two claps, one bow**.
5. For the stone stacks at Amanoyasukawara, **only add new stones and do not dismantle existing ones**.
6. To protect nature, take your trash with you, and absolutely no throwing items into the waterfall basin.

If you are unsure about basic worship etiquette, please also refer to the [Complete Guide to Basic Shrine Visits](/blog/shrine-visit-basics/).

## <span id="faq"></span>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. How do I book a boat for Manai Falls?**

A. Currently, as of **2026, the "Rental Boat Reservation Site" is transitioning to an online numbered ticket system**. For same-day tickets, you'll need to queue up early in the morning, aiming to **arrive before 8 AM**. On weekends and holidays, wait times can extend to **several hours**.

**Q2. Do I need to make a reservation for Takachiho Shrine's Yokagura?**

A. Same-day tickets (admission fee 1,000 yen for adults) are fine. Performances start **every evening at 8:00 PM**, with reception opening around 7:30 PM. For the **authentic community Yokagura** performed in parishioners' homes during **winter (mid-November to February)**, please check with the tourism association.

**Q3. What is the distance between Takachiho Gorge and Amano Iwato Shrine?**

A. It's about a **15-minute drive**. Walking is not feasible. Bus services are infrequent, so a **rental car or taxi** is convenient. The tourism association also offers **sightseeing bus tours**.

**Q4. Can I swim in the waterfall basin?**

A. **Swimming is strictly prohibited**. The water is considered sacred, and it is also dangerous for safety reasons. Please refrain from swimming and limit your experience to viewing from a boat or taking photos from the walking paths.

**Q5. When can I see the sea of clouds?**

A. The **sea of clouds at Kunimigaoka** is best seen from **mid-September to November** on **clear early mornings (around sunrise)**. It's more likely to appear on mornings with significant temperature differences, with a probability of about 30-40%. It's recommended to check the previous night's weather forecast and depart around 5 AM.
