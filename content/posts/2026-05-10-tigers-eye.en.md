The **chatoyancy effect**, where the stripes shift with the angle of light, is caused by parallel crocidolite fibers within the stone. There are several variations, including the blue-toned "Hawk's Eye" and the red "Red Tiger's Eye," which gets its color from oxidation.

## <span id="meaning"></span>Stone Lore, History, and Cultural Background

Its stone lore embodies "**Insight, Decision, and Victory**."

Ancient Roman soldiers carried Tiger's Eye as an amulet into battle, believing it would give them **"discerning eyes"** to perceive enemy movements. In Asian Buddhist cultures, the **"eye of the tiger"** was also regarded as a protective charm against misfortune, and in Egypt, it was sometimes embedded in the eyes of royal statues.

Since modern times, it has been discussed in the context of **financial and career luck**, and in Japan particularly, it has become widely known as a stone for "business prosperity," "decision-making, and resolve."

## <span id="faq"></span>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. What is the difference between Red Tiger's Eye and Hawk's Eye?**

A. **Red Tiger's Eye** is a type that has turned red due to oxidation, symbolizing greater action and passion. **Hawk's Eye** is bluish-gray and represents insight from a more distant perspective. Choose based on your preference and purpose.

**Q2. Is it the best stone for career luck?**

A. If you are frequently involved in negotiations, discussions, and decision-making, Tiger's Eye is one of the strongest candidates. For research-oriented individuals who prefer to think things through carefully, Fluorite or Lapis Lazuli might be more suitable.

**Q3. Does the direction of the stripes have any meaning?**

A. Some traditions suggest that horizontal stripes symbolize stability, while vertical stripes represent propulsion. Ultimately, choosing the **stripe direction that intuitively appeals to you** will feel the most satisfying.

**Q4. How should I choose the color of a bracelet?**

A. You can **differentiate their use based on color image**: bright gold tones for optimism, deep reddish-brown for persistence, and blue tones (Hawk's Eye) for calmness.

**Q5. Is this stone mainly for men?**

A. While it is certainly popular among men, its sophisticated colors also fit well in women's office settings. It's a stone that can be used by anyone, regardless of gender.
