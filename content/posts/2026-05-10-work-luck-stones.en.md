## Meanings and Effects of Each Stone

### Main Stone: Tiger's Eye (Golden Brown Stripes)

**Tiger's Eye** is a stone of **"decision and insight"** with stripes resembling a tiger's eye. Revered since ancient times as the "**all-seeing eye**" by warriors and merchants, it remains a beloved charm for modern executives and investors.

-   **Activating Decision-Making Power**: The ability to overcome hesitation and make choices
-   **Insight and Foresight**: Understanding others' intentions
-   **Focus for Crucial Moments**: For presentations, negotiations, and interviews

For more details, refer to [The Meaning, Stone Language, and Effects of Tiger's Eye](/blog/tigers-eye/).

### Citrine (Yellow Quartz)

The **"Stone of Business Prosperity."** Its bright yellow color fosters **confidence and positivity**, empowering you in negotiations and sales. When combined with Tiger's Eye, it forms the classic **"decision-making and confidence"** duo. For more details, refer to [The Meaning, Stone Language, and Effects of Citrine](/blog/citrine/).

### Lapis Lazuli (Deep Blue)

Revered in Egypt and Mesopotamia as the **"Stone of the Heavens" and "Stone of Truth,"** it symbolizes leadership and wisdom. It's a guardian stone for those in **leadership positions** and individuals who need to make responsible decisions. For more details, refer to [The Meaning and Effects of Lapis Lazuli](/blog/lapis-lazuli/).

### Fluorite (Green to Purple)

Known as the **"Stone of Geniuses,"** it aids concentration. It's said to excel at **clearing thoughts** and organizing confused information. Ideal for desk work, analytical tasks, and proposal creation.

### Onyx (Jet Black)

A guardian stone that **helps you resist temptations and maintain focus**. For modern people who often get distracted by smartphones and social media, it serves as an **anchor to bring your awareness back to "what needs to be done."**

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. I'm worried about looking out of place if I show my stones in the office.**

A. A **small bracelet** or **tumbled stone discreetly placed in a pen holder** feels natural. Many people also place a **single, ring-sized stone** in a corner of their desk, and few people notice, so there's no need to worry.

**Q2. What if I'm considering a job change?**

A. For job hunting, the classic combination is **Tiger's Eye and Iolite (the compass stone).** Please also refer to our specialized article [Power Stones for Career Change Luck](/blog/career-change-luck-stones/).

**Q3. What if I want to give one as a gift?**

A. A **Tiger's Eye bracelet** is a classic choice for a boss or senior colleague, a **Fluorite tumbled stone** for new graduates, and a **Lapis Lazuli pendant** for celebrating independence.

**Q4. Can I hold multiple stones simultaneously when I want to focus?**

A. **Up to two or three stones** is practical. The set of three – **Fluorite (focus), Onyx (resisting temptation), and Clear Quartz (amplification)** – is a classic for desk work.

**Q5. How often should I cleanse my stones?**

A. **Every two weeks to once a month** is a general guideline. If you feel tired from work during the week, cleanse them sooner. Placing them on **Clear Quartz tumbled stones**, **moonlight bathing**, or using **white sage** are safe methods.

### The Editorial Team's Recommended "1-Month Observation Protocol"

1.  **Week 1**: When you arrive at your desk in the morning, gaze at your stone → State "the single most important task for today" out loud.
