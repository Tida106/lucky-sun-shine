## Introduction

Aquarius is an **Air sign** that the sun passes through from **January 20th to February 18th**. Its ruling planet is **Uranus**, symbolizing **"innovation, freedom, and originality."** Among the 12 zodiac signs, Aquarius is the most **ahead of its time**, possessing the power to **create new values** without being constrained by existing frameworks. In this article, we'll put together a guide to crystals that suit the Aquarian personality and fortune, **focusing on Amethyst as the guardian stone**, and also covering complementary stones, lucky colors, fortune-boosting actions, and FAQs—all in one place!

Aquarians are people of the motto: **"All for everyone, while staying true to yourself."** Their strengths lie in **original ideas**, **independence from groups**, and a strong **spirit of philanthropy**. **Weaknesses (the flip side)**: Can be seen as eccentric, Difficulty expressing emotions, Can appear aloof, Difficulty fitting into groups

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Why is Amethyst a good match for Aquarius?**

A. Amethyst is the stone of **"spirituality and intuition,"** and Aquarius is the **"future-oriented"** sign that foresees the times. Both deeply resonate through their **"ability to grasp the unseen,"** enhancing original ideas and intuitive accuracy.

**Q2. How is this "Amethyst as main stone" different from Virgo's?**

A. For **Virgo, Amethyst resonates with "purification of thought,"** while for **Aquarius, it resonates with "spirituality and innovation."** Even with the same stone, Virgo uses it **"to organize"** while Aquarius uses it **"to open new paths,"** showing a difference in nuance.

**Q3. Which stone should I give as a gift?**

A. For an **Aquarius born in January or February**, an **Amethyst pendant** (3,000 to 15,000 JPY) is a classic choice. For researchers, an **octahedral Fluorite crystal** will be appreciated, and for creative professionals, a **Labradorite ring** is a great idea!

**Q4. Aquarians are said to be unemotional, is that true?**

A. That's actually the **flip side of their "calm, objective perspective."** If you supplement with **Rose Quartz** for "self-love" and **Moonstone** for "intuitive emotions," those seemingly shallow emotions can transform into **deep philanthropy**.

**Q5. Which zodiac signs are compatible with Aquarius?**

A. Generally, other Air signs **Gemini and Libra**, and Fire signs **Aries and Sagittarius** are considered compatible.
