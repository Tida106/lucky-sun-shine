## Introduction

Aries (Ohitsuji-za) is the **pioneer** of the twelve zodiac signs. The sun passes through this sign from **March 21 to April 19**. It belongs to the **fire element** and its ruling planet is **Mars**. Aries embodies the fundamental energies of "**moving forward, initiating, and burning brightly**," making it a zodiac sign overflowing with vitality. In this article, we'll pack everything into one page: power stones that suit the Aries personality and luck, **focusing on the guardian stone Carnelian**, along with compatible stones, lucky colors, auspicious actions, and FAQs.

## Meanings and Effects of Each Stone

### Main Stone: Carnelian (Orange-Red)

**Carnelian** is known as the "**Stone of Action and Achievement**," having been used in pharaohs' breastplates in ancient Egypt and as a signet stone carried by **Napoleon** into battle. Its origin is the Latin word "**carne**" (meaning "flesh"), symbolizing vitality itself.

- **Activating Drive**: Encourages you to overcome hesitation and take the first step.
- **Achievement and Success**: Provides the energy to push towards your goals.
- **Restoration of Vitality**: The power of red resonates with the First Chakra (Root Chakra).
- **Courage**: Calms fears and nervousness.

For more details, please refer to [Meaning, Stone Language, and Effects of Carnelian](/blog/carnelian/).

### Compatible Stone: Garnet (Deep Red)

**Garnet** is the "**Stone of Harvest and Victory**." It supports Aries' drive to **produce results**. As a charm for long-term challenges and crucial moments, combining it with Carnelian creates a harmonious flow of "**action and reward**." For more details, please refer to [Meaning, Stone Language, and Effects of Garnet](/blog/garnet/).

### Compatible Stone: Hematite (Dull Metallic Luster)

**Hematite** is the "**Stone of Victory**" that ancient Roman warriors **embedded in their shields**. It supports Aries' weakness of "**moving too much and getting nowhere**" by providing a **grounding** force that helps you **stay rooted**. For more details, please refer to [Meaning, Stone Language, and Effects of Hematite](/blog/hematite/).

### Compatible Stone: Citrine (Golden Yellow)

**Citrine** is known as the "**Merchant's Stone**" and the "**Sun Stone**." It further enhances Aries' **brightness and positive outlook**, while boosting their **confidence**. For more details, please refer to [Meaning, Stone Language, and Effects of Citrine](/blog/citrine/).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Do these stones suit me if Aries is my Moon Sign or Rising Sign, not just my Sun Sign?**

A. Yes, absolutely! People who have **Aries placements in their horoscope (especially Moon, Venus, Mars, or Rising Sign)** are said to have good compatibility with Aries-aligned stones. Even if Aries isn't your Sun sign, you can still use these stones as a charm for times when your **actions are stalled** or when you **need an extra spark of fire**.

**Q2. Is Ruby not suitable for Aries?**

A. **Some schools of thought consider Ruby to be a primary stone for Aries**. This article, however, focuses on Carnelian as it's the most common and popular choice in modern Japan. Ruby, with its hardness of 9 and rarity, is also highly recommended to be added as **special occasion jewelry**.

**Q3. Which stone should I choose as a gift?**

A. For **Aries born in March or April**, a **Carnelian bracelet** (¥3,000-¥10,000) is a classic choice. For men, a **Hematite bracelet** is often selected, and to convey a celebratory feel, a **single-stone Garnet pendant** is a lovely option.

**Q4. What if I feel Carnelian "doesn't suit" me?**

A. For Aries individuals who are **"already too active,"** Carnelian might be too intense. In such cases, using **Amethyst** or **Aquamarine** to **supplement "calmness"** would be a better fit.

**Q5. Which zodiac signs are compatible with Aries?**

A. Generally, other fire signs like **Leo and Sagittarius**, and air signs like **Gemini and Libra** are considered to be highly compatible. For more details, please check the corresponding signs in the [Comprehensive Guide to Power Stones by Zodiac Sign](/blog/zodiac-powerstones-guide/).
