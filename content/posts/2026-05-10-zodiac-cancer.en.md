## Introduction

The zodiac sign Cancer (**June 22 - July 22**) is a **Water element** sign, with **the Moon (Luna)** as its ruling planet. It symbolizes **"family love, nurturing, and sensitivity."** Among the 12 zodiac signs, Cancer has the deepest affection for **home, family, and loved ones**, and excels at sensing others' feelings with delicate sensitivity. In this article, we'll summarize the power stones that suit Cancer's personality and fortune, focusing on its **guardian stone, Moonstone**, and covering complementary stones, lucky colors, auspicious actions, and an FAQ, all on one page.

## Meanings and Effects of Each Stone

### Main Stone: Moonstone (Milky White and Blue Sheen)

**Moonstone** was revered in ancient India as an **"incarnation of the Moon Goddess Diana."** It is said to be most in tune with Cancer, ruled by the Moon, symbolizing **"lunar rhythms, femininity, and new beginnings."**

-   **Activates intuition**: Stimulates the Sixth Chakra (Third Eye)
-   **Harmonizes femininity**: Symbolically supports rhythms related to menstruation, pregnancy, and menopause
-   **New beginnings**: Encourages new moon wishes
-   **Protects sensitivity**: Transforms delicacy from a "weakness" into a "strength"

For more details, please refer to [Moonstone Meaning, Stone Language, and Effects](/blog/moonstone/).

### Complementary Stone: Rose Quartz (Pale Pink)

**Rose Quartz** is a quintessential stone of **"love and self-love."** It helps balance Cancer's tendency to **"love others while putting themselves last"** by encouraging them to **"love themselves too."** For more details, please refer to [Rose Quartz Meaning, Stone Language, and Effects](/blog/rose-quartz-meaning/).

### Complementary Stone: Pearl

**Pearl** symbolizes **"health, longevity, and purity."** It is deeply connected to Cancer's theme of **"protecting the home"** and serves as a family amulet, often worn for ceremonies and special occasions.

### Complementary Stone: Kunzite (Pale Pink to Purple)

**Kunzite** is known as the **"Mother of Love,"** a stone of unconditional love. It most gently supports Cancer's **nurturing nature.** For more details, please refer to [Kunzite Meaning and Effects](/blog/kunzite/).
