## Welcome!

Leo, or Shishiza as we say in Japanese, is a **Fire element** sign that the sun passes through from **July 23 to August 22**. Its ruling planet is **the Sun (Sol)** itself, and among the 12 zodiac signs, Leo's mission is to **"shine authentically."** It symbolizes **"charisma, self-esteem, and creativity,"** and Leos naturally possess a strong aptitude for being in the spotlight and expressing themselves. In this article, I've put together a one-page guide covering power stones that suit Leo's personality and fortune, **focusing on their guardian stone, Tiger's Eye**, along with compatible stones, lucky colors, good luck actions, and FAQs!

Leos are signs that live their lives **"as grandly as the Sun."** They value leadership, expressiveness, and their authentic selves, and they have an innate talent for **shining brightly in front of people.**

**Strengths**: Charisma, generosity, creativity, dramatic expression, pride

## Meanings and Effects of Each Stone

### Main Stone: Tiger's Eye (Yellow-brown and Black)

**Tiger's Eye**, living up to its name "Tiger's Eye," is a stone with a shimmering light due to its **chatoyancy (cat's eye effect)**. Loved by everyone from ancient Roman soldiers to modern business professionals, it's a prominent stone for **"decision-making and discerning the truth."**

-   **Decisiveness**: A tiger's gaze that cuts through hesitation
-   **Financial and Career Luck**: Popular as the "Stone of Business Prosperity"
-   **Discerning the Truth**: Insight that isn't swayed by appearances
-   **Boosting Charisma**: Enhances dignity and presence

For more details, please refer to [Tiger's Eye Meaning, Stone Lore, and Effects](/blog/tigers-eye/).

### Compatible Stone: Citrine (Golden Yellow)

**Citrine** is known as the **"stone that crystallizes sunlight,"** and it most directly strengthens Leo's **"solar nature."** For more details, please refer to [Citrine Meaning, Stone Lore, and Effects](/blog/citrine/).

### Compatible Stone: Sunstone (Orange)

**Sunstone** is literally the **"stone of the sun."** It boosts Leo's **leadership and their charisma to attract people.** For more details, please refer to [Sunstone Meaning and Effects](/blog/sunstone/).

### Compatible Stone: Ruby and/or Garnet (Deep Red)

**Ruby** is the **"King of Gemstones,"** and **Garnet** is the stone of **"harvest and victory."** They support Leo's **passion and competitive spirit.** For more details, please refer to [Garnet Meaning, Stone Lore, and Effects](/blog/garnet/).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Why is Tiger's Eye a good match for Leo?**

A. Tiger's Eye is known as the **"stone of decision and dignity,"** and Leo is a zodiac sign that strives to **"live authentically and boldly."** Both deeply resonate with the theme of **"believing in oneself without hesitation,"** making them the strongest combination for leaders and those who are often in the spotlight.

**Q2. I heard that some traditions consider Ruby the main stone for Leo. Is that true?**

A. Yes, that's right! Some traditions consider **"Ruby as the gem of the sun,"** and in **European classical astrology** particularly, Ruby is often the star for Leo. In this article, we've adopted Tiger's Eye as the **"most widely adopted and practical choice in Japan,"** but adding Ruby as a **"special occasion jewel"** is also highly recommended!

**Q3. Which stone should I give as a gift?**

A. For **Leos born in July and/or August**, a **Tiger's Eye bracelet** (3,000–12,000 yen) is a classic choice. If you want to add a celebratory touch, a **single-stone Citrine pendant** or a **Ruby ring** would be very much appreciated. A great feature of Leo-friendly stones is that they suit men just as well, without any awkwardness!

**Q4. How can Leos make the most of their high pride?**

A. Pride is an expression of **"high standards for oneself."** If you cultivate an "eye for discerning the truth" with **Tiger's Eye** and embrace "warm dignity" with **Sunstone**, your pride can transform into **charisma!**

**Q5. Which zodiac signs are compatible with Leo?**

A. Generally, other Fire signs like **Aries and Sagittarius**, and Air signs like **Gemini and Libra** are considered to be good matches.
