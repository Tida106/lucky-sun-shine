Hey there! Libra is a **Wind element** zodiac sign, with the Sun passing through it from **September 23rd to October 23rd**. Its ruling planet is **Venus**, symbolizing **"harmony, aesthetic sense, and balance."** Libras are known for having the best **aesthetic sense** and **fairness** among the 12 zodiac signs, possessing a talent for **creating harmony** between people. In this article, Sun-chan will bring you a one-stop guide to power stones that perfectly suit Libra's personality and fortune, **focusing on the guardian stone Rose Quartz**, and covering compatible stones, lucky colors, actions for good fortune, and even an FAQ!

## Meaning and Effects of Each Stone

### Main Stone: Rose Quartz (Pale Pink)

**Rose Quartz** is **Venus's quintessential stone**, sharing the same **Venusian rulership** as Libra. It symbolizes **love, beauty, and self-love**, making it the most natural stone to support Libra's **aesthetic sense and relationships.**

-   **Enhances Aesthetic Sense**: Brings out inner beauty.
-   **Harmonizes Relationships**: Fosters empathy for others while maintaining personal boundaries.
-   **Promotes Self-Love**: Helps Libras, who can be prone to people-pleasing, find their "inner compass."
-   **Boosts Love Luck**: Blessings from Venus!

For more details, check out [The Meaning, Stone Language, and Effects of Rose Quartz](/blog/rose-quartz-meaning/).

### Compatible Stone: Ametrine (Purple and Yellow)

**Ametrine** is a rare stone where **Amethyst (purple) and Citrine (yellow) coexist in one crystal.** It resonates most symbolically with Libras when they want to balance **"conflicting choices."** For more, see [The Meaning and Effects of Ametrine](/blog/ametrine/).

### Compatible Stone: Opal (Iridescent)

**Opal** is the stone of **"iridescent creativity."** It helps Libras express their **diverse aesthetic sense.** Check out [The Meaning and Effects of Opal](/blog/opal/) for more.

### Compatible Stone: Aquamarine (Light Blue)

**Aquamarine** is the stone of **"communication and integrity."** It empowers Libras to **"gently convey difficult truths."** Learn more at [The Meaning and Effects of Aquamarine](/blog/aquamarine/).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Why is Rose Quartz a main stone for both Libra and Taurus?**

A. Both signs are **ruled by Venus**. The difference lies in how they **utilize Rose Quartz's loving energy**: **Taurus, an Earth sign, focuses on sensual pleasures**, while **Libra, an Air sign, emphasizes harmony in human relationships.**

**Q2. How can Libras make their indecisiveness work for them?**

A. It's actually a **"virtue of seeking fairness."** We recommend a combination of **Ametrine** to practice "making decisions after considering both sides" and **Tiger's Eye** to "boost you at the moment of decision."

**Q3. Which stone is best for a gift?**

A. For **Libras born in September or October**, a **Rose Quartz pendant** (3,000 E5,000 JPY) is a classic choice. **Matching jewelry** for a partner, or an **Opal ring** (which is also an October birthstone) would also be delightful.

**Q4. Do Libras get easily exhausted by relationships?**

A. Yes, Libras are prone to feeling drained due to their **high empathy**. We recommend using **Rose Quartz** to replenish self-love, and **Morion** or **Black Tourmaline** to create a "shield that doesn't absorb others' emotions."

**Q5. Which zodiac signs are most compatible with Libra?**

A. Generally, fellow Air signs like **Gemini and Aquarius**, and Fire signs like **Aries and Leo** are considered highly compatible.
