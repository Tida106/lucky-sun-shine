**Q1. Why is Turquoise a good match for Sagittarius?**

A. Turquoise has been known as the **'traveler's amulet'** since ancient times, perfectly aligning with Sagittarius's themes of **'adventure, freedom, and movement.'** Just like Native Americans **tied it to their horse's saddles** for safe journeys, this stone naturally resonates with Sagittarians today, Sun-chan says!

A. Yep, it's true! Turquoise can actually **change color to a greenish hue** from **sebum, sweat, cosmetics, or detergents** (this is sometimes called **'turquoisematter'** or 'veiling'). Some people love this 'aging of personality' and see it as unique, but if you prefer to keep its vibrant blue, just make sure to **gently wipe it dry after each use**.
