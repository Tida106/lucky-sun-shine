Scorpio is a **Water element** zodiac sign, with the Sun transiting through it from **October 24 to November 22**. Its ruling planet is **Pluto**, symbolizing **"depth, insight, and transformation."** It's the most **intense and mysterious** sign among the 12 zodiacs, shying away from superficial relationships and seeking genuine connections. In this article, we'll put together a one-page guide covering crystals suited for the Scorpio personality and fortune, **focusing on their guardian stone, Obsidian**, along with compatible stones, lucky colors, fortune-boosting actions, and FAQs.

## Meanings and Effects of Each Stone

### Main Stone: Obsidian

**Obsidian** is a **"volcanic glass"**, a mysterious stone revered as the **"Mirror of Truth"** by ancient Mayans and Aztecs. It resonates most deeply with Scorpio's ability to **"discern the essence"**.

-   **Mirror of Truth**: Reflects your inner self and others' true intentions.
-   **Releasing the Past**: Helps let go of trauma and attachments.
-   **Powerful Protection**: Cuts through negative energies.
-   **Concentration**: Grounds the First Chakra.

**Charoite** is one of the **"World's Three Great Healing Stones,"** known as the stone of **"transformation and realization."** It strongly supports Scorpio's theme of **"profound change at life's turning points."** For more details, please refer to [Meaning and Effects of Charoite](/blog/charoite/).

### Compatible Stone: Garnet (Deep Red)

### Compatible Stone: Labradorite (Aurora)

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Why is Obsidian a good match for Scorpio?**

A. Obsidian is known as the **"Mirror reflecting truth" and a stone of "releasing the past,"** while Scorpio is the sign that **"discerns the essence and transforms."** Both resonate most deeply with the theme of **"facing the depths, not just the surface,"** and are considered a powerful combination to support major turning points in life.

**Q2. I've heard Obsidian can be too intense. Is that true?**

A. Since Obsidian **"reflects even your inner shadows,"** it can sometimes feel intense for beginners in healing or when your heart is feeling fragile. In such cases, combining it with **Rose Quartz** or **Amethyst** as a **"cushion of gentleness"** can bring comfort.

**Q3. Which stone would be a good gift?**

A. For **Scorpios born in October or November**, an **Obsidian bracelet** (¥2,000-¥10,000) is a classic choice. If you want to express depth, a **Garnet pendant** would be lovely, and to highlight their mysterious side, a **Labradorite ring** would be appreciated.

**Q4. How can a Scorpio's jealousy be used positively?**

A. That's actually the **flip side of "the strength to love deeply."** If you use **Obsidian** to "objectively view your emotions" and **Rose Quartz** to "practice trusting others," jealousy can transform into **profound love**.

**Q5. Which zodiac signs are most compatible with Scorpio?**

A. Generally, other Water signs like **Cancer and Pisces**, and Earth signs like **Virgo and Capricorn**, are considered to be good matches.
