## Meaning and Effects of Each Stone

### Main Stone: Rose Quartz (Pale Pink)

**Rose Quartz** is known as the **"oldest love charm stone,"** even unearthed from Mesopotamian ruins dating back to 7000 BCE. Its pale pink color symbolizes **"self-love, romantic love, and beauty,"** and it's often said to be the stone **closest to Venus.**

- **Self-love and Self-esteem**: Rediscover the feeling of cherishing yourself
- **Romantic luck**: Bring out honest feelings
- **Aesthetic sense**: Boost inner beauty
- **Generosity**: Encourage kindness and forgiveness

For more details, refer to [The Meaning, Stone Language, and Effects of Rose Quartz](/blog/rose-quartz-meaning/).

### Complementary Stone: Emerald / Jade (Green)

**Emerald** symbolizes **"eternal youth and love,"** while **Jade** represents **"prosperity and longevity."** They play a supportive role in transforming Taurus's **perseverance** into **results (fruits).** For more details, refer to [The Meaning and Effects of Jade](/blog/jade/).

### Complementary Stone: Aventurine (Green)

**Aventurine**, often called **"Indian Jade,"** is a stone of **gentle wealth and healing.** It's highly compatible with Taurus's **"steady accumulation"** nature, fostering long-term abundance. For more details, refer to [The Meaning and Effects of Aventurine](/blog/aventurine/).

### Complementary Stone: Morganite (Pale Pink)

**Morganite**, a sister stone to Aquamarine, symbolizes **"gentleness and marriage."** It boosts Taurus's **marriage and family luck.** For more details, refer to [The Meaning and Effects of Morganite](/blog/morganite/).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Why is Rose Quartz a good match for Taurus?**

A. Rose Quartz is considered **Venus's stone**, and it's believed to be most attuned to Taurus, which is ruled by Venus. The gentleness of Rose Quartz resonates with Taurus's themes of **"cherishing oneself and enjoying beautiful things."**

**Q2. What if I don't like pink?**

A. You have the option to make **Aventurine (pale green)** or **Jade (deep green)** your main stone. Since Taurus is an **"Earth" element** sign, **green stones** can also sufficiently support its zodiac energy.

**Q3. Which stone would be a good gift?**

A. For **Taurus individuals born in April or May**, a **Rose Quartz pendant** (¥3,000 - ¥15,000) is a classic choice. If it's for a wedding gift, a **Morganite ring**, or to add a celebratory touch, a **single Emerald pendant** would be much appreciated.

**Q4. Are May's birthstone (Emerald) and Taurus's main stone different?**

A. Yes, **May's birthstone is Emerald**, and Taurus's main stone is **Rose Quartz**; different traditions exist in parallel. It's recommended to **combine both**, and for more on May's birthstone, you can also refer to [May Birthstones: Emerald and Jade](/blog/birthstone-may/).

**Q5. Which zodiac signs are compatible with Taurus?**

A. Generally, other Earth signs like **Virgo and Capricorn**, and Water signs like **Cancer and Pisces** are considered compatible.
