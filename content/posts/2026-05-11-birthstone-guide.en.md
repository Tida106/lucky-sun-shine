## How to Choose and Wear Your Birthstone

### Main or Alternative Stone: Which to Choose?

In the modern jewelry industry, **multiple birthstones** are recognized for each month. In this quick reference guide, I've listed both the main birthstone (traditionally the most recognized stone) and alternative stones.

-   If the **main stone is expensive and hard to get** (like diamond, emerald, ruby, or sapphire, etc.), choosing an alternative stone is a practical option.
-   It's perfectly fine to choose based on **color and aesthetic preference**. After all, the stone you're drawn to is the one you'll truly enjoy wearing.
-   When **gifting as an accessory**, prioritize the recipient's preferred color and style.

### Tips for Feeling the Effects

-   **Wear it daily for the first month of your birth month**: This is when the sun's energy is said to resonate most strongly with the stone.
-   **Incorporate it into your daily life with a pendant or ring**: Accessories that touch your skin directly are highly recommended!
-   **Stones gifted on your birthday are especially meaningful**: It's said that birthstones received as a gift are particularly imbued with energy.
-   **Keep it by your bedside, even while you sleep**: During your birth month, make a habit of keeping it close by, even when you're sleeping.

## Basic Purification Methods

-   **Place it on clear quartz chips**: This is the safest method and suitable for almost all stones.
-   **Pass it through white sage smoke**: 1 to 2 minutes is sufficient.
-   **Moonlight bathing**: Place it by a window on a full moon night.

> Please always check the **specific purification method for your individual stone**, as some stones are sensitive to water, salt, and direct sunlight. Turquoise, Opal, Moonstone, Emerald, and Lapis Lazuli, in particular, are sensitive to water, so be careful!

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Isn't there only one birthstone per month?**
A. In modern times, many months have multiple alternative stones recognized. That's why this guide's quick reference table lists both the main birthstone and alternative stones. In Japan, the list of "birthstones" was revised in 2021 for the first time in about 60 years, and 2 to 5 types of stones are officially registered for each month.

**Q2. What if I don't feel a connection to my birth month's stone?**
A. There's no need to force yourself to choose! Please select **whatever stone you're drawn to, including the alternative stones**. Another popular approach is to wear the birthstone of a family member or partner as a "good luck charm."

**Q3. Do expensive birthstones (diamond, emerald, ruby, sapphire) only have meaning if they are real?**
A. Culturally, **alternative stones are perfectly fine**. In fact, if you wear it every day, an alternative stone that you don't have to worry about scratching is more practical. Many enthusiasts choose to own a genuine stone as a special piece of jewelry for life's milestones (like weddings or anniversaries), distinguishing it from their everyday wear.

**Q4. What should I keep in mind when gifting a birthstone?**
A. The golden rule is to consider the recipient's **skin tone and preferred style** (modern or classical). For example, don't just decide "it must be Topaz because they were born in November"; it's a good idea to be flexible and consider if Citrine might be more affordable and easier for daily wear. Beyond color, secretly observing the jewelry they wear on their hands or neck can make choosing much easier.

**Q5. Can I wear a birthstone other than my own, just for that specific month?**
A. Of course! It's traditionally said that each month's birthstone **"resonates most with that month's energy."** So, it's considered effective to wear a birthstone for a month even if it's not your own.

**Q6. Is it okay to gift birthstones to children?**
A. It's **a popular gift imbued with hopes and wishes**. You could gift a small tumbled stone as a paperweight in the year they're born, and then have it fashioned into jewelry for their coming of age or 20th birthday. This makes it a gift that can be cherished across generations. For infants and toddlers who might put things in their mouths, it's safer to display it out of their reach to prevent choking.
