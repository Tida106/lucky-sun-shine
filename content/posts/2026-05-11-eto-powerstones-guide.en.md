## The Relationship Between Chinese Zodiac and Power Stones

The Chinese zodiac, or "Eto" as it's known in Japan, originated from ancient China's twelve animal signs. It represents a **12-year cycle of time and fortune**. Each animal is associated with a specific **direction, season, and one of the Five Elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water)**. It's believed that your birth year's zodiac sign reflects your **"innate nature throughout life."**

The connection with power stones is made based on each zodiac sign's **personality, element, and traditional beliefs**. For instance, the **Rat, with its "Water element,"** is often paired with **Citrine** (which is believed to warm water and attract wealth). The **Horse, possessing a "Fire element,"** might be linked to **Carnelian** (to transform energy into achievement). And for the **Dragon, deeply connected to "Dragon God folklore,"** the royal stone **Lapis Lazuli** is a fitting choice. This shows how stones are chosen for their "compatibility" from both ancient legends and the Five Elements.

The year you become a "**Toshi Otoko**" or "**Toshi Onna**" (the year of your own zodiac sign, occurring every 12 years) is considered a special milestone to reset your life's rhythm. It's a long-standing custom to get a new guardian stone during this significant year.

> **Please Note**: The "effects" of power stones are cultural and symbolic in nature. They are not guaranteed to have medical or scientific efficacy.

## How to Find Your Chinese Zodiac Sign

Your Chinese zodiac sign is determined by the remainder when your birth year (Western calendar) is divided by 12.

| Remainder | Zodiac Sign |
|---|---|
| 4 | Rat (Nezumi) |
| 5 | Ox (Ushi) |
| 6 | Tiger (Tora) |
| 7 | Rabbit (Usagi) |
| 8 | Dragon (Tatsu) |
| 9 | Snake (Hebi) |
| 10 | Horse (Uma) |
| 11 | Sheep (Hitsuji) |
| 0 | Monkey (Saru) |
| 1 | Rooster (Tori) |
| 2 | Dog (Inu) |
| 3 | Boar (Inoshishi) |

**Example**: Born in 1990 → 1990 ÷ 12 = 165 remainder 10 → **Year of the Horse**

> **Please Note**: In the traditional lunar calendar, the new year begins around Risshun (February 4th). If you were born in early January or February, your zodiac sign might be that of the previous year, so please be aware.

## Chinese Zodiac Compatibility

Traditionally, the Chinese zodiac signs have both **"highly compatible combinations"** and **"challenging combinations."**

### San-Go (Tripartite Harmony) — The Best Compatibility

Three zodiac signs form a triangle and resonate powerfully with each other.

- **Monkey, Rat, and Dragon** (Tripartite Harmony of Metal, Water, and Earth)
- **Tiger, Horse, and Dog** (Tripartite Harmony of Wood, Fire, and Earth)
- **Snake, Rooster, and Ox** (Tripartite Harmony of Fire, Metal, and Earth)
- **Boar, Rabbit, and Sheep** (Tripartite Harmony of Water, Wood, and Earth)

### Riku-Go (Six Harmonies) — Complementary Compatibility

Two zodiac signs form a harmonious pair, complementing each other.

- **Rat and Ox** / **Tiger and Boar** / **Rabbit and Dog** / **Dragon and Rooster** / **Snake and Monkey** / **Horse and Sheep**

### Chuu (Opposition) — Compatibility that Requires Care

These are zodiac signs in opposite positions. While friction can arise, it's also said that they are **strongly attracted to each other**.

- **Rat and Horse** / **Ox and Sheep** / **Tiger and Monkey** / **Rabbit and Rooster** / **Dragon and Dog** / **Snake and Boar**

Compatibility is a **"tendency," not a "fixed rule."** Even with a **Chuu** relationship, it's believed that by respecting each other's differences, the relationship can last and thrive.

## How to Spend Your "Toshi Otoko" or "Toshi Onna" Year

The year of your own Chinese zodiac sign, which comes around once every 12 years (your "Toshi Otoko" or "Toshi Onna" year), is considered a special milestone for aligning your life's rhythm.

- **Get a new guardian stone for the New Year**: As a protective charm to accompany you throughout the year.
- **Cleanse your stones around Setsubun (February 3rd)**: To align with the start of the traditional lunar new year.
- **Strengthen your protection if it coincides with your "yakudoshi" (unlucky year)**: By using Clear Quartz, Morion, or other protective stones in combination.
- **Make big decisions in the first half of the year**: It's easier to ride the year's flow.
- **Write down your goals and place them with your stone by your bedside**: For new moon manifestations.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Should I prioritize my Chinese zodiac stone or my astrological zodiac stone?**

A. **It's ideal to have both!** The Chinese zodiac reflects your **"innate nature over a 12-year cycle,"** while astrological signs are influenced by the **"period the sun passes through."** They represent different rhythms. Enjoy deepening your connection with guardian stones by combining them. For comparison, you can also check out the [Complete Guide to Astrological Power Stones](/blog/zodiac-powerstones-guide/).

**Q2. What if I feel my Chinese zodiac stone doesn't "match" me?**

A. Traditional compatibility is just a **"guideline."** The stone you feel drawn to is often said to be the one meant for you, so there's no need to force yourself to stick to a guardian stone if it doesn't feel right. It's perfectly fine to choose from **Clear Quartz** or other **purpose-specific stones** (check out the [Complete Guide to Lucky Power Stones by Purpose](/blog/luck-powerstones-complete-guide/)).

**Q3. Is it true that the zodiac sign changes with the traditional lunar new year?**

A. Yes. In ancient times, the Chinese zodiac traditionally considered **Risshun (around February 4th)** as the start of the new year. For those born between January 1st and February 3rd, your zodiac sign might officially be that of the previous year according to this calendar. If you're unsure, feel free to use both zodiac signs as your protective charms.

**Q4. What's a good Chinese zodiac charm for children?**

A. A **tumbled stone of their guardian stone** in a charm bag is a safe option. To prevent accidental ingestion, choose a stone that is **5cm or larger**. Many families attach them to their children's backpacks or school bags.

**Q5. Is it okay to gift a Chinese zodiac stone?**

A. **Chinese zodiac signs are deeply personal protective symbols**, so they make excellent gifts! They are also a classic gift for someone celebrating their "Toshi Otoko" or "Toshi Onna" year. **Bracelets** (3,000-10,000 JPY) or **pendants** with their guardian stone are usually well-received.
