## 3 Steps to Choosing Your Crystal by Purpose

1.  **First, choose one category and one stone.**
    Start with one main stone from the category that most closely matches your goal. If you get multiple at once, it becomes harder to gauge your personal connection with them.
2.  **Wear it for about two weeks to assess how it feels.**
    If it feels right, continue. If you feel restless or uncomfortable, switch to a complementary stone or try a different stone within the same category.
3.  **Once comfortable, add one complementary stone.**
    Using a combination of one to two stones in your daily routine is a style that's easy to maintain long-term.

## Basic Purification Methods

The more categories and stones you have, the more important regular purification becomes.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Can I benefit from having stones for multiple categories simultaneously?**

A. **Around two to three categories** is generally realistic. For example, combining related purposes like "Wealth Luck (Citrine) and Career Luck (Tiger's Eye) and Protection (Onyx)" is an easy pairing. However, if the purposes are scattered, such as "Love Luck and Career Change Luck and Health Luck," your own energy may also become dispersed.

**Q2. Should I wear crystals every day?**

A. **Generally, yes, daily wear** is traditional. However, taking them off during bathing, sports, or water-related chores helps them last longer. As you get used to it, you can also enjoy choosing "this category today" based on your mood.

**Q3. Should I part with a stone once my wish comes true?**

A. There's no need to part with it. Most people keep stones that have helped grant their wishes, considering them "deeply connected stones." However, if a stone clearly feels like its purpose is complete, you can respectfully store it in a drawer or offer it to a shrine for a ceremonial burning, among other methods.

**Q4. Among the 10 categories, what's the recommended "first stone" for beginners?**

A. **Clear Quartz (Crystal)** — it can be combined with any category and is easy to handle. The classic approach is to gradually add purpose-specific stones that you want next, pairing them with Clear Quartz.

**Q5. What's the difference between buying a crystal as jewelry and buying a rough or tumbled stone?**

A. **Jewelry** offers the appeal of being easily worn every day. **Rough and tumbled stones** are often felt by enthusiasts to have stronger energy due to being unprocessed. The most flexible approach is to have both and use them interchangeably.

## Editor's Team Perspective: Prioritizing When You're Undecided on a Category

Many people actually find all 10 categories appealing. The editor's team suggests choosing one category from "**the area causing you the most stress right now**."

-   Feeling drained from work → Career Luck (Tiger's Eye)
-   Persistent financial worries → Wealth Luck (Citrine)
-   Stuck in a loop with love → Love Luck (Rose Quartz)

Laying out "this and that" wishes often leads to **dispersed focus, making it harder for any specific category to be effective** — this is a common realization among long-time stone enthusiasts. We believe that focusing on one category and one stone for the first three months is the shortcut to success.

### Common Misconceptions

-   ❌ "**The stronger the wish, the stronger the stone needed**" → ✅ Stones touted as "the strongest" can have an abrupt energy that might be challenging for beginners. **Starting with gentler stones** is more sustainable.

-   ❌ "**Choosing the wrong category will have negative effects**" → ✅ Stones generally do not "interfere." Changing categories is fine; **feel free to experiment**.
