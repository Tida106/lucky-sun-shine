## Characteristics of Power Spots in Kanto

The power spot shrines in the Kanto region are characterized by their distinct personalities depending on the area: "Urban Sanctuary Forests," "Shonan's Coastal Sacred Sites," and "Chichibu's Mountain Spirituality."

-   **Urban Type**: Major sanctuary forests like Meiji Jingu, easily accessible near train stations for casual visits.
-   **Coastal Type**: Sacred sites in Shonan, such as Enoshima Shrine, where the sea and Benzaiten worship are intertwined.
-   **Mountain Type**: Solemn shrines situated on Kanto's most spiritual mountains, like Mitsumine Shrine.

With excellent access by Shinkansen and limited express trains, it's easy to plan a 1-night, 2-day trip from Tokyo to visit multiple famous shrines.

## <span id="meiji"></span>Meiji Jingu (Tokyo) — Kanto's Largest Urban Sanctuary Forest

Enshrining **Emperor Meiji and Empress Shoken**, Meiji Jingu is Tokyo's largest sanctuary forest. This vast 700,000 m² (approx. 173 acres) forest, though man-made, was designed to naturally transform into a mature forest over 100 years. Located just a 1-minute walk from Harajuku Station, right in the heart of the city, it's a rare sacred site where you can experience the profound tranquility of a deep forest.

-   **Location**: 1-1 Yoyogikamizonochō, Shibuya City, Tokyo
-   **Highlights**: Meoto Kusunoki (Married Couple Camphor Trees), Kiyomasa's Well, North Sando Forest
-   **Access**: 1-minute walk from Harajuku Station (JR Yamanote Line) or Meiji-jingumae Station (Tokyo Metro)
-   **Benefits**: Love and Relationship, Family Safety, National Peace
-   **Best Time to Visit**: Hatsumode (New Year's Visit - Japan's highest number of visitors), Fresh green season in May

## <span id="enoshima"></span>Enoshima Shrine (Kanagawa) — Shonan's Coastal Sacred Site

Enshrining the **Munakata Sanjojin (Three Munakata Goddesses)**, Enoshima Shrine is one of Japan's **Three Great Benzaiten Shrines**. The very location of **Enoshima Island, floating on the Shonan sea**, is a power spot in itself. The classic route involves visiting the **three shrines: Hetsunomiya, Nakatsunomiya, and Okutsunomiya**. There are also many other highlights, such as the **Eight-Armed Benzaiten at Hoanden** and the **Dragon Love Bell**.

-   **Location**: 2-3-8 Enoshima, Fujisawa City, Kanagawa
-   **Highlights**: Hetsunomiya (Hoanden), Nakatsunomiya, Okutsunomiya, Dragon Love Bell, Chigogafuchi
-   **Access**: 15-minute walk from Katase-Enoshima Station (Odakyu Line)
-   **Benefits**: Love and Relationship, Financial Luck, Improvement in Performing Arts, Maritime Safety
-   **Best Time to Visit**: Clear days from spring to early summer (for spectacular ocean views), Summer sunsets

→ [Enoshima Shrine Visiting Guide: Shonan's Coastal Sacred Site Enshrining Japan's Three Great Benzaiten and Munakata Sanjojin](/blog/enoshima-jinja/)

## <span id="mitsumine"></span>Mitsumine Shrine (Saitama) — Chichibu's Foremost Power Spot in Kanto

Enshrining **Izanagi-no-Mikoto and Izanami-no-Mikoto**, Mitsumine Shrine is a sacred mountain site located at an **elevation of 1,100 meters**. It is renowned for its **Okamisama (Wolf as Divine Messenger) worship**, unique **Mitsutorii gate**, and the **solemn pilgrimage path to the Okunomiya (Inner Shrine)**, making it one of Kanto's foremost power spots. The sight of **sea of clouds** spreading in the early morning is truly exceptional.

-   **Location**: 298-1 Mitsumine, Chichibu City, Saitama
-   **Highlights**: Mitsutorii gate, Zuijinmon gate, Okunomiya, Sea of Clouds, Okamisama's Gokenzoku Haishaku (receiving divine wolf protection)
-   **Access**: Approximately 75-minute bus ride from Seibu-Chichibu Station (Seibu Chichibu Line) (or about a 1.5-hour drive)
-   **Benefits**: Warding off Evil, Exorcism, Fulfillment of Wishes
-   **Best Time to Visit**: Early morning during the sea of clouds season (spring and autumn), Fresh green season

## Model Itineraries for Exploring Kanto

Here are some recommended plans if you want to visit all three shrines:

### Classic: 2 Nights, 3 Days Based in Tokyo

-   **Day 1**: Morning - Meiji Jingu → Afternoon - Explore Omotesando and Harajuku → Evening - Overnight stay at a hotel in Tokyo
-   **Day 2**: Morning - Take an early train to Fujisawa → Visit the three shrines of Enoshima Shrine → Lunch on Enoshima Island → Travel to Kamakura or return to Tokyo
-   **Day 3**: Take a bus from Seibu-Chichibu Station to Mitsumine Shrine → Visit Okunomiya → Return to Seibu-Chichibu → Head back to Tokyo

### Focus on Warding off Evil: Mitsumine Shrine Solo Trip

-   Early Morning - Seibu-Chichibu Station → Bus to Mitsumine Shrine → Half-day visit for the sea of clouds and Okunomiya → Return to Tokyo

## Key Visiting Tips

Here are some common tips for visiting the three Kanto shrines:

-   **Avoid the center of Meiji Jingu's approach path**: The middle of the path is for the deities.
-   **Enoshima Island is best visited at low tide**: Shops on the approach path may close early, so aim to arrive in the early afternoon.
-   **Pay attention to the weather at Mitsumine Shrine**: At an elevation of 1,100m, the temperature is 5-10°C lower than in the city, so warm clothing is essential.
-   **Limit shrine hopping to a maximum of two shrines per day**: This allows you to focus, including travel time.
-   Each shrine offers unique **Goshuin (shrine stamps)** — You can collect them all in one Goshuincho for your Kanto pilgrimage.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Which shrine in Kanto is known for "Japan's most visited Hatsumode (New Year's visit)"?**

A. **Meiji Jingu** is known for having **Japan's highest number of Hatsumode visitors** (approximately 3 million annually). If you want to avoid crowds, visiting **after January 3rd**, in the **early morning between 5 and 7 AM**, or during evening hours (within the shrine's opening times) is recommended.

**Q2. Can Enoshima Island be explored in one day?**

A. **Yes, it is possible.** You can visit the three shrines of Enoshima Shrine, Enoshima Iwaya Caves, and Chigogafuchi in about **3-4 hours**. If combining with Kamakura, **prioritize the three shrines of Enoshima Shrine**, and visit Chigogafuchi depending on the weather.

**Q3. Is it more convenient to visit Mitsumine Shrine by car or public transport?**

A. **A car offers more flexibility**, but **roads may be closed due to snow and ice during winter**. For **first-time visitors and during winter, public transportation** (bus from Seibu-Chichibu) is recommended.

**Q4. Are there other famous shrines in Kanto?**

A. Yes, there are many other renowned shrines in Kanto, such as **Kashima Jingu (Ibaraki), Katori Jingu (Chiba), Hikawa Shrine (Saitama), and Samukawa Shrine (Kanagawa)**. Sun-chan's site plans to introduce them sequentially.

**Q5. Is it okay to bring crystals to a shrine?**

A. Yes, absolutely! It's common to hold them in your palm before passing through the torii gate, or place them near your omamori or Ofuda (charms or talismans) after your visit. For more details, please refer to [The Basics of Crystals and Feng Shui](/blog/fengshui-powerstone-basics/).
