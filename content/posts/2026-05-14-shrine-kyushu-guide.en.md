## Features of Kyushu's Power Spots

Kyushu's power spot shrines and sacred sites are where several themes intertwine: **"The Stage of the Tenson Korin Myth," "Volcanoes and Rebirth," "Maritime Trade and Benzaiten Worship," and "World Natural Heritage Sites."** It's a region rich in grand-scale spiritual places.

-   **Tenson Korin Myth**: The mythical stage of Takachiho and Kirishima.
-   **Volcanoes and Rebirth**: The relationship between Mount Aso, Sakurajima, Kirishima Mountains, and the local shrines.
-   **Maritime Trade**: Munakata Taisha's World Heritage site, Okinoshima, the "Island of Gods."
-   **World Natural Heritage**: Yakushima's Jomon Sugi cedar and Yaku Shrine.

Access from Tokyo to Hakata is about **5 hours by Shinkansen (bullet train)** or **2 hours by plane**. The classic plan is to base yourself in **Fukuoka, Kumamoto, Miyazaki, or Kagoshima** and spend 2 to 3 nights exploring.

## <span id="munakata"></span>Munakata Taisha (Fukuoka Prefecture) — World Heritage "Island of Gods," Okinoshima

Munakata Taisha enshrines the **Munakata Three Goddesses** (Tagorihime-no-Kami, Takitsuhime-no-Kami, and Ichikishimahime-no-Kami), serving as the general guardian of the sea. **Okinoshima, the "Island of Gods,"** is registered as a World Heritage site, and the shrine has been deeply revered on a national scale since ancient times as a **guardian deity for maritime trade routes** to the continent. It operates under a three-shrine system: **Hetsugu, Nakatsugu, and Okitsugu**.

-   **Location**: 2331 Tajima, Munakata, Fukuoka (Hetsugu)
-   **Highlights**: Hetsugu (open for worship), National Treasures in Shinpokan Museum, Okinoshima (general public not allowed to land)
-   **Access**: About a 12-minute bus ride from JR Kagoshima Main Line "Togo" Station
-   **Blessings**: Maritime safety, financial luck, business prosperity, international exchange
-   **Recommended Season**: Year-round; allow half a day if including the Shinpokan Museum visit.

## <span id="aso"></span>Aso Shrine (Kumamoto Prefecture) — Symbol of Revival, One of Japan's Three Great Romon Gates

Aso Shrine is Higo Province's Ichinomiya, enshrining **Takeiwatatsu-no-Mikoto** and the **Aso Twelve Gods**. The romon gate and haiden (worship hall) collapsed in the **2016 Kumamoto Earthquake**, but the **romon gate was completely restored in December 2023**, resurrecting **one of Japan's Three Great Romon Gates**. It is the land of the pioneering myth where a deity "kicked open the caldera."

-   **Location**: 3083-1 Miyaji, Ichinomiya-machi, Aso, Kumamoto
-   **Highlights**: Romon Gate (restored), Shaden (main hall), Takasago-no-Matsu pine, Aso Caldera
-   **Access**: About a 15-minute bus ride from JR Hohi Main Line "Aso" Station (or by car)
-   **Blessings**: Love and relationships, marital harmony, bountiful harvests,厄除 (warding off evil)
-   **Recommended Season**: After spring's controlled burns, summer's fresh greenery, autumn's highlands

## <span id="kirishima"></span>Kirishima Jingu (Kagoshima Prefecture) — Vermilion Shrine Enshrining the Protagonist of Tenson Korin

Kirishima Jingu is an ancient shrine enshrining **Ninigi-no-Mikoto**, the **protagonist of the Tenson Korin myth**. Its **vermilion-lacquered shrine complex is a National Treasure**, and it's also known as a destination for **Sakamoto Ryoma and Oryo's honeymoon**. Located at the **foot of Mount Takachiho**, it is a sacred site imbued with the spiritual energy of the Kirishima mountain range.

-   **Location**: 2608-5 Taguchi, Kirishima, Kirishima, Kagoshima
-   **Highlights**: Vermilion-lacquered National Treasure main shrine, sacred tree, views of the Kirishima mountains
-   **Access**: About a 13-minute bus ride from JR Nippo Main Line "Kirishima Jingu" Station
-   **Blessings**: Tenson Korin, national peace and stability, bountiful harvests, love and relationships
-   **Recommended Season**: Early summer for Miyama Kirishima azaleas, autumn for foliage

## <span id="takachiho"></span>Takachiho Gorge (Miyazaki Prefecture) — The Divine Scenery of Ama-no-Iwato Myth and Columnar Joints

This Miyazaki gorge is considered **the stage of the Tenson Korin myth**. It is famous for its breathtaking scenery, where **columnar jointed** rock walls lead to the **Manai Falls**, and a **rental boat** ride right under the waterfall is a classic experience. **Takachiho Shrine** hosts **Yokagura (nighttime sacred dance)** performances every evening, and the myths of Ama-no-Iwato (the rock cave of heaven) still resonate at **Ama-no-Iwato Shrine and Amanoyasugawara**.

-   **Location**: Mitai, Takachiho-cho, Nishiusuki-gun, Miyazaki
-   **Highlights**: Manai Falls, columnar joints, Yokagura at Takachiho Shrine, Ama-no-Iwato Shrine, Amanoyasugawara
-   **Access**: About a 1 hour and 30 minute bus ride from JR Nippo Main Line "Nobeoka" Station (or by car)
-   **Blessings**: Fulfillment of wishes, mythical experience, purification
-   **Recommended Season**: Spring and autumn (summer is crowded, winter is severely cold)

## <span id="yakushima"></span>Yakushima (Kagoshima Prefecture) — World Natural Heritage Site, the Island of Jomon Sugi

Yakushima is a **World Natural Heritage** island boasting the **Jomon Sugi** (cedar tree estimated to be 2,000 to 7,000 years old). **Shiratanisunsui Gorge** is said to be the **model for "Princess Mononoke,"** and **Yaku Shrine** is the largest shrine on the island, enshrining **Yamahiko**. The **entire island is a power spot**, making it a truly unique place.

-   **Location**: Kumage District, Yakushima, Kagoshima
-   **Highlights**: Jomon Sugi (10-hour round-trip trek), Shiratanisunsui Gorge, Yakusugi Land, Yaku Shrine, Mount Miyanoura
-   **Access**: Yakushima Airport (approx. 40 mins from Kagoshima Airport) / High-speed ferry "Toppy and Rocket," ferry (from Kagoshima Port)
-   **Blessings**: Nature worship, spiritual growth, purification
-   **Recommended Season**: Spring to early summer (relatively less rain), autumn for foliage

## Model Itineraries for Exploring Kyushu

Visiting all five locations requires a **minimum of 3 to 5 days**. Here are some themed combinations:

### Classic: Tenson Korin Route (3 Days, 2 Nights)

-   **Day 1**: Miyazaki Airport → Takachiho → Takachiho Shrine's Yokagura → Overnight in Takachiho
-   **Day 2**: Takachiho → Kumamoto → Aso Shrine → Overnight in Aso
-   **Day 3**: Aso → Kagoshima → Kirishima Jingu → Return from Kagoshima Airport

### Fukuoka and Munakata (2 Days, 1 Night)

-   **Day 1**: Fukuoka → Munakata Taisha → Shinpokan Museum visit → Overnight in Fukuoka city
-   **Day 2**: Fukuoka city sightseeing or visit Dazaifu, etc. → Return Home

### Yakushima (4 Days, 3 Nights)

-   **Day 1**: Kagoshima Airport → Yakushima Airport → Overnight in Miyanoura area
-   **Day 2**: Jomon Sugi trekking (10-hour round trip) → Overnight
-   **Day 3**: Shiratanisunsui Gorge and Yaku Shrine → Overnight in Yakushima
-   **Day 4**: Yakushima → Return Home

## Key Visiting Tips

Common tips for visiting Kyushu's five locations:

-   **Okinoshima is off-limits to the public**: Experience its essence at the Shinpokan Museum instead.
-   **Aso Shrine is a symbol of recovery**: Visit respectfully and offer prayers for its complete restoration.
-   **Kirishima Jingu's vermilion National Treasure shrine** is a highlight: Take photos only in permitted areas.
-   **Rental boats at Takachiho Gorge are highly recommended to book in advance**: Waiting times can exceed an hour on the day.
-   **Yakushima's weather is unpredictable**: Be fully prepared with rain gear and equipment, and don't hesitate to turn back if necessary.
-   **Renting a car for travel within Kyushu is efficient**, especially recommended for Aso, Kirishima, and Takachiho.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Can I visit all the famous shrines in Kyushu in one trip?**

A. **Physically, it's challenging.** The travel distances between Fukuoka, Kumamoto, Miyazaki, and Kagoshima are long. **A minimum of 5 days to a week** is more realistic. It's recommended to **focus on 2 to 3 locations based on a theme**.

**Q2. Can I go to Okinoshima?**

A. **Women are forbidden** from landing, and **men are also prohibited from landing except for specific rituals**. This has been strictly enforced since its World Heritage registration. Visiting the **Shinpokan Museum at Hetsugu** is the alternative to learn about it.

**Q3. Is the Jomon Sugi trek in Yakushima suitable for beginners?**

A. It is a full-fledged **10-hour round-trip mountain hike**. **Prior training, appropriate gear, and a reliable guide** are recommended. Beginners should start with shorter courses like **Yakusugi Land** or **Shiratanisunsui Gorge** for safety.

**Q4. When can I see the Takachiho Yokagura (night sacred dance)?**

A. It is performed **every evening at Takachiho Shrine from 8:00 PM to 9:00 PM** (please check in advance). Experiencing Kagura in the setting of myth is unique. From **November to February**, more elaborate **"all-night Kagura"** performances are also offered in various local communities.

**Q5. Is it okay to bring a crystal when visiting a shrine?**

A. Yes. It's common practice to hold it in your palm before passing through the torii gate, or to place it near an omamori (charm) or Ofuda (talisman) after your visit. For more details, please refer to [Basics of Crystals and Feng Shui](/blog/fengshui-powerstone-basics/).
