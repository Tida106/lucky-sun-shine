Mineralogically, Lemurian Quartz **has the same composition as regular quartz**. Its chemical composition, hardness, and physical properties are identical to clear quartz. What definitively sets it apart are the **"barcode-like horizontal striations" etched on its crystal sides (prism faces)**. These striations have a **"rough, tactile feel"** when touched, which is the maximum distinguishing characteristic of Lemurian Quartz.

In 1999, Brazilian miners discovered **"crystals with unique stripe patterns unlike other quartz"** in the Serra do Cabral mine. People in the spiritual community then imbued these crystals with the symbolism of **"crystals carrying the wisdom of the ancient continent of Lemuria,"** and they rapidly spread worldwide. It holds a unique position as **"the newest and most mythical presence in the quartz crystal industry."**

With a Mohs hardness of 7, it is durable enough for daily use and **impact-resistant due to its lack of cleavage**. It often circulates as points (crystals), and many enthusiasts use it as a **"handmade meditation tool,"** holding it in their hands.

## <span id="care"></span>Purification and Care

Lemurian Quartz has a **Mohs hardness of 7** and is robust; it is a versatile stone that **responds well to most purification methods**, just like clear quartz.

-   **Running Water**: Briefly rinsing with lukewarm water is fine for a short time.
-   **Placing on a Quartz Cluster or Tumbled Stones**: A classic overnight purification method.
-   **Passing through White Sage Smoke**: 1 to 2 minutes is sufficient. **A traditional purification fitting for spiritual rituals.**
-   **Moonlight and Sunlight Bathing**: Place by a window 3 days before and after a full or new moon. It is also resistant to sunlight.
-   **Sound Bath with Tuning Fork or Crystal Tuner**: Effective if it does not touch metal.

Purification methods to avoid:

-   **Burying in coarse salt for extended periods** (can cause discoloration of metal fittings or plating).
-   **Prolonged operation in an ultrasonic cleaner** (may wear down the crystal face striations).
-   **Strong polishing** (if the barcode-like striations on the crystal face disappear, the "records of wisdom" of Lemurian Quartz are lost).

For daily care, **gently wipe with a soft cloth**. To **preserve the crystal face striations**, absolutely do not use abrasive cleaners or strong detergents. The **striations are the source of Lemurian Quartz's value**, so avoiding over-polishing is key to a long relationship with the stone.

## <span id="fake"></span>Distinguishing Natural and Similar Stones

-   **Natural Lemurian Quartz** has **barcode-like horizontal striations** carved on its **crystal sides (prism faces)**. When traced with a finger, these striations **feel distinctly rough**.
-   **Difference from regular Clear Quartz**: They look very similar, but are distinguished by the **presence or absence of striations**. Clear quartz crystal faces are **relatively smooth**, whereas Lemurian Quartz has **distinct barcode-like striations**.
-   **Colombian Lemurian**: In addition to the Brazilian origin (the original), **Colombian-sourced** crystals are also sometimes called "Lemurian." While the striations may differ slightly, they are essentially in the same category.
-   **Fake Lemurian**: Counterfeits with **artificially carved striations** on regular quartz occasionally circulate. They can be distinguished by the **naturalness and tactile feel of the striations**.
-   **Pink and Smoky Lemurian variants**: Color variations such as Pink Lemurian and Smoky Lemurian also exist.
-   The most reliable way to ensure authenticity is to choose **crystals with a gemological certificate or from a trusted spiritual specialty store**.

The **essence of choosing Lemurian Quartz** lies in the **clarity of the striations on its crystal faces**. Crystals with **clear, horizontal, barcode-like patterns** are considered the most authentic "Lemurian."
