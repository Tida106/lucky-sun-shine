## <span id="why-pink"></span>Why Love Stones Tend to Be in the Pink Spectrum

Most of the stones spoken of as talismans for love luck are overwhelmingly in the **pink, red, white, and pale purple** spectrum. This isn't just a coincidence; it's deeply connected to the history of **cultures worldwide expressing "love" with the color pink.**

- In ancient Greek mythology, **Rose Quartz** was offered to **Aphrodite (Venus), the Goddess of Love.**
- In the Inca Empire, **Rhodochrosite (Inca Rose)** was revered as a legendary stone that brings a **"rose-colored life."**
- In medieval Europe, there was a custom for lovers to exchange Rose Quartz as an **"amulet of love."**
- Even in Japan, **Pearl** has been cherished as a **versatile jewelry for formal occasions** and a symbol of "pure love."

From a color psychology perspective, **pink is said to regulate the parasympathetic nervous system, promoting a sense of calm and gentleness.** As a color that supports the preliminary stages of love like **"self-love," "compassion," and "serenity,"** choosing pink stones is not only symbolic but also visually logical.

## <span id="care"></span>Care and Purification Methods

Many love luck stones have a **Mohs hardness of 6-8**, which is hard enough for daily use, but they do have **individual weaknesses**:

| Stone | Main Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Rose Quartz | Risk of **fading with direct sunlight (prolonged exposure)** |
| Inca Rose | **Surface deterioration from coarse salt and acidic substances** / Prone to chipping from impact |
| Morganite | Be careful of impact and sudden temperature changes |
| Rhodonite | Potential for chipping with strong impact |
| Kunzite | **Fades in strong light** / Avoid prolonged wear in direct sunlight |
| Amethyst | Purple color fades in direct sunlight |
| Moonstone | **Relatively soft (Mohs 6)** and vulnerable to impact |
| Aquamarine | Vulnerable to sudden temperature changes |
| Pearl | **Water, salt, and cosmetics are all detrimental** / Soft and delicate |

Common **safe purification methods** include **"placing on a crystal cluster or crushed crystal pieces,"** "**passing through white sage smoke**," and "**moonlight bathing**." Specifically, **Pearl, Rose Quartz, and Kunzite dislike strong sunlight and water**, so avoid running water and prolonged sun exposure.

## <span id="faq"></span>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Which is the "strongest" stone for boosting love luck?**

A. **There is no single "strongest" answer.** Culturally, Rose Quartz is often called the "absolute classic of love stones," but **the stone you can cherish is the strongest for you personally**  Ethis is the essence of choosing power stones. Please consider symbolism, budget, and personal preference comprehensively when making your choice.

**Q2. If I wear a love luck stone, will I definitely find a partner or get married?**

A. **We cannot guarantee that.** The effects of power stones are **cultural and symbolic**, and do not guarantee specific romantic success, reconciliation, marriage, or new encounters. **"By carrying a charm, you may feel more positive and take more action, which could indirectly change your situation"**  Eexpecting such an indirect effect is a realistic way to approach them.

**Q3. Which stone is for reconciliation?**

A. Traditionally, **Inca Rose (Rhodochrosite)** is spoken of as a symbol for **"restoring a specific relationship,"** but **it does not guarantee reconciliation.** The original purpose is to accompany you as a charm that supports **"objectively re-evaluating past relationships" and "cultivating forgiveness within yourself." Rhodonite (the stone of forgiveness)**, when combined, can also be a catalyst for **"forgiving yourself"** even before reconciliation.

**Q4. Is it strange for men to wear love luck stones?**

A. **Not strange at all.** While Rose Quartz is often spoken of as a feminine stone, its meanings of **self-love and harmonizing interpersonal relationships** can be applied regardless of gender. There are **many discreet ways to use them**, such as carrying a tumbled stone in your pocket or placing one in a tray on your desk. **Garnet, Amethyst, and Kunzite** have also been cherished as men's jewelry since ancient times.

**Q5. Is it okay to give a love luck stone to my partner as a gift?**

A. **It's absolutely fine.** In fact, there's a worldwide tradition of **"couples wearing matching stones,"** and **matching Rose Quartz tumbled stones or bracelets** are classic gifts. However, it's best to give them with the intention of **"wishing each other happiness as a charm,"** rather than **"forcibly influencing your partner's love luck."** This is a healthy way to approach it.

**Q6. What if I feel "I have a love luck stone, but I can't find a partner"?**

A. **Stones are "symbols of action," not "action itself."** The original process is that carrying a love luck stone **awakens an awareness of "wanting to change my situation,"** which then leads to specific actions (going out, new hobbies, self-improvement, honest communication). Remember the premise that the charm is a catalyst, and action is up to you. **"Without rushing, at your own pace"** is the healthiest distance to keep with love luck stones.
