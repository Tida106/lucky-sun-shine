## <span id="why-gold"></span>Why Money Luck Stones Tend to Be Yellow and Gold

When it comes to stones believed to bring financial luck, shades of **yellow, gold, and golden brown** make up the vast majority. This isn't a coincidence; it's deeply tied to a long history where, across cultures worldwide, the "**radiance of the sun = wealth and abundance**" has been synonymous with prosperity.

- In ancient Egypt, the **sun god Ra** was depicted in gold.
- In medieval Europe, **merchants** were said to carry **Citrine (yellow quartz)** close to them at all times.
- In the Himalayas, **Rutilated Quartz**, which contains "**golden needles**," was revered.
- Even in Japan, gold leaf, gold coins (koban), and the mallet of luck (uchide no kozuchi) became lucky charms, symbolizing "**gold = business prosperity**."

From a color psychology perspective, yellow is believed to stimulate the adrenal glands and encourage positive emotions. As a color that supports the precursors to financial luck—"**confidence," "proactiveness," and "brightness**"—it's a visually logical choice, not just a symbolic one, for a talisman.

## <span id="citrine"></span>① Citrine: The Classic for Protection and Business Prosperity

**Citrine** (Yellow Quartz) is the undisputed king of money luck stones, known as the "**Stone of Business Prosperity**" and the "**Stone of the Sun**." Its bright yellow hue is said to resonate with the Third Chakra (solar plexus), boosting **confidence and a positive outlook**.

- **Symbol of attracting an income flow**: Loved for millennia as a talisman for merchants and business owners.
- **Support for confidence and self-esteem**: Useful in price negotiations and personal branding situations.
- **Brightness and positivity**: A great stone for resetting your mood on down days.

Also recognized as a **November birthstone**, it's perfect as a first serious money luck stone for those who want to start at an **affordable price**.

## <span id="rutilated"></span>② Rutilated Quartz: Nicknamed the "Strongest Wealth Stone"

This mystical crystal features **golden needles (rutile) running through clear quartz**. It's known as the "**strongest class of wealth stone**" and has traditionally been said to attract **sudden, windfall-type luck**.

- Its rare appearance, hosting "**golden needles**."
- A traditional talisman for **stock investments and short-term ventures**.
- Believed to support **intuition for seizing opportunities**.

**Finely-needled varieties from the Himalayas** fetch higher prices, and it's also notable for its wide price range, from **jewelry starting at 1 carat for ¥10,000 to more affordable tumbled stones**.

## <span id="care"></span>Care and Purification Methods

Most money luck stones have a **Mohs hardness of 6-7**, which is sufficiently hard for daily use, but they do have **individual weaknesses**:

| Stone | Primary Weakness |
|---|---|
| Citrine | Risk of fading from direct sunlight (prolonged exposure) |
| Rutilated Quartz | Be careful of cracks at the boundaries of internal needles |
| Tiger's Eye | Strong detergents and coarse salt can damage surface stripes |
| Pyrite | **Rusts in humidity** (avoid high-humidity storage) |
| Jade | Potential for cleavage breaks with strong impact |
| Sunstone | Sensitive to sudden temperature changes |
| Amber | **Soft and easily scratched** (Mohs 2-2.5) |
| Aventurine | Green color may fade in direct sunlight |
| Topaz | **Perfect cleavage** — avoid strong impact |

Common **safe purification methods** include "**placing on a crystal cluster or crushed crystals**," "**passing through white sage smoke**," and "**moonbathing**." Please note that **Amber and Pyrite dislike water**, so avoid water purification methods for them.

## <span id="faq"></span>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Which stone is the "strongest" for financial luck?**

A. **There is no single "strongest" answer**. Culturally, Rutilated Quartz is often called the "strongest class of wealth stone," but **the stone you value the most is the strongest for you** — this is the essence of choosing crystals. Please consider symbolism, budget, and personal preference when making your choice.

**Q2. If I wear a money luck stone, will my income definitely increase?**

A. **We cannot say that it will increase**. The effects of crystals are **cultural and symbolic**, and do not guarantee income, profit, or financial outcomes. A realistic approach is to expect an indirect effect: "**Having a talisman may encourage more positive actions, which could then change your situation.**"

**Q3. Is it okay to wear multiple money luck stones together?**

A. **Yes, it's perfectly fine**. However, it's often said that increasing the number of combinations too much can disperse their energy, so **keeping it simple with 1-2 stones** is a style that lasts. Starting with classic combinations like **Citrine and Rutilated Quartz** or **Citrine and Tiger's Eye** is recommended.

**Q4. Which is more effective: keeping a money luck stone in my wallet or wearing it?**

A. **Either is fine**. If you choose to **keep it in your wallet, use a small tumbled stone** (placed in a nylon bag or special pouch to avoid friction with coins). If you **wear it, a bracelet or pendant** is suitable. Choose the method that best fits your lifestyle and that **you can consistently maintain**, as that is often the most effective.

**Q5. My Pyrite has rusted. What should I do?**

A. Pyrite (Iron Pyrite) has a tendency to **rust in humidity**. You can prevent this by **storing it with a desiccant**, but if it has already rusted, the practical approach is to **gently wipe it with a soft cloth and consider replacing it** depending on its condition. There is also a traditional ritual of gratitude, seeing it as having "taken a hit on your behalf."

**Q6. What if I feel like my "money luck stone isn't increasing my income"?**

A. **A stone is a "symbol of action," not "action itself."** The true process is that owning a money luck stone **awakens your awareness to "wanting to change your situation,"** which then leads to concrete actions (studying, starting a side hustle, job hunting, saving). Remember the premise that the talisman is a catalyst, but the action is up to you.
