## <span id="why-black"></span>Why Protection and Evil-Warding Stones Tend to Be Black

Power stones traditionally associated with protection and warding off evil show a strong tendency to be either **deep black (like Morion, Onyx, Black Tourmaline, and Obsidian)** or to have a **metallic black sheen (like Hematite)**. This isn't a coincidence; it stems from an ancient symbolic idea found in cultures worldwide that **"black absorbs light, and therefore blocks negative energies."**

-   **Black is a color that absorbs light** — It has long been seen symbolically as "absorbing external influences and blocking them from within."
-   It is said that **ancient Roman warriors wore black stones as a shield**.
-   In Tibet and Mongolia, **Morion (black quartz) was revered by high priests as a stone for warding off evil**.
-   Even in Japan, **"black robes" and "black dyes" remain traditional colors for protection against evil**.

From a color psychology perspective, **black is said to visually convey a sense of depth and stability**. As a color that emphasizes a mental boundary—**"I am protected beyond this point"**—it is a visually logical choice, not just for talismans but also for their appearance. On the other hand, **white stones (Clear Quartz, Selenite)** symbolize **"purification and new beginnings,"** **purple stones (Amethyst)** represent **"spirituality that wards off negative relationships,"** and **blue stones (Lapis Lazuli)** signify **"calm protection."** These stones are also cherished for protection and warding off evil, but in ways distinct from the black variety.

## <span id="black-tourmaline"></span>③ Black Tourmaline | The "Electric Stone," a Modern Protection Staple

Black Tourmaline (Schorl), also known as the **"electric stone,"** is the most iron-rich variety within the Tourmaline group. It has become a popular healing stone, widely used in modern times as a **"charm for PC environments" and "a countermeasure against electromagnetic waves,"** establishing itself as a contemporary classic.

-   **Pyroelectricity and Piezoelectricity** — Physical properties that scientifically earn it the name "electric stone."
-   **Rapidly Gained Popularity** as a charm for modern desk workers.
-   **Affordable Price Range** — Excellent as an introductory stone for protection.

## <span id="care"></span>Care and Purification Methods

Many protection and evil-warding stones range widely in **Mohs hardness from 2 to 7**, and due to the tradition of them **"absorbing negative energies," frequent purification is recommended**:

| Stone              | Primary Vulnerability                                  |
| :----------------- | :----------------------------------------------------- |
| Morion             | Risk of fading with prolonged direct sunlight           |
| Onyx               | Prone to chipping from strong impacts                  |
| Black Tourmaline   | Sensitive to rapid temperature changes                 |
| Clear Quartz       | Virtually universal and robust                         |
| Obsidian           | **Natural glass, so it is brittle and can break easily** |
| Hematite           | **Rusts with moisture** (avoid humid storage)          |
| Amethyst           | Purple color fades with direct sunlight                |
| Lapis Lazuli       | **Vulnerable to water and salt** (risk of discoloration) |
| Selenite           | **Fatally vulnerable to water** (Mohs 2.5, dissolves in water) |

**Common safe purification methods** include **placing them on a clear quartz cluster or tumbled stones**, **passing them through white sage smoke**, or **moon bathing**. Specifically, **Selenite, Lapis Lazuli, and Hematite dislike water**, so avoid purification by running water. Many users of protection stones, following the tradition that **they "absorb negative energies," practice purification once to several times a month**.

## <span id="faq"></span>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. If I wear a protection stone, am I guaranteed to avoid misfortunes?**

A. **No, it cannot be guaranteed.** The effects of power stones are **cultural and symbolic in nature**, and they do not guarantee the avoidance of specific misfortunes, the repulsion of evil spirits, or the prevention of problems. A realistic approach is to expect an indirect effect, such as **"my mind might feel calmer by having an amulet, and as a result, I might act more thoughtfully."**

**Q2. Are there any stones that can eliminate "curses" or "spiritual afflictions"?**

A. **Spiritual phenomena such as "curses," "spiritual afflictions," or "evil spirits" are not scientifically or medically proven.** The traditions and symbolism described in this article are limited to **"emotional support and peace of mind as an amulet,"** and do not guarantee any further effects. If you are experiencing **strong anxiety or fear such as "I feel cursed" or "I am suffering from spiritual afflictions," it is crucial to prioritize consulting with a specialist, such as a psychiatrist or counselor.** Please welcome amulets purely as a supplementary presence.

**Q3. Which stone should I wear during an unlucky year?**

A. Traditionally, **Morion, Clear Quartz, and Amethyst** are often chosen as **"talismans for unlucky years,"** but **they do not guarantee the avoidance of misfortunes.** While having an amulet can be a wonderful habit as **"a reminder to stay vigilant during a challenging year,"** it is crucial to remember that **practical actions like getting health check-ups, being cautious while driving, and carefully reviewing contracts are the essential preparations for such a year.**

**Q4. What protection stone is good for a new home or when moving?**

A. Traditionally, **Morion, Black Tourmaline, and Clear Quartz clusters** are chosen as **"charms for new spaces."** It is common to place them in the **entrance, bedroom, or living room**, but remember that this is **"a way to consciously begin a new life on a positive note," and it is not a substitute for actual crime prevention or disaster preparedness.** Practical measures like **locks, alarm systems, and smoke detectors** should always be the top priority.

**Q5. Misfortunes keep happening even though I have a protection stone. Is the stone not working?**

A. **Stones are symbols of action, not action themselves.** **Persistent troubles and misfortunes often have real-world causes. Rather than attributing it to the stone, it's more important to clarify the situation and take necessary actions (such as health check-ups, consulting with specialists, or improving your lifestyle).** Remember the premise: **"Amulets are a trigger and support, but action is up to you."** If you are experiencing serious anxiety or suffering, it is important to consult a medical institution, counseling service, or specialized support agency.

**Q6. Aren't black stones ominous?**

A. **No, they are not ominous.** **Black stones have been revered across cultures worldwide since ancient times as "the color of protection" and "the color that absorbs negative energies."** Even in Japan, **"black robes" and "black dyes" remain traditional colors for warding off evil**, and **black is chosen for mourning attire** as a "color of formal respect." **The idea that "black equals ominous" is a modern, biased impression;** traditionally, it is understood as **"the most powerful color for protection."**
