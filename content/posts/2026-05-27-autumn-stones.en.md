## <span id="autumn-care"></span>Autumn Power Stone Care and Dryness Prevention

Autumn is a season where the **"air rapidly dries out."** While **most stones themselves** are resilient to dryness, **attached parts** like **bracelet cords, elastic, and leather** require attention.

### ✂️ Dryness Prevention for Cord, Elastic, and Leather Parts

| Part | Autumnal Consideration |
|---|---|
| **Silicone Elastic (Operon Elastic)** | Prone to cracking due to age and dryness |
| **Leather Cord** | Dries and stiffens, prone to breaking |
| **Silk or Cotton Thread** | Prone to fraying due to dryness |
| **Metal Parts** | Prone to oxidation from condensation and sweat salinity |

**Once a year** – especially from **autumn to the onset of winter** – it is recommended to **check the condition of your bracelet cords** and have them **repaired or restrung** if necessary.

### 💧 Stones Vulnerable to Dryness

Even some stones themselves can **crack or fade** due to **dryness.**

| Vulnerable Stone | Reason |
|---|---|
| **Opal** | Hydrous mineral, prone to cracking when dry |
| **Amber** | Resin-derived, color change due to dryness |
| **Malachite** | Surface luster can change |
| **Pearl** | Luster dulls from dryness and friction |

**When storing,** avoiding **excessive desiccants** and **direct sunlight by a window** – simply being mindful of these two points can significantly extend the life of your stones.

## <span id="faq"></span>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. What is the absolute must-have stone for autumn?**

A. **There is no "absolute single stone."** While several stones like **Citrine, Amber, Smoky Quartz, and Sapphire** resonate with **autumnal symbolism,** the right choice depends on **"your situation, preferences, and intuition."** The essence of choosing a power stone is that **the stone you can cherish is the best one for you.**

**Q2. Can power stones heal autumn fatigue (summer's exhaustion)?**

A. **I cannot assert that they "can heal." Autumn fatigue and seasonal discomfort** are **medical matters,** and **hydration, sleep, nutrition, and necessary medical advice from a doctor** are paramount. The stones in this article are meant to be **"charms for your feelings,"** to be enjoyed with a **psychological distance,** such that **"gazing at them brings peace of mind" or "wearing them helps settle your mood."**

**Q3. What is a recommended bracelet combination for "autumn for reading"?**

A. **"Lapis Lazuli and Fluorite"** is a classic combination for wisdom and concentration. **"Sapphire and Amethyst"** combines sincere learning with quiet contemplation. If your **eyes get tired from long hours of reading,** placing **green stones like Jade or Aventurine on your desk to gaze at** is also a lovely idea.

**Q4. What should I be aware of when taking stones with me for autumn leaf viewing?**

A. Pay attention to **weather changes.** In the mountains, **rain, humidity, and temperature differences can be significant,** which can cause **bracelet cords to deteriorate from moisture** or **metal parts to oxidize from condensation.** Carrying them in a **charm pouch** is the safest and most charming way to enjoy them.

**Q5. Does a moonlight bath on the Mid-Autumn Moon night enhance its effects?**

A. **There is no guarantee that "effects will be enhanced."** However, the traditional practice of **"exposing stones to moonlight on a special night"** has been cherished worldwide as a **"ritual for marking a milestone and setting one's mind."** The **time spent gazing at stones with heartfelt intention** – that ritualistic aspect is the true joy of a moonlight bath.

**Q6. Will owning an autumn birthstone make me stronger in autumn?**

A. **There is no guarantee that "you will become stronger."** Birthstones are charms with the symbolic meaning of **"connecting to one's roots"** and have **no direct effect on changing temperature, physical condition, or seasonal influences.** Enjoying them for their **psychological effect,** such as **"feeling your mood settle by wearing autumnal colors,"** is recommended.

**Q7. What is the best time to welcome a new stone in autumn?**

A. **There is no "absolute best timing."** Choose a day that **you feel is a "milestone,"** such as the **Autumnal Equinox (around September 23rd), Jugoya (Mid-Autumn Moon, traditionally August 15th of the lunar calendar), Niiname-sai (November 23rd), or your own birthday.** **"How you welcome it with what feelings"** is more important than "when you welcome it."

**Q8. Are opals and ambers prone to deterioration if worn in autumn?**

A. **It's not that they "deteriorate just because it's autumn,"** but **dryness** requires attention. Avoiding **excessive desiccants,** **direct sunlight by a window,** and **prolonged exposure to direct heating** – being mindful of these three points will help maintain their beauty for a long time.

**Q9. Should I believe the "divinatory meanings" of power stones?**

A. **It is not something you are forced to "believe or not believe."** This site consistently values the approach of **"enjoying cultural symbolism."** We do not adopt **definitive divinatory conclusions** such as **"people born in ○ month have a ○ personality" or "those who possess ○ stone will always be lucky."** Enjoying them as a **casual reference** is the secret to a healthy and long-lasting relationship with power stones.
