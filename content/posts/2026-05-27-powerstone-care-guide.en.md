## <span id="three-stages"></span>Three Stages of Care: Daily Cleaning, Cleansing, and Long-Term Storage

Crystal care combines three distinct actions. Being aware that **each has a different purpose and frequency** will help reduce confusion:

| Stage | Purpose | Frequency Guideline |
|---|---|---|
| **① Daily Cleaning** | Removes physical dirt like sweat, sebum, and cosmetics | Every time you wear it, at the end of the day |
| **② Cleansing** | Cultural and symbolic "resetting of feelings" | Once to several times a month (depends on the stone) |
| **③ Long-Term Storage** | Prevents physical deterioration and contact with other stones | In a dedicated case when not being worn |

You don't need to do **"all three"** stages. The reality of crystals is that **"daily cleaning alone is often enough to keep them long-lasting,"** and cleansing can be approached with the attitude of **"those who want to perform it as a ritual do so."** There's no problem with that.

## <span id="daily"></span>① Daily Cleaning | Essential Care After Wearing

The most important and practical aspect is **"daily cleaning."** By also caring for the **metal settings (metal parts) and not just the stone itself**, your entire piece of jewelry will last longer.

### 3 Basic Steps

1.  **Gently wipe the surface with a soft cloth**: To remove sweat, sebum, and cosmetic residue.
2.  **Rinse quickly with lukewarm water if necessary** (only for water-friendly stones / refer to the suitability chart).
3.  **Store only after completely drying**: Remaining moisture can lead to deterioration and mold.

### What Not to Do

-   ❌ **Strong or acidic detergents**: These will deteriorate both the stone and the metal fittings.
-   ❌ **Ultrasonic cleaners**: For stones with many inclusions or delicate stones, this can expand existing cracks.
-   ❌ **Sudden changes in hot or cold water**: Stones sensitive to temperature changes can crack.
-   ❌ **Storing while wet**: Moisture can penetrate the crystalline structure of some stones.

Simply by forming the habit of **"wiping after wearing,"** the **beauty of your crystal 5 to 10 years later will be significantly different** — this is the most crucial point in crystal care.

## <span id="purification"></span>② Cleansing | Method-Specific Steps

Cleansing is an act traditionally performed as a **"ritual for resetting one's feelings."** While **it is not scientifically proven to have "effects,"** the very act of **"consciously caring for your stone"** is believed to deepen your connection with it as an amulet.

### <span id="m-cluster"></span>Method 1: Quartz Cluster and Gemstone Chips (Most Recommended, All-Purpose)

**Steps**:
1.  Prepare a quartz cluster (raw stone) or gemstone chips on a clean dish.
2.  Place your crystal on top.
3.  Leave it there for **6 to 12 hours (overnight)**.
4.  The next morning, pick it up with gratitude.

**Characteristics**: **Safe for almost all stones** and can be used for those sensitive to water, salt, or sunlight. This is the most recommended and classic method.

### <span id="m-sage"></span>Method 2: White Sage Smoke

**Steps**:
1.  Light a white sage leaf on a heat-resistant dish.
2.  Immediately blow out the flame, allowing only smoke to emanate.
3.  **Gently rotate the stone in the smoke for 1 to 2 minutes**.
4.  Ensure sufficient ventilation and completely extinguish the fire.

**Characteristics**: **Suitable for almost all stones**. Since no water is used, it is safe for water-sensitive stones.

**Safety Considerations**: As fire is involved, **always perform on a heat-resistant dish**. **Ensure adequate ventilation**, and confirm the flame is completely extinguished before leaving. **Do not perform near curtains or paper products**.

### <span id="m-moonlight"></span>Method 3: Moonlight Bath

**Steps**:
1.  **The three days before and after a full moon** are traditionally recommended.
2.  Place the crystal in a location where **direct moonlight falls**, such as a windowsill or balcony.
3.  Leave it there **overnight (6 to 12 hours)**.
4.  The next morning, pick it up with gratitude.

**Characteristics**: **Suitable for almost all stones**. Especially recommended for **stones deeply associated with the moon (Moonstone, Selenite, Pearl)**.

**Caution**: Place it in a location where **it will not get wet from rain**. Moonlight can still reach through a window indoors, so safe indoor placement is recommended.

### <span id="m-sunlight"></span>Method 4: Sunlight Bath (Short Duration)

**Steps**:
1.  Place the crystal where it receives **morning sun (soft light from the morning)**.
2.  Remove it after a **maximum of 1 to 2 hours**.
3.  Do not leave it in direct sunlight for an extended period.

**Characteristics**: Suitable for **solar-affiliated stones** such as **Citrine, Carnelian, Tiger's Eye, and Quartz**.

**Important NO**: **Purple stones (Amethyst), pink stones (Rose Quartz, Kunzite), and blue stones (Aquamarine, Turquoise)** are **absolutely NOT suitable** as they will **fade in direct sunlight**. Refer to the suitability chart for details.

### <span id="m-water"></span>Method 5: Running Water

**Steps**:
1.  Turn on **room temperature to lukewarm water** at a low flow rate.
2.  Expose the crystal to the water for **30 seconds to 1 minute**.
3.  **Completely wipe away all moisture with a dry, soft cloth**.

**Characteristics**: Suitable for water-friendly stones such as **Quartz, Amethyst, and Citrine**. Short duration is the general rule.

**Important NO**: Stones such as **Lapis Lazuli, Turquoise, Pearl, Selenite, Angelite, Aragonite, and Malachite** will deteriorate with water, so they are **absolutely NOT suitable**.

### <span id="m-salt"></span>Method 6: Salt

**Steps**:
1.  Place **natural salt (coarse salt)** in a clean small dish.
2.  **Do NOT directly bury** the crystal in the salt (NG) — instead, use an indirect method: **place it on a small dish, with another small dish of salt placed nearby**.
3.  Leave it there for **6 to 12 hours**.
4.  After completion, discard the salt (do not reuse).

**Characteristics**: Considered a powerful cleansing method, but poses a **risk of deterioration for many stones**.

**Important NO**: **Directly burying the stone is NOT suitable for many stones**. It can cause corrosion of metal settings, surface scratches, and deterioration due to salt and moisture. **Not recommended for beginners**.

### <span id="m-sound"></span>Method 7: Tuning Fork and Crystal Bowl

**Steps**:
1.  Strike the tuning fork against metal to produce a sound (or gently tap a crystal bowl).
2.  Allow the sound to resonate **near the crystal (10 to 30 cm away)**.
3.  Perform a sound bath for **1 to 3 minutes**.

**Characteristics**: **Suitable for all stones**. It is the safest method as it does not use water, salt, or fire. The only caution is **not to touch metal parts directly**.

### <span id="m-meditation"></span>Method 8: Intention (Meditation-Based)

**Steps**:
1.  Hold the crystal **cupped in both hands**.
2.  **Take several deep breaths** — calming your mind.
3.  Express feelings of gratitude, such as **"Thank you for always being by my side."**
4.  Hold it for **1 to 5 minutes**.

**Characteristics**: **Zero physical impact on the stone and no tools required**, making it the closest method to **"cleansing as a ritual."** Many users find this alone sufficient in terms of **deepening their connection with the amulet**.

## <span id="storage"></span>③ Long-Term Storage | Preventing Physical Deterioration

How you store your stones when not wearing them is also a crucial factor that affects their beauty.

### 5 Basic Principles of Storage

1.  **Avoid humidity**: Store with a desiccant like silica gel. **Hematite, Pyrite, Aragonite, Selenite, and Howlite** are especially vulnerable to moisture.
2.  **Avoid direct sunlight**: Store in a dark place to prevent fading. Pay special attention to **purple, pink, and blue-toned stones**.
3.  **Avoid stone-to-stone contact**: Do not place stones of different hardness in the same compartment. **Soft stones (Pearl, Selenite, Howlite)** should be kept in **individual cloth pouches**.
4.  **Avoid contact with metal**: Rubbing against the metal fittings or chains of other jewelry can cause scratches. Utilize **jewelry box dividers**.
5.  **Avoid temperature fluctuations**: Do not store near heating appliances or in fluctuating temperatures by a window. A **drawer with a stable room temperature** is ideal.

### Common Storage Items

-   **Jewelry box (with dividers)**: Types that allow individual storage.
-   **Silk or soft cloth pouches**: For individual storage, especially suitable for Pearl and Selenite.
-   **Silica gel desiccant**: For moisture control.
-   **Light-blocking case**: For protection against direct sunlight.

## <span id="safety"></span>Safety Considerations | What to Be Careful Of During Care

We've also compiled safety considerations to **"protect yourself"** during the care process:

### ① Fire and Ventilation when Using Sage

-   ❌ Do not perform in a **closed, sealed space with windows shut** (can cause choking from smoke, activate smoke alarms).
-   ✅ **Open windows for ample ventilation** / **Perform on a heat-resistant dish** / **Confirm the fire is completely extinguished**.
-   ⚠️ Individuals with **asthma or respiratory conditions** should refrain from using sage or choose an alternative cleansing method.

### ② Metal Corrosion from Salt

-   ❌ Do not allow **silver or gold metal settings** to come into direct contact with salt.
-   ⚠️ **Plated products** are particularly prone to rapid deterioration from salt.
-   ✅ Salt cleansing should be limited to **the stone itself** and is not recommended for stones set in jewelry.

### ③ Homes with Small Children and Pets

-   ⚠️ **Small tumbled stones or raw crystals** pose a choking hazard (especially for children under 2).
-   ✅ Store on a **high shelf out of children's reach**.
-   ⚠️ Do not place stones that are **small enough for cats or dogs to ingest** in play areas.
-   ⚠️ Exercise particular caution with stones containing heavy metals or copper, such as **Malachite, Turquoise, and Pyrite**.

### ④ Handling Cleansing Tools

-   Confirm **complete extinguishment** of sage.
-   Discard salt used for cleansing **without reusing it** (tradition states not to return absorbed energy).
-   **Regularly cleanse quartz clusters with salt water** (the cluster itself also needs care).

### ⑤ Mineral Allergies and Skin Reactions

-   ⚠️ Individuals with **metal allergies** should check the material of the metal setting.
-   ⚠️ If **stones that directly touch the skin** cause itching or a rash, discontinue use.
-   ⚠️ For copper-bearing minerals such as **Malachite and Azurite**, be careful of dust during polishing (normal use is not an issue).

## <span id="repair"></span>Guidelines for Repair and Replacement

When you've had a stone for a long time, it might **chip, crack, or fade**. Here are some guidelines for what to do:

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| **Surface is cloudy or dirty** | Often restored with cleaning or repolishing. |
| **Small chip** | Often can be recut by a specialist (carat weight will decrease). |
| **Large crack** | Difficult to repair. A tradition is to **accept it as having "taken your place" and welcome a new stone**. |
| **Fading (due to UV, etc.)** | Often irreversible. **Many enthusiasts perceive this as the stone having "fulfilled its role"**. |
| **Metal setting deterioration or stone dislodged from setting** | Can be repaired at a jewelry repair shop. |
| **Crack expands** | May still be fine for decorative purposes. **Decide whether to continue wearing it based on safety**. |

**"How to part with a broken stone"** — traditionally:

-   **Return it to the earth** (in natural, pesticide-free soil, a garden, or within shrine grounds, where permitted).
-   **Store it carefully in a drawer** (to keep as a memento).
-   **Offer it to a shrine or temple** (at locations that accept offerings for ceremonial burning).

Rather than **carelessly discarding it as trash**, these methods allow you to **"send it off with gratitude,"** which is the traditional way of interacting with crystals.

## <span id="faq"></span>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Is cleansing really necessary?**

A. **It is not an act scientifically proven to have effects**. You **don't need to consider it "mandatory,"** and it's perfectly fine to approach it with the attitude of **"those who value it as a ritual do it."** **Daily cleaning (removing dirt) has a physical purpose**, so we recommend making that a habit.

**Q2. Will forgetting to cleanse my stone cause it to give "bad influences"?**

A. **No, it won't**."Stones will curse you if you forget to cleanse them," or "Negative energy will accumulate and bring misfortune" — such spiritual assertions **are not scientifically or medically proven**, and there's no need to feel unnecessary anxiety. **Amulets are merely "emotional support,"** and cleansing is just a **"ritual"** to deepen that connection.

**Q3. What if I want to cleanse many stones all at once?**

A. The most efficient way is to **prepare a quartz cluster or gemstone chips in a large tray** and **place multiple stones on it at once**. **Sage smoke** can also be used for multiple stones simultaneously in a larger space. **However, the essence of care lies in the feeling of "attending to each one individually,"** so it's also wonderful to **care for them separately when you have time**.

**Q4. Even if a stone is labeled "Water OK," are hot springs and pools still not allowed?**

A. **No, they are NOT allowed**. While **running tap water (for a short duration) is OK**, **sulfur in hot springs, chlorine in pools, and salt in seawater** will accelerate deterioration in almost all stones. **Removing your jewelry during bathing or sports** is key to making it last longer.

**Q5. My stones are touching each other in my jewelry box. Is that okay?**

A. **Caution is needed if there are differences in hardness**. It's ideal to **place soft stones (Pearl, Selenite, Howlite, Angelite) in individual cloth pouches**. Even stones of similar hardness can get **abrasion scratches from prolonged contact**, so a **jewelry box with dividers** is essential equipment for long-term preservation.

**Q6. Where can I buy cleansing supplies (sage, quartz clusters)?**

A. **Crystal specialty stores and healing shops** are reliable standard places. You **can also purchase online**, but make sure to confirm that **"white sage is a legal plant (generally available for circulation)."** There are also similar-sounding plants like **California sage and common sage**, so choose products **where the intended use is clearly specified**.
