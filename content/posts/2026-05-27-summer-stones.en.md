## <span id="summer-care"></span>Summer Crystal Care and Precautions

Summer is a season when **"direct sunlight, moisture, sweat, and salt"** can significantly impact stones. To **keep your stones healthy and beautiful for a long time**, please be mindful of the following points.

### ☀️ Stones Vulnerable to Direct Sunlight (Beware of Fading)

Some stones can experience fading with prolonged **direct sunlight**. Avoid prolonged outdoor activities or leaving them in a car.

| Vulnerable Stones | Reason |
|---|---|
| **Amethyst** | Purple pigments tend to fade |
| **Rose Quartz** | Pink color tends to fade |
| **Citrine (especially heat-treated)** | Color may change |
| **Fluorite** | Multiple colors tend to fade |
| **Opal** | Vulnerable to drying and cracking |

### 💧 Stones Vulnerable to Moisture, Sweat, and Salt

Some stones can experience **deterioration or discoloration** due to **sea bathing, pools, hot springs**, or **sweat from strenuous exercise**.

| Vulnerable Stones | Reason |
|---|---|
| **Turquoise** | Porous and absorbs water |
| **Lapis Lazuli** | Water and detergents can cause discoloration |
| **Malachite** | Vulnerable to water and acids |
| **Pearl** | Deteriorates with sweat and cosmetics |
| **Calcite** | Vulnerable to acids (sweat) |

### Recommended Summer Storage and Purification

| Method | Suitable Stones |
|---|---|
| **Moonlight Bath (night window)** | Almost all stones (safe for light-sensitive ones too) |
| **Clear Quartz Cluster** | Almost all stones (versatile) |
| **Selenite** | Almost all stones (versatile) |
| **Running Water** (short duration) | Only water-resistant stones (e.g., Clear Quartz, Tiger's Eye) |
| **Sunlight Bath** (short duration, morning only) | Clear Quartz, Citrine, Tiger's Eye, Carnelian |

## <span id="faq"></span>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. What is the absolute must-have stone for summer?**

A. **There is no "absolute single stone."** While several stones resonate with summer's symbolism, such as **Aquamarine, Larimar, Citrine, and Peridot**, the right answer depends on **your circumstances, preferences, and intuition**. The essence of choosing crystals is that **the stone you can cherish is truly the best one for you.**

**Q2. Can crystals prevent summer fatigue?**

A. **No, they cannot.** **Summer fatigue and heatstroke are within the medical domain**, and **hydration, rest, air-conditioned environments, and nutrition are paramount.** The stones in this article are meant as **"sentimental charms"** to be enjoyed from a **psychological perspective**, where **"gazing at them brings peace of mind"** or **"wearing them helps compose your feelings."** **If you have health concerns, please consult a medical professional.**

**Q3. Is it okay to wear my favorite bracelet to the beach?**

A. **It depends on the stone.** Water-resistant stones like **Clear Quartz and Tiger's Eye** are generally fine for short periods, but for stones vulnerable to moisture and salt, such as **Turquoise, Lapis Lazuli, Pearl, and Malachite**, **it's generally best to avoid wearing them.** The safest way to use them is to **take them off and place them in a storage pouch at the beach.**

**Q4. Does direct sunlight purify stones?**

A. **"Sunlight purification" is a traditionally recognized method**, but for **light-sensitive stones (e.g., Amethyst, Rose Quartz, Fluorite, Opal)**, it can cause **fading**. Please **check the specific precautions for each stone** before using this method. **Moonlight baths, clear quartz clusters, and selenite** are **versatile methods that can be used for almost all stones.**

**Q5. Is it okay to wear stones to summer festivals and fireworks displays?**

A. **Generally, yes, it's fine.** However, there is a risk of a **bracelet string breaking in a crowd** or the **possibility of metal parts oxidizing due to sweat**. So, carrying them in a **charm pouch** is also a lovely option. Please choose **a way that you can enjoy without stress** as a **"charm to support your feelings."**

**Q6. If I wear my summer birthstone, will I become stronger in summer?**

A. **There's no guarantee that you will "become stronger."** Birthstones are charms with the symbolic meaning of **"aligning with one's roots,"** and they **do not directly alter temperature, physical condition, or seasonal effects.** It's recommended to enjoy them for their **psychological effect**, where **"wearing summery colors helps compose your feelings."**

**Q7. What is the best timing to welcome a new stone in summer?**

A. **There is no "absolute timing."** Please choose **a day that feels like a "milestone" to you**, such as **the summer solstice (around June 21st), summer milestones (Tanabata, Obon), or your birthday.** **It's more about "what feelings you welcome it with" than "when you welcome it"** that matters.

**Q8. Should I believe in the "fortune-telling meanings" of crystals?**

A. **It is not meant to force you to "believe or not believe."** This website consistently values the perspective of **"enjoying cultural symbolism."** We do not adopt **definitive astrological conclusions** such as "people born in X month have X personality" or "people who wear X stone are always lucky." **Enjoying them as casual references** is the secret to a long and healthy relationship with crystals.
