## <span id="why"></span>Why are Bracelets Chosen So Often?

Power stones come in many forms: bracelets, pendants, rings, raw stones, and tumbled stones. Among these, **bracelets** are overwhelmingly chosen by everyone, from beginners to long-time enthusiasts. There are three main reasons for this.

### 1. Ease of Use: Low Barrier to Wearing

You just slip it onto your wrist, and you're good to go! No special skills or knowledge are required. They suit any outfit or occasion, and if the size is right, anyone can wear them immediately.

### 2. Constant Companionship: A Reminder for Your Intentions

Bracelets are always in your sight, and the gentle sensation on your wrist lasts all day. This constant presence makes them powerful reminders of "how you want to be," which is a unique strength of bracelets.

### 3. Freedom of Combination: Single Stones or Multiple Stones

You can enjoy a bracelet made with just one type of stone (a "**single-stone bracelet**") or one that combines multiple stones (a "**mixed bracelet**"). There's also room for future growth, like swapping out stones later or adding more bracelets for layering.

## <span id="size"></span>How to Choose Your Size (Measuring Your Wrist)

Size is the most crucial factor determining your satisfaction with a bracelet. It's a common story to buy a beautiful bracelet, only for it to end up forgotten in a drawer because the size isn't quite right. Let's start by **measuring your wrist correctly**.

### How to Measure Your Wrist

1.  Prepare a paper tape measure or a soft measuring tape.
2.  Wrap it around the **thinnest part of your wrist** (at the base, on the palm side) to measure its circumference.
3.  That value is your "**wrist size**."

If you don't have a tape measure, you can use a piece of string and then measure its length with a ruler.

### Just-Fit Versus Relaxed Fit

| Type          | Wrist +α    | Features                                                              |
| :------------ | :---------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Just-Fit      | +1cm (approx.) | Stays in place when you move, fits well next to a watch, might feel tight with larger beads |
| Standard      | +2cm (approx.) | Most common relaxed fit, drops slightly onto the back of your hand    |
| Relaxed       | +3cm (approx.) | Ideal for those who want to showcase their bracelet with movement, suits larger beads |

### Bead Size and Impression

| Bead Diameter | Impression          | Recommended For                                            |
| :------------ | :------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------- |
| 6mm           | Delicate, Elegant   | Slender wrists, people who want to layer with other accessories |
| 8mm           | Standard, Versatile | If you're unsure, start here                               |
| 10mm          | Prominent, Powerful | Men, people who want the bracelet to be a focal point      |
| 12mm and above | Unique, Distinctive | Those who enjoy a raw stone feel or rare stones          |

> **Tip**: As bead diameter increases, the inner circumference of the bracelet tends to shorten. Even for the same wrist size, a 10mm bead bracelet and a 6mm bead bracelet will require different overall lengths. When purchasing, be sure to confirm the "**bead diameter and inner circumference**" together.

## <span id="combination"></span>Basics of Bracelet Combinations

Here are some design tips for creating mixed bracelets (bracelets combining multiple stones).

### 1. Bead Count: Multiples of 8 are Considered Auspicious

Traditionally, the total number of beads in a bracelet has been considered auspicious when it's a **multiple of 8** (e.g., 16 beads, 24 beads, 32 beads). Adjust the bead diameter and total count to fit your wrist size.

| Bead Diameter | Approximate Total (for 17cm wrist) |
| :------------ | :------------------------------- |
| 6mm           | Around 28 beads                  |
| 8mm           | Around 22 beads                  |
| 10mm          | Around 17 beads                  |
| 12mm          | Around 14 beads                  |

### 2. Main and Supporting Roles: Focus on 1 or 2 Stones as the Main Act

If all stones are arranged in the same proportion, the energy might feel like there's **no main act**. A common composition is **1 or 2 main stones → 2 or 3 complementary stones → an amplifying clear quartz**.

-   **Main Stone**: The stone you want to focus your intentions on the most right now (e.g., Citrine = financial luck).
-   **Complementary Stone**: Stones that support the main stone (e.g., Tiger's Eye = decisiveness).
-   **Amplifier**: [Clear Quartz](/blog/clear-quartz/) = stabilizes and amplifies energy.

### 3. Color Balance: Limit to 3 Colors

Using too many colors can make the bracelet lose its cohesion. A stable and visually appealing design often comes from a three-color composition: **main color + complementary color + neutral (white or clear)**.

### 4. Role of Clear Quartz: Always Include a Few Beads

[Clear Quartz](/blog/clear-quartz/) is believed to **amplify the effects of other stones and harmonize their energy**. If you're unsure about a combination bracelet, simply adding a few clear quartz beads can stabilize the entire piece.

## <span id="how-to-wear"></span>How to Wear (Using Left and Right Hands)

There's an ancient **energy principle regarding the left and right hands** for bracelets.

-   **Left Hand** = Receiving (Input): The side for taking in energy.
-   **Right Hand** = Emitting (Output): The side for releasing energy from yourself.

### Stones for the Left Hand (for receiving wishes)

For wishes like love luck, health, healing, protection, intuition, or improving relationships – when you want to **draw something into yourself**, wear it on your left hand. Examples: [Rose Quartz](/blog/rose-quartz-meaning/), [Amethyst](/blog/amethyst/), [Moonstone](/blog/moonstone/).

### Stones for the Right Hand (for giving wishes)

For stones that **release something outwards**, such as financial luck (drawing in and attracting), decisiveness, action, or warding off evil, wear them on your right hand. Examples: [Citrine](/blog/citrine/), [Tiger's Eye](/blog/tigers-eye/), [Onyx](/blog/onyx/).

### Relation to Your Dominant Hand

Your dominant hand is more active and prone to bumps and scrapes. Therefore, it's recommended to wear **stones with lower hardness (Mohs hardness 5 or less)** on your non-dominant hand. Turquoise, Lapis Lazuli, Malachite, and Larimar, in particular, require extra care.

### Wearing Direction

Some bracelets have beads with logos or shaped components that indicate a "front." Generally, it's fine to wear it so the **front faces you when you look at it**. There are no strict rules.

## <span id="broken"></span>What to Do When Your Bracelet Breaks or Stretches

One day, your bracelet might just snap—it's an experience many enthusiasts have.

Some interpret it as "**the stone has fulfilled its purpose**" or "**it took something on your behalf**," while others view it purely physically as "**the elastic simply deteriorated and wore out from repeated wearing and removal**." Both interpretations are valid; there's **no single right answer** here.

However, one thing is certain: it's a stone you've spent a long time with. Whether you send it for re-stringing, express your gratitude and store it away, or return it to the earth – it's recommended to **bring closure** in a way that feels right to you, with a heart full of thanks.

> **Physical Perspective is Also Important**: There's no need to over-interpret "broken = a premonition of something bad." Elastic has a lifespan, and it will inevitably stretch and break with repeated wearing and removal. Simply getting it re-strung and continuing your relationship with it is a perfectly valid choice.

## <span id="fake"></span>Beware of Fakes and Treated Stones

Unfortunately, in the world of natural stones, a certain number of **fakes** and **treated stones** are in circulation. Even experts find it difficult to identify them perfectly, but it's good to know a few basics.

### Common "Fake or Treated" Patterns

| Type              | Description                                      |
| :---------------- | :----------------------------------------------- |
| Glass and Plastic | Completely different material. Common in cheap items |
| Dyed              | Original stone infused with color. Common in turquoise and chalcedony |
| Heat-Treated      | Treatment to enhance color vibrancy. Widely common in citrine and aquamarine |
| Impregnation      | Resin infused to improve strength and color        |
| Synthetic Stone   | Artificially created stone with the same composition |

### Key Points to Spot Them

-   **Color variation and inclusions**: Natural stones are rarely perfectly uniform.
-   **Feel and temperature**: Natural stones often feel cool, while resin or plastic feels lukewarm.
-   **Price**: Be wary of items priced extremely low compared to the market rate.
-   **Labeling**: Check for descriptions like "**treated**," "**colored**," or "**synthetic**."
-   **Certificate of Authenticity**: For expensive items, inquire if a certificate can be issued.

Treatments common in the industry, such as heat treatment, are not necessarily "fakes" and are often standard in the market. The problem arises when they are **sold at high prices as natural stones without disclosure**. For significant purchases, it's always safest to ask the salesperson about the presence of any treatments.

## <span id="faq"></span>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q. Which hand should I wear my bracelet on, left or right?

A. The basic rule is: **left hand for wishes you want to receive and right hand for wishes you want to release**. Traditionally, love luck, health, healing, and intuition are for the left, while financial luck, action, and warding off evil are for the right. Some people wear them on both hands to create "circulation." You can find more details in the [Correct Way to Wear Power Stones Guide](/blog/how-to-wear-powerstones/).

### Q. Can I layer it with other accessories?

A. Generally, yes! Layering with watches or fashion bracelets is quite natural. However, if you layer stones with **a large difference in hardness** (e.g., clear quartz Mohs 7 and malachite Mohs 3.5-4) directly rubbing against each other, the softer stone will get scratched. It's safer to place a different material in between or wear them on separate hands.

### Q. Should I wear it in the bath or while sleeping?

A. It's **best to take most stones off**. Water-sensitive stones (like turquoise, malachite, lapis lazuli, calcite, and more) can discolor or deteriorate. During sleep, continuous stress on the elastic can shorten its lifespan. The ideal is to **take it off and let it rest on a stone tray or clear quartz chips for purification**.

### Q. How soon can I expect to feel the effects?

A. This varies greatly from person to person, and there's no set timeframe like "**it will work in X days.**" "Effects" are not scientifically guaranteed; they are cultural and symbolic. Many enthusiasts say they didn't just "feel it work," but rather that "**their awareness changed, leading to changed actions.**" Approaching it calmly as a **spiritual reminder** without expecting immediate results will ultimately lead to the most satisfying relationship with your stone.

### Q. How many bracelets can I layer?

A. There are no rules! Some people layer 3 to 4 bracelets. However, it's good to be mindful of **stone compatibility**. Keeping it to around **one main bracelet + one complementary bracelet + one clear quartz bracelet** helps prevent the energy from becoming scattered.

### Q. My bracelet broke or stretched. What should I do?

A. Elastic has a lifespan, so first, consider **re-stringing it**. Many specialty stores offer this service. If it's a stone you've had for a long time, expressing your gratitude before sending it for re-stringing is a ritualistic gesture that can help you continue your connection.

### Q. Is it okay to give or receive power stones as gifts?

A. Power stones are perfectly fine to give and receive as gifts. In fact, a **stone imbued with someone's thoughts and feelings** becomes a meaningful talisman in itself. If the size isn't right, it can often be **adjusted by re-stringing**, so don't hesitate to consult the giver first.

### Q. I'm unsure about my first bracelet.

A. If you're wondering what to get "within the bracelet form," a combination of **birthstone and intuition** is usually the choice you'll regret the least. If you're still deciding on "**your very first power stone**" in general, you might also want to check out the [Beginner's Guide to Your First Power Stone](/blog/powerstone-beginner-guide/) or the [Complete Guide to Choosing Power Stones](/blog/how-to-choose-powerstones/).
