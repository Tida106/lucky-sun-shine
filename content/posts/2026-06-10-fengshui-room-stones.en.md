## Introduction

Ever bought a crystal, only to wonder where on earth to put it? Haven't we all felt that way?

In Feng Shui, it's believed that **different types of "Qi" (energy) flow through each space in your home.** The Qi in your bedroom supports peaceful sleep and restoration, while the Qi in your entrance invites energy from the outside world. By choosing and placing stones that match each space, the stones and the environment are said to "resonate," making it easier for them to unleash their full potential.

In this article, I've put together a list of **recommended stones, their placement, and how to use them** for seven different spaces in your home. If you've bought stones but aren't sure where to put them, this is your ready-to-use placement guide!

## ① Entrance: Guarding Against Negative Energy and Welcoming Good Qi

### Why the Entrance is the Most Important

In Feng Shui, **the entrance is the gateway for all the Qi (energy) in your home.** Both good and bad energy from outside first pass through here. Keeping your entrance tidy is said to directly boost the overall luck of your entire home.

### Recommended Stones

**Black Tourmaline (The Strongest Protection Against Negative Energy)**

-   It's considered a stone that blocks negative energy brought in from outside.
-   Placing one to two pieces on either side of the entrance or on top of a shoe cabinet is said to act as a "gatekeeper."
-   Tumbled stones (polished stones) are more practical than raw stones, as they are easier to wipe dust off.

**Clear Quartz (Universal Purification and Energy Boost)**

-   This foundational stone is believed to amplify good energy and brighten the entire space.
-   Placing a cluster or raw stone on a small dish atop a shoe cabinet is a classic choice.
-   If it's in a spot where sunlight hits, the refraction of light will make the space even more vibrant.

**Placement Tips**

-   Direct placement on the threshold (Genkan-Tataki) is a no-go (a position where stones might be stepped on or kicked implies "being treated carelessly" for the stone's energy).
-   **On top of the shoe cabinet, slightly below eye level** is generally best.
-   Placing a small dish or cloth underneath the stone shows respectful treatment.

## ② Living Room: Harmonizing Family and Relationships

### The Role of Qi in the Living Room

The living room is where families gather and conversations happen. In Feng Shui, it's believed that energy influencing **family relationships, social luck, and communication** flows here. Homes where family members get along well are also said to have smoother external relationships.

### Recommended Stones

**Rose Quartz (Love and Harmony)**

-   This stone is said to gently harmonize relationships and deepen family bonds.
-   It's recommended to place it on top of a TV stand or on a visible shelf.
-   Arranging several pieces together in a small dish can also blend well with your interior decor.

**Amethyst (Mental Harmony and Relaxation)**

-   It is said to soothe irritation and tension, creating a calm atmosphere.
-   Placing it near the sofa or on a shelf in a gathering area for the family is said to be effective.
-   A cluster (a formation of crystals) has a strong presence on its own and is popular as a decorative stone.

**Citrine (Brightness and Prosperity)**

-   It's believed to activate the overall energy of the home with its bright energy.
-   Placing it near a window or on a sunny shelf is said to bring out its qualities as a "sun stone."

**Placement Tips**

-   Placing Rose Quartz in the **southwest corner** of the room is said to align with Feng Shui's "direction of relationships."
-   Displaying it with family photos is also a great idea.

## ③ Bedroom: Nurturing Peaceful Sleep, Healing, and Love Life

### The Bedroom: Your "Qi Recharging Station"

People spend about one-third of their day in the bedroom. During this time, both mind and body recover, and energy for the next day is stored. **Peaceful sleep, healing, and romantic luck** are all said to be nurtured by the Qi in the bedroom.

### Recommended Stones

**Moonstone (Peaceful Sleep, Feminine Intuition, and Love Life)**

-   Known as the "stone of the moon," it's said to improve sleep quality and promote pleasant dreams.
-   The bedside table is a classic placement spot.
-   If you want to boost your love life, placing it near your pillow is recommended.

**Howlite (Anxiety Relief and Deep Sleep)**

-   This white stone is said to quiet thoughts and make it easier to fall asleep.
-   Placing it under your pillow or on your bedside table is said to be effective.
-   Its white appearance also harmonizes with the clean aesthetic of a bedroom.

**Amethyst (Nightmare Protection and Mental Stability)**

-   This stone has long been placed in bedrooms as an amulet to ward off nightmares.
-   There's also a traditional use of placing small stones in each of the four corners of the bed to create a "barrier" or protective space.

**Placement Tips**

-   **Avoid placing near mirrors**: In Feng Shui, mirrors are said to reflect energy and make sleep shallower.
-   Also, be mindful of placing them near TVs or electronic devices (the heat from electromagnetic waves can sometimes damage stones).

## ④ Home Office and Study: Enhancing Concentration and Career Luck

### Harmonizing the Energy of Your Workspace

A home office is an excellent place for stones that enhance **concentration, judgment, career luck, and financial luck.** Organizing your desk area is said to directly correlate with improved performance.

### Recommended Stones

**Tiger's Eye (Decision-Making, Career Luck, and Wealth Luck)**

-   Known as the "stone of will," it's believed to boost decisiveness and initiative.
-   Placing it in the front-right (southeast direction) of your desk is said to bring a double effect of wealth and career luck.

**Citrine (Financial Luck, Self-Esteem, and Creativity)**

-   Also called the "merchant's stone," it's believed to attract positive energy for business prosperity.
-   Placing it to the right of your computer or on top of documents is a classic choice.
-   Keep it in a visible spot, with the intention of "placing good financial luck."

**Fluorite (Concentration and Logical Thinking)**

-   This stone is said to organize thoughts and help maintain concentration.
-   It's recommended to place it next to your screen or somewhere visible during work.

**Placement Tips**

-   Considering the **three divisions of your desk (left: receiving, center: working, right: sending)** can help harmonize the flow of Qi.
-   More detailed placement methods are explained with differentiation in the next section.

## ⑤ Kitchen and Dining: Cultivating Health, Abundance of Food, and Financial Luck

### Dining Area: Your "Qi Replenishment Station"

The kitchen and dining area are places where the Qi of **food and health** gathers. In Feng Shui, "eating" is directly linked to "replenishing Qi," and it's said that organizing your dining area positively influences not only health luck but overall abundance.

### Recommended Stones

**Green Aventurine (Health, Growth, and Abundance)**

-   This green stone corresponds to the Wood element in the Five Elements. It's believed to promote health and growth.
-   It's recommended to place it in the center of the dining table or on a kitchen shelf in a small dish.
-   Sprinkling small tumbled stones in the pot of a houseplant is also a popular arrangement.

**Citrine (Abundance, Financial Luck, and Appetite Stimulation)**

-   Its bright yellow energy is said to transform the dining table into a "place of abundance."
-   Placing it on a decorative shelf above the dining table or in a china cabinet is a great idea.
-   Since meals are placed in the center of the dining table, choose an unobtrusive spot like a shelf at the end.

**Rose Quartz (Family Bonds and Enriching Mealtime)**

-   It's believed to warm the energy of the dining table where families gather.
-   Placing it near dining chairs, on a shelf within view, is said to make meal times more harmonious.

**Placement Tips**

-   **Avoid placing directly next to the stove or sink**: Heat, steam, and water can sometimes degrade the stones.
-   The kitchen is a place where fire and water coexist (a "clash of fire and fire and water" in the Five Elements). To balance this, **stones of the Earth element (Tiger's Eye and Citrine)** are considered a good match.

## ⑥ Toilet and Bathroom: Purification, Cleanliness, and Dispelling Misfortune

### Treating Water Spaces as "Purification Zones"

In Feng Shui, toilets and bathrooms are considered "places where Qi flows out." However, on the flip side, they can also be utilized as spaces to enhance the Qi of **purification, dispelling misfortune, and cleanliness.**

### Recommended Stones

**Selenite (Purification, Light, and Cleanliness)**

-   Also known as the "stone of light," it's believed to fill the space with clean energy.
-   Placing it on a toilet shelf or next to the sink is said to create a clean energy flow.
-   Since it has a **tendency to dissolve in water**, be careful not to place it directly next to the sink or bathtub, or let it submerge.

**Clear Quartz (Universal Purification)**

-   A classic purification stone. It's believed to absorb negative energy in toilets and bathrooms.
-   Purification once or twice a month is especially important here (as it's said to absorb a lot of water's energy).
-   It's recommended to start with one to two small tumbled stones.

**Placement Tips**

-   **Never leave stones indefinitely**: Stones placed in wet areas require purification and wiping once or twice a month.
-   **Do not submerge**: Selenite dissolves in water. Citrine and Tiger's Eye also should not be submerged for long periods.
-   It's generally safer not to keep valuable or cherished stones permanently in the bathroom.

## ⑦ Children's Room: Promoting Growth, Protection, and Talent Development

### Stones That Harmonize With Children's Energy

When choosing stones for a child's room, it's recommended to select them with **a focus on security, protection, and growth support.** Children are said to be more sensitive than adults, so gentler stones are considered more suitable than overly strong ones.

### Recommended Stones

**Rose Quartz (Love, Security, and Emotional Stability)**

-   Its warm "energy of love" is believed to gently stabilize a child's emotions.
-   It's safest to display it on a high shelf or in a corner of a study desk, out of reach.

**Amethyst (Protection, Mental Calmness, and Concentration)**

-   This stone has long been used as an amulet to protect children.
-   It also supports peaceful sleep. Place it on a bedside table or shelf.

**Sodalite (Learning and Logical Thinking)**

-   This deep blue stone is said to harmonize the energy for studying and learning.
-   It's recommended to place it on a study desk or next to a bookshelf.

**Safety Precautions**

-   **Beware of choking hazards with small stones**: Place small tumbled stones or tiny chips out of reach of toddlers.
-   It's safer to display **larger raw stones or clusters** on high shelves.
-   Pointed raw stones (like Clear Quartz points) should be placed considering the risk of falling and injury.

## Placement and Care Notes

### Wet Areas (Kitchen, Toilet, and Bathroom)

-   **Water-Sensitive Stones**: Selenite (dissolves in water) and Morion (may discolor if submerged for long periods).
-   For stones in wet areas, it's essential to **wipe them with a dry cloth once or twice a month.**
-   Stones that dissolve easily should not be placed in wet areas; instead, display them on a nearby shelf.

### Direct Sunlight

-   **Stones That Fade in Sunlight**: Amethyst, Rose Quartz, and Citrine (may fade with prolonged direct sunlight exposure).
-   Short periods of sun exposure (1 to 2 hours) for purification are generally okay, but avoid placing them in constantly sunny spots.
-   Moonbathing (placing by a window on a full moon night) is considered a safe purification method for many stones.

### Choking Hazards and Safety Management

-   Small stones less than 3cm in diameter should be placed **on high shelves in homes with toddlers or small pets.**
-   Also be careful about places where pets might lick them (floor, low shelves).
