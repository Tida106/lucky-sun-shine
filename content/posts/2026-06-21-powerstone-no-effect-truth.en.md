## First, Our Most Important, Honest Statement

**Crystals have no scientifically proven effects.**

We have no intention of being ambiguous about this. Terms like "vibration," "energy," and "chakra influence" — there is currently no reproducible experimental data or medical evidence to support them. Holding a stone will not cure an illness, nor will it magically increase your financial fortune.

Even on this website, in articles about individual stones, we only write in the form of hearsay, like "it is said to be..." or "it is believed to...". This is not a **rhetorical device to avoid making definitive statements**, but rather because **there is truly no basis for making such claims**.

Therefore, if you are asking, "Do they work scientifically?", our answer is a simple one: **"No, they don't."**

## So, Why Do People Own Stones?

Even acknowledging the lack of scientific effects, the fact remains that people worldwide have worn stones for thousands of years. Why is that? We believe there are four main reasons.

### ① The Psychological Effect of Feeling Secure with an Amulet

This is called the **placebo effect**, but a placebo is by no means "nothing."

Someone who puts on their favorite stone before leaving home in the morning gains **a moment of calm** just as they step out. A person entering a meeting in a calm state has a little more room to choose their words carefully. Those carefully chosen words subtly change how others react. Over half a year, that can certainly lead to the result that "human relationships have slightly improved."

The stone itself didn't do anything. However, **your action of "holding the stone and taking a pause"** has indeed changed something. Whether you call this "effective" depends on the definition of the word, but at the very least, **it has meaning**.

### ② Value as Culture and History

Throughout ancient Egypt, Mesopotamia, and China, humankind has imbued stones with meaning. It wasn't that they were "scientifically wrong," but rather, it was **a language people used to understand the world in an era when science didn't exist**.

That language is still alive today. Birthstones, diamonds in wedding rings, omamori stones from shrines — entering into this symbolic system, passed down through culture, isn't about **"believing in superstition," but rather "participating in a story that humanity has cherished."** This isn't about effects; it's about context.

### ③ Simply Because They Are Beautiful

This is a very important perspective.

Seeing the pale pink of rose quartz can soothe your heart. Being simply mesmerized by the purple translucence of amethyst. **That alone is a perfectly valid reason to own a stone.** You wouldn't ask someone buying a painting, "Does that painting have any effects?" Nor would you ask someone listening to music, "What is that song good for?"

If you don't expect effects, a stone is simply a beautiful mineral. The value of **keeping beautiful things close** is something no one can deny.

### ④ They Become a Catalyst for Self-Reflection

The time you spend thinking "Which stone should I choose?" is the same as **the time you spend thinking "What do I truly desire right now?"**

Someone looking for a stone for love luck is acknowledging that they are troubled by matters of the heart. Someone looking for a stone for financial luck is trying to re-evaluate their relationship with money. **The process of choosing a stone clarifies the outline of your desires** — this effect happens not because the stone is magical, but because **the act of choosing itself is an act of introspection**.

## "If They Don't Work, Is There No Point in Buying Them?"

After reading this far, you might be thinking, "If it doesn't work, then what's the point of paying for it?"

But consider this: probably no one would say an amulet is "meaningless because it has no medical effect." Nor would they say an accessory is "unnecessary because it has no function." And there should be no one who dismisses collectible figures or records as "useless because they lack practicality."

**Stones belong in that company.** They are amulets, accessories, objects of hobby, and tools for a small ritual to connect with yourself. When you remove the yardstick of "effects," **there is still something substantial left**.

Conversely, **if you buy one expecting too many "effects," you'll be disappointed.** If you buy it thinking your life will change in three days, in three days you'll feel it "didn't work." This isn't the stone's fault; it's a matter of how you bought it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q. Why do some people feel the effects while others don't?
**A.** We believe it's mainly due to **differences in expectations and daily usage.** People who feel the effects tend to use stones as "ritual tools," taking a moment to check their state each morning when they put them on. Those who don't feel effects often buy them and leave them in a drawer. Our view is that the difference comes not from the stone itself, but from **how one interacts with the stone**.

### Q. Do more expensive stones have greater effects?
**A.** **Price and effects are unrelated.** High-quality stones are beautiful and offer greater joy of ownership, but that's a matter of value, not effects. Even a 100-yen natural stone can hold far more meaning for someone who wears it daily and uses it for self-reflection than an expensive bracelet left dormant in a drawer.

### Q. I have an illness or worries, can I rely on stones?
**A.** **Stones are not a substitute for medical care.** If you're not feeling well, please consult a medical institution, and if you're struggling emotionally, seek help from a professional. Stones are merely **amulets to keep by your side, in parallel with professional treatment.** It's crucial not to get the order wrong.

### Q. Is there value in owning one even if I don't believe in them?
**A.** Yes, there is. **"Owning it because it's beautiful" and "owning it as part of culture"** are perfectly valid reasons. There's no need for non-believers to believe. Simply enjoy the design as an accessory or decorative item. There's absolutely no problem with that.
