## <span id="basics"></span>Basic Concepts of Colors and Power Stones

Colors have universal images that humanity has intuitively understood since ancient times, regardless of culture. **Red signifies passion, blue tranquility, and green healing**—these perceptions are echoed in modern color therapy and color psychology, and they also correspond to Feng Shui's Five Elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water).

In the world of power stones, the **"symbolism embedded in colors" and the "meanings traditionally associated with stones"** often overlap. For example, the gentle pink of Rose Quartz has been universally cherished as a symbol of "gentleness and love," while the deep blue of Lapis Lazuli was considered the "color of truth and wisdom" in ancient Egypt.

> The biggest advantage of choosing by color is that **"your favorite color is a sign of what you need right now."** As you read this article today, be sure to note which colors you are naturally drawn to.

## <span id="choose"></span>Favorite Color vs. Needed Color – Which Should You Choose?

After looking at these eight colors, you're probably torn between two feelings:

- **"I want to choose by my favorite color"** — You're purely drawn to a certain hue.
- **"I want to choose by the color I need"** — You want a color that aligns with your current life theme.

To put it simply, **both approaches are correct**. And often, **the color you "feel drawn to" is also the color you need right now.**

In the world of color therapy, it's said that "people unconsciously prefer colors that represent the energy they currently lack." So, first and foremost, **trust the color you chose intuitively.** The reasons will follow.

> However, also cherish any colors gifted to you. A color you wouldn't choose yourself can sometimes open new doors to yourself.

## <span id="combine"></span>Tips for Combining Multiple Colors

When combining multiple colors for bracelets or hand stones, there are two traditional approaches:

### ① Combining Similar Themes

This method involves **layering colors with similar themes**, such as "pink and white" or "yellow and brown." It's gentle, cohesive, and reassuring for beginners.

Example: **Rose Quartz (pink) and Clear Quartz (white)** → Nurturing love luck with purification.

### ② Combining Complementary Themes

This method involves **combining contrasting colors**, such as "black and yellow" or "blue and red." It creates a balance of yin and yang: protection and offense, calmness and passion.

Example: **Onyx (black) and Citrine (yellow)** → Attracting wealth while protecting it.

## <span id="faq"></span>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q. Do all stones of the same color have the same effect?
**A.** While color is a significant clue, each stone has its own story and symbolism. For example, even among blue stones, Lapis Lazuli is associated with "truth and courage," while Aquamarine signifies "healing and dialogue," with different nuances. It's recommended to **narrow down the general direction by color, then check the specifics in individual articles for each stone.**

### Q. Flashy colors are hard to wear daily. What should I do?
**A.** We recommend **carrying small tumbled stones or chips in a pouch**, or **incorporating them as a single bead in a bracelet.** The energy of a color is said to depend not on its size, but on whether you keep it close to you.

### Q. I was recommended a color I dislike. Should I still wear it?
**A.** There's no need to force yourself. A color that you instinctively "dislike" might be a mismatch for your current state. Please feel free to choose a different color. Compatibility with colors can also change over time.

### Q. Can you guarantee the effects?
**A.** No, we cannot. The meanings of colors and stones are cultural and symbolic; **there are no scientifically proven effects.** Please enjoy them purely as "charms" or "daily mood boosters."
