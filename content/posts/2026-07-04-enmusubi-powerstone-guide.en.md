It is widely loved as a first stone for "centering yourself" before seeking encounters.

## Tips for Wearing Power Stones

### Why Wearing on the Left Hand is Recommended

In the world of power stones, the **left hand is often considered the "receiving hand,"** and wearing a bracelet on the left is recommended for "receiving" connections and good fortune. Conversely, the right hand is seen as the "giving hand," and is used when you want to project energy outwards.

### The Chest Area, Near the Heart Chakra, is also Recommended

Rose Quartz, Morganite, and Aventurine are all said to correspond to the Fourth Chakra (Heart Chakra). Wearing these stones as a pendant near your chest is believed to help you directly receive the energy of love.

### Carry Them in a Pouch or Bag

If you're not a fan of stone bracelets, carrying small tumbled stones (polished, rounded stones) in a pouch or bag is also a good option. Touching the stone in your daily life and saying a quiet "May I have good connections today" is a natural way to use it as a charm.

### Q. Do love matchmaking stones work when I'm alone?

Yes, they can be used when you are alone. In fact, in terms of "centering yourself," nurturing self-love with Rose Quartz during periods of solitude is often said to lead to better encounters later on.

### Q. Can married people wear love matchmaking stones?

Of course! Love matchmaking refers to good connections with all people, not just romantic partners or spouses, but also friends, work connections, and community ties. Many married individuals use them with the goal of "being blessed with good work connections" or "making new friends."

### Q. Can men wear love matchmaking stones?

Absolutely! There are no gender restrictions for love matchmaking stones. While Moonstone is sometimes called a "feminine stone," some men use it to "enhance sensitivity and intuition."

### Q. Can I use multiple stones together?

Yes, you can. However, some believe that having too many stones can disperse energy, so it's recommended to start with 1 or 2 stones. For tips on combining stones, please refer to [Power Stone Combination Guide](/blog/powerstone-combinations/).

### Q. How long does it take to feel the effects?

The experience varies from person to person. Some feel "more positive immediately after starting to wear them," while others experience "gradual changes." Stones are ultimately charms, and remember that opportunities for encounters and connections come from your own actions. It's important to have a long-term approach, regularly [purifying](/blog/purification-complete-guide/) your stones to maintain their energy.
