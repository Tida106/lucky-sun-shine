# For HSPs Who Dislike Morion: 3 Gentle, "Non-Black" Protective Stones

Do you feel utterly drained after being in crowds? Do you find yourself taking the tense atmosphere at work or others' bad moods personally, causing you distress? Are you struggling with "vicarious fatigue" or "empathic fatigue," my dear? Especially for sensitive and gentle women with an HSP (Highly Sensitive Person) temperament, psychological boundaries with others can be permeable, making it easy to absorb negative emotions from your surroundings into your heart.

You might be tempted to try black quartz (Morion), rumored to be the "strongest for protection," to shield yourself. However, many people feel daunted by its jet-black and rugged appearance, or once they try wearing it, they might feel a resistance, thinking, "it feels heavy and cold somehow" or "its power is too strong and caused 'stone sickness.'"

In fact, that feeling is completely natural, you know. Morion has the property of "blocking and absorbing" all kinds of negative energy with a powerful shield. However, for HSP women who have already lost mental reserves due to daily exhaustion, Morion's strong blocking energy can sometimes feel "too strong and intimidating," overwhelming the mind and body, and leading to "stone sickness."

Furthermore, for HSPs who often experience sensory sensitivities (especially tactile sensitivity), Morion's weight (specific gravity of approximately 2.65) and the chilling coolness characteristic of quartz can unconsciously become a source of stress, causing tension and stiff shoulders. What's more, a jet-black Morion can stand out awkwardly against natural clothing, creating new tension for sensitive women who are conscious of how they are perceived.

If Morion doesn't resonate with you, there's absolutely no need to blame yourself and think, "my heart is weak." Instead of repelling negative energy with rigid armor, why not try a gentle approach that embraces your sensitivity and protects you without denying it?

This time, we'll introduce three "non-black" barrier stones that protect you not by "blocking" but by "purifying (washing away)," "detaching (building boundaries)," and "transforming (sublimating into warmth)"—gentle approaches that are less burdensome on your heart.

## 1. Your Amulet, Like Water: Oise-ishi Cultivates the "Tokowaka" Purification System

Do you find that once you absorb others' negative emotions, you struggle to let them go, accumulating them until you feel heavy? For you, my dear, we recommend **Oise-ishi (Ise Stone)**, which gleams in pure white.

Oise-ishi is a beautiful white quartz collected in the basin of the Miyagawa River, Mie Prefecture's largest river. This stone has deep historical ties to Ise Jingu Shrine, considered the spiritual home of the Japanese people. During the Shikinen Sengu, a ritual rebuilding of the shrine that occurs every 20 years, there is a ceremony called "Oshiraishi-mochi Gyoji," designated as a national intangible folk cultural property. In this ceremony, pure white "oshiraishi" (sacred white stones) gathered from the Miyagawa river basin are laid in the grounds of the new main sanctuary.

At the root of this ceremony is the Shinto concept of **"Tokowaka" (eternal youth and freshness)**. While material things and spiritual energy (ki) inevitably degrade and decay, "Tokowaka" maintains eternal beauty by constantly renewing and purifying them. In Shinto, "sin" and "defilement" are not seen as absolute evils, but rather as states where life force has diminished ("kigare" - spirit exhaustion). It has emphasized restoring oneself to a pure state through "Misogi" (purification by washing in water) and "Harai" (exorcism and expiation), metaphorically letting things flow from the river to the sea.

The charm of Oise-ishi lies not in "repelling" negative energy, but in a mechanism of acceptance and purification that "gently washes away impurities with clear water," much like this Shinto approach. It helps you release emotions you've absorbed from others, letting go of them into the natural flow instead of carrying them alone. In color psychology, white symbolizes "purification and reset," gently returning your mind and body to a neutral state without overstimulating your autonomic nervous system.

**【Everyday Usage Scenes】**
On days when you absorb grumpy auras on a crowded train or in the office, and feel too exhausted to move once you get home, try touching your Oise-ishi on your wrist and consciously think, **"Let's let today's fatigue (kigare, or spirit exhaustion) flow away with the water right here."** By using handwashing or bathing as a reset trigger, you can smoothly cleanse your mind and turn it into a wonderful switch to greet tomorrow as your "Tokowaka" new self. It also perfectly blends with soft office casual attire, and it's a bonus that it doesn't give off an intimidating protective aura to those around you.

## 2. Gently Drawing Boundaries: Tidying Up Your Mind with Prehnite

Do you feel exhausted from endlessly listening to someone's complaints at work, or do you end up burdening yourself by not being able to refuse requests? The translucent, muscat-green stone known as **Prehnite (Grape Stone)** will gently accompany a kind soul like you.

Prehnite was discovered in South Africa in 1788, a historical stone, being the first mineral in history to be named after its discoverer, Baron von Prehn. As its Japanese name "Grape Stone" suggests, raw Prehnite often forms in clusters resembling bunches of Muscat grapes. Prehnite often contains black to dark green needle-like crystals (epidote) within it, and its beauty lies in the way a strong core is felt within its soft, healing green.

Prehnite's core themes are **"letting go" and "mental decluttering."** The biggest reason HSPs experience vicarious fatigue is when the boundaries between themselves and others become blurred, causing them to shoulder others' problems and emotions as if they were their own.

Rather than coldly rejecting others, Prehnite helps you gently draw boundaries, as if placing a transparent filter, thinking, **"This far is their issue, and this is my heart."** In color psychology, its refreshing muscat-green color calms sympathetic nervous tension, relaxes muscles, and brings a sense of security. Furthermore, the epidote core within gently reminds you of your "inner compass," keeping you from being swayed by your surroundings, you know.

**【Everyday Usage Scenes】**
If you're about to hear negative complaints at work, touch your Prehnite and use it as an anchor to mentally draw a line, thinking, **"This is their issue, and not my emotion."** Also, on nights of information overload, when your brain feels overheated from social media news, take a deep breath while gazing at this gentle green. It will help you declutter unnecessary emotions from your mind, supporting a digital detox that leads to peaceful sleep, you know. It adds a refreshing accent to natural linen and cotton outfits.

## 3. Surprising Lightness and Warmth: The Sunny Haven Amber Delivers

Do you want to wear power stones but find your arm feels heavy and gets tired? Do you dislike the chilling cold when you put a stone on in winter? For such HSP women with sensory sensitivities, the ultimate gentle companion is **Amber**.

Unlike hard minerals found underground, Amber is an "organic gemstone," formed from fossilized tree resin from tens of millions of years ago. It possesses remarkable physical characteristics that set it apart from other stones. First, it boasts **incredible lightness** (specific gravity of approximately 1.08), light enough to float in saltwater, and weighing less than half of typical quartz. Furthermore, being resinous, its thermal conductivity is very low, so it doesn't have that "chilling" coldness when touched. Instead, it reflects your body heat, returning a gentle, skin-like warmth.

Its energy dynamics are also unique. Instead of "absorbing and blocking" negative energy like Morion, Amber, imbued with ancient solar energy and the life force of the earth, acts like a circulating filter, taking in negative energy and **"transforming it into warm, positive energy to be released."** In color psychology, its honey-like amber color warms a heart chilled by fear and anxiety from within, drawing out positive feelings.

**【Everyday Usage Scenes】**
Because it completely lacks the physical stressors of "weight and coldness," it's perfect as an everyday amulet to wear as your partner. Even when worn as a necklace near your delicate chest (close to your heart chakra), it won't strain your neck. When the atmosphere at work feels tense, or your spirit shrinks after being subjected to critical opinions, gently clasp your Amber. Its gentle warmth will convey a sense of security to your brain, acting as a buffer to smoothly deflect the prickly external atmosphere, you know.
