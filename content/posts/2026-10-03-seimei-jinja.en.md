Hello! It's Sun-chan from Lucky Sun Shine! ☀️

Have you ever visited **Seimei Shrine (Seimei Jinja)** in Kyoto? The thousand-year-old capital of Kyoto is full of fascinating temples and shrines, but Seimei Shrine is a truly special place, filled with a mysterious atmosphere unlike any ordinary shrine!

Seimei Shrine enshrines **Lord Abe no Seimei**, a legendary astronomer and onmyoji (yin-yang diviner) who actually lived during the Heian period. After Lord Seimei passed away, the shrine was built on the site of his former residence in 1007, by order of Emperor Ichijo.

The history board in the precinct says that the enshrined deity is the spirit of Abe no Seimei and that the shrine was established in the 4th year of Kanko (1007). It also explains the "Seimei Kikyo" seal and describes the five-pointed star as a mark against evil ☀️

At most shrines, the name of the deity is displayed on the torii gate, but at Seimei Shrine's first torii, you'll find a golden **"five-pointed star" (gobosei, also called the Seimei Kikyo)** proudly displayed instead! This five-pointed star represents the harmony of the ancient wisdom known as "Onmyo Gogyo" (the theory of yin-yang and the five elements: wood, fire, earth, metal, and water), and is considered a powerful protective barrier symbol that wards off all evil spirits and misfortune.

## ☀️ The Power Spots of "Yin-Yang Five Elements," Purification, and Protection Found Within the Shrine Grounds

The grounds of Seimei Shrine are a true "hands-on power spot" where the cosmic worldview of Onmyodo and the energy of nature are condensed together. Let's look at the points that will cleanse your daily bad luck and invite good fortune!

### Seimei Kikyo (Five-Pointed Star / Gobosei)
* **【Features】:** A beautiful, single-stroke five-pointed star crest engraved on the first torii gate, as well as on the lanterns, ema (votive plaques), and roof tiles throughout the shrine grounds.
* **【Deep Background and Origins】:** In Onmyodo, it symbolizes the cycle and perfect harmony of the five basic elements that make up all things: wood, fire, earth, metal, and water. It's said that by these elements nurturing one another and maintaining balance, an absolute barrier is formed that prevents the intrusion of evil.
* **【Sun-chan's Top Pick Explanation】:** The moment you pass through the torii gate, you'll feel a crisp, clear atmosphere, as if the clutter in your heart is dissolving away! As you look up at the golden five-pointed star, try visiting with the image of bringing your own inner balance into harmony ☀️
* **【Official Info】:** [Seimei Shrine Official Website (Shrine Grounds Guide)](https://www.seimeijinja.jp/guide/)

### Seimei Well (Seimei-i)
* **【Features】:** Pure, famous water that still wells up continuously from the shrine grounds today, long counted among the famous waters of central Kyoto.
* **【Deep Background and Origins】:** This sacred spring is said to have been brought forth by Lord Abe no Seimei's spiritual power. The zodiac signs are carved into the stone slab of the well, and every year on the day of Risshun (the start of spring), shrine priests turn the water spout to face "Ehou," the most auspicious direction of that year. Records even show that the tea master Sen no Rikyu once drew water here to hold a tea ceremony.
* **【Sun-chan's Top Pick Explanation】:** The water welling up here is auspicious energy itself, a gift from heaven and earth! After purifying yourself at the temizuya (water pavilion), be sure to take a deep breath in front of the Seimei Well and feel the blessed power flowing from that year's lucky direction ☀️ (Note: if you take some water home to drink, please boil it first for safety.)
* **【Official Info】:** [Seimei Shrine Official Website (Shrine Grounds Guide)](https://www.seimeijinja.jp/guide/)

### Yakuyoke Momo (Protective Peach)
* **【Features】:** A bronze statue of a peach enshrined to the right of the main hall, polished to a shine from being gently touched by visitors.
* **【Deep Background and Origins】:** In Chinese mythology, Onmyodo, and even in the Japanese myths of the Kojiki and Nihon Shoki, the peach has long been revered as a sacred fruit and medicine that "wards off evil and dispels malevolent spirits." Even the old folktale of Momotaro setting off to defeat ogres has its roots in the peach's power to ward off evil.
* **【Sun-chan's Top Pick Explanation】:** Try gently stroking the peach with both hands, as if entrusting it with the anxiety, stress, and negative feelings (impurities) that build up in everyday life. Once you're done, you'll feel your heart grow lighter, and a positive smile will come naturally! ☀️
* **【Official Info】:** [Seimei Shrine Official Website (Shrine Grounds Guide)](https://www.seimeijinja.jp/guide/)

### Sacred Tree (Camphor Tree)
* **【Features】:** A massive camphor tree over 300 years old, with roots firmly planted in the shrine grounds, warmly watching over all who visit.
* **【Deep Background and Origins】:** Camphor trees have long been used as insect repellent and medicine (camphor), and are seen as a symbol of protecting and healing life. It's treasured as a being that richly carries the energy of "Wood" from the Yin-Yang Five Elements theory, conveying the vitality of the natural world to this day.
* **【Sun-chan's Top Pick Explanation】:** Sun-chan recommends gently placing both hands on the sacred tree and taking a deep breath! You'll feel the tree's warmth and the strength of the earth seep into you, fully recharging your energy ☀️
* **【Official Info】:** [Seimei Shrine Official Website (Shrine Grounds Guide)](https://www.seimeijinja.jp/guide/)

## ☀️ Mysterious Episodes and Charms Where Historical Romance Meets Modern Culture

Seimei Shrine is also an exciting spot where a thousand years of historical romance beautifully resonates with modern sports and pop culture!

### Ichijo Modoribashi Bridge and the Shikigami Statue
* **【Features】:** A miniature recreation of the Ichijo Modoribashi Bridge near the entrance of the shrine grounds, along with a stone statue of a shikigami (spirit servant) quietly standing beside it, charming yet mysterious.
* **【Deep Background and Origins】:** The real Ichijo Modoribashi Bridge was considered, in the Heian capital, to be the boundary between "this world" and "the otherworld." It's said that because Lord Seimei's wife was frightened by the strange appearance of his shikigami (the spirits and demons he commanded), he had the shikigami live beneath the bridge, using them to perform "hashiura," a fortune-telling practice that read the footsteps of people crossing the bridge to divine good or bad fortune. Abe no Seimei also appears in popular works such as "Fate/Grand Order," continuing to captivate many people today.
* **【Sun-chan's Top Pick Explanation】:** Take a commemorative photo with the shikigami statue, and let your imagination wander to the dramas of the onmyoji who were once active in the Heian capital! It makes the fantasy of history feel so much closer ☀️
* **【Official Info】:** [Seimei Shrine Official Website (Shrine Grounds Guide)](https://www.seimeijinja.jp/guide/)

### Mizukagami Mamori (Water Mirror Charm)
* **【Features】:** A stylish, dignified charm featuring a golden Seimei Kikyo shining crisply against a jet-black background.
* **【Deep Background and Origins】:** In the Yin-Yang Five Elements theory, the color "black" symbolizes the element of "Water." It carries the meaning of keeping your heart calm like the clear surface of the Seimei Well, sharpening your focus, and repelling misfortune and disaster.
* **【Sun-chan's Top Pick Explanation】:** This good luck item is perfect for anyone wanting to summon "this is the moment" focus for exam studies or daily work, or for anyone wanting to build an unshakeable sense of self without being swayed by surrounding noise ☀️
* **【Official Info】:** [Seimei Shrine Official Website (Charms and Amulets Guide)](https://www.seimeijinja.jp/omamori/)

### Katsu Mamori (Victory Charm)
* **【Features】:** An extremely popular charm among visitors, featuring a five-pointed star set against a vivid red-and-white background.
* **【Deep Background and Origins】:** This charm gained major attention when Yuzuru Hanyu won gold at the 2018 PyeongChang Winter Olympics while skating to "SEIMEI," a program portraying Lord Seimei. It's believed to grant not only victory over others, but also the strong mental fortitude to "overcome your own weakness and self-indulgence."
* **【Sun-chan's Top Pick Explanation】:** This is a charm Sun-chan wholeheartedly recommends to everyone facing a decisive moment in life, whether it's an exam, a competition, a career change, or an important business negotiation!
