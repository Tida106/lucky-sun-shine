## 🐱 [The Great Reversal of the Zodiac Myth] Why the "Sleeping Cat" Guards the Most Sacred Ground! The "True Peace" Won by the Excluded Cat

In the folklore of the Chinese zodiac, the cat, who missed the banquet, was eternally excluded from the calendar, and has long been portrayed in folk tales as a "foolish loser." In Fruits Basket too, Kyo Sohma, possessed by the spirit of the cat, was forced to carry the full weight of his family's prejudice, isolation, and trauma.

And yet, at Nikko Toshogu Shrine, which enshrines Tokugawa Ieyasu, this very structure is completely overturned!

### The National Treasure "Sleeping Cat" (Nemuri-neko), and Coexistence with the Sparrows
* **【Features】:** A small wooden carving of a cat, perched on a lintel in the East Corridor of Toshogu Shrine, surrounded by peony flowers (crafted by the legendary master carver Hidari Jingoro). On its reverse side, two sparrows are carved, playing happily amid a bamboo grove.
* **【Deep Background and Origins】:** At Nikko Toshogu, the official animals of the Chinese zodiac are placed in "outer, public locations," such as the Five-Story Pagoda. And yet, standing guard overhead at the East Corridor—the single most important gate leading to Lord Ieyasu's mausoleum (his resting place, the Okusha)—is none other than the **cat**, the very animal excluded from the zodiac! The cat sleeps with its claws hidden and front paws braced, maintaining a quiet readiness to pounce at any moment. Precisely because the cat, which should by nature be a natural predator, is sound asleep, the fragile sparrows can enjoy their lives in peace. This can be read as a symbol of the peace the Tokugawa shogunate built: ending the bloody chaos of the Sengoku period and establishing over 260 years of peace under heaven ("Pax Tokugawa").
* **【Sun-chan's Top Pick Explanation】:** Isn't it the ultimate catharsis that the cat, excluded in myth, has risen to become the "highest-ranking guardian deity," protecting the inner sanctum of the nation's supreme deity (Tosho Daigongen) all on its own! This is a spot especially worth seeing for anyone who has ever been pushed out by society, or felt wounded by loneliness. You can receive, with your whole body, the warm, affirming energy of: "you don't have to deny yourself—you have a precious place that belongs only to you" ☀️
* **【Official Info】:** [Nikko Toshogu Shrine Official Website (Shrine Grounds Guide)](https://www.toshogu.jp/)

### The Five-Story Pagoda (Gojunoto) and the Zodiac Signs of the Three Tokugawa Shoguns
* **【Features】:** A graceful vermilion pagoda, about 36 meters tall (an Important Cultural Property), standing before the Omotemon Gate. It features a "shinbashira" (central pillar) structure that even inspired the vibration-damping technology used in the Tokyo Skytree.
* **【Deep Background and Origins】:** The twelve zodiac animals are carved around the pagoda's first level, following their proper directions. At the front, you'll find the Tiger, Rabbit, and Dragon, the birth-year zodiac signs of the three Tokugawa shoguns—Ieyasu, Hidetada, and Iemitsu—displayed prominently side by side. It seems to be a special arrangement, wishing for the lasting continuity of Tokugawa rule.
* **【Sun-chan's Top Pick Explanation】:** This is a place where you can feel the strength to carve out your own future through firm conviction and wisdom, no matter how harsh your fate or how great the adversity you face! It will give you a powerful push whenever you want to make your dreams come true, or want to step into a leadership role ☀️
* **【Official Info】:** [Nikko Toshogu Shrine Official Website (Five-Story Pagoda)](https://www.toshogu.jp/)

## 🐵 [The Three Wise Monkeys and Over 5,000 Carvings at the Sacred Stable] "See No Evil, Hear No Evil, Speak No Evil" Was Only Panel Two?! The Eight-Panel Story That Heals Your Life

The "Shinkyusha" (Sacred Stable) draws an especially large crowd among all the buildings at Toshogu Shrine. This building, where the shrine's sacred horse was cared for, is carved with the world-famous "Three Wise Monkeys" relief. But did you know that those three monkeys are actually just one part of a full eight-panel series depicting the course of a human life?

### The Sacred Stable and the Eight-Panel "Three Wise Monkeys" Life-Cycle Reliefs
* **【Features】:** The only building at the otherwise lavishly decorated Toshogu Shrine left in the warm, natural tone of unpainted wood. Wrapped around its lintel are eight connected carvings, personifying monkeys to depict "the growth and spiritual journey of a human life."
* **【Deep Background and Origins】:** These carvings were placed at the stable from the old belief that "monkeys heal and protect horses from illness." The eight-panel story closely mirrors the emotional growth process the characters in Fruits Basket go through as they overcome their trauma and worries.
  * **Panel 1 [A Mother's Love]:** A mother monkey gazes into the distance, cherishing her child's future (unconditional love and the beginning of life).
  * **Panel 2 [The Three Wise Monkeys (Childhood)]:** "See no evil, hear no evil, speak no evil." A teaching that, during the impressionable years of childhood, a child should be shielded from exposure to malice and wrongdoing, so a pure foundation for the heart can grow.
  * **Panel 3 [Independence and Solitude]:** A young monkey sits deep in thought before setting out on its own (the solitude and anxiety of adolescence).
  * **Panel 4 [Great Ambitions]:** Two monkeys look up at the blue sky, holding great ambitions (the hope and forward momentum of youth).
  * **Panel 5 [Setback and Friendship]:** A friend gently rests a hand on the back of a monkey who has been struck down by life's obstacles (setback, and the importance of the empathy and friendship that supports us through it).
  * **Panel 6 [Love and Inner Conflict]:** A monkey hugs its knees, troubled by the pain of romantic worry (the anxiety and bittersweetness of love).
  * **Panel 7 [The Rough Waves of Marriage]:** Fierce waves are carved at the feet of a devoted monkey couple (the resolve to face life's hardships and joys together, hand in hand).
  * **Panel 8 [New Life]:** A mother monkey, now pregnant. And the story gently circles back around to the mother's love of Panel 1.
* **【Sun-chan's Top Pick Explanation】:** "It's okay to let someone support your back when things are hard." "You don't have to carry life's rough waves alone—you can get through them hand in hand with someone important to you." Gentle messages like these come through from the wood carvings, and it makes my heart well up. The "friend quietly standing beside" in Panel 5 feels just like Tohru's warmth, wrapping around everyone who's been hurt! This is the ultimate good-luck therapy spot, loosening up a heart worn down by relationships, right down to its core ☀️
* **【Reference Info】:** [Nikko Tabi Navi (Nikko Toshogu Shrine, Sacred Stable and Three Wise Monkeys)](https://www.nikko-kankou.org/)

### Yomeimon Gate and the Cosmic Barrier of the North Star
* **【Features】:** A National Treasure nicknamed the "Twilight Gate" (Higurashi no Mon) because you never tire of looking at it, even until dusk. Over 500 sacred beasts and sages are carved into it without a single gap, including the "baku" who devours nightmares, and the "kirin," a messenger of peace.
* **【Deep Background and Origins】:** Tenkai (Jigen Daishi), who served as a religious advisor to the Edo shogunate, is said to have arranged for Toshogu Shrine to be positioned almost directly north of Edo Castle. True north is the direction of the "North Star" (Hokushin), the unmoving center of the night sky that never shifts. It's also said that there was an intention to unite "Tosho Daigongen," the deified form of Lord Ieyasu, with the sacred energy of the North Star.
* **【Sun-chan's Top Pick Explanation】:** Standing in front of Yomeimon Gate and gazing at the gate and main hall through the torii, you can feel an immense, cosmic-scale power, as if you've connected in a perfectly straight line with the very axis of the universe! It will grant you the strongest grounding power whenever you want to steady your own luck without wavering ☀️
* **【Official Info】:** [Nikko Toshogu Shrine Official Website (Yomeimon Gate)](https://www.toshogu.jp/)
