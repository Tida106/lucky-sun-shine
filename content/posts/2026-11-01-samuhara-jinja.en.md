In the middle of Osaka's Itachibori district stands **Samuhara Shrine**, known as a shrine of a deity of protection and for its charm rings inscribed with distinctive characters. This guide covers its background and what to know before you visit.

## What is Samuhara Shrine?

The shrine is in Nishi Ward, Osaka. Its roots lie in the **Inner Shrine (Oku-no-miya)** in Kamo, Tsuyama City, Okayama Prefecture. Together, the Osaka shrine and the Okayama Inner Shrine are known as Samuhara Shrine.

- **Address**: 2-5-6 Itachibori, Nishi-ku, Osaka 550-0012
- **Phone**: +81-6-6538-2251 (check hours and offerings on the official website or by phone)

## The deity: Samuhara Okami

Samuhara Shrine enshrines the **three creator deities**: Ame-no-Minakanushi, Takamimusubi and Kamimusubi. Together they are called "Samuhara Okami." The blessings said to be associated with them include protection, health, longevity and family prosperity.

## History

According to the shrine's official website:

- It carries on the tradition of an old shrine in Okayama.
- The first chief priest, Tanaka Tomisaburo, believed he had returned safely from the Sino-Japanese and Russo-Japanese wars and from a shipwreck thanks to the Samuhara talisman.
- In 1935 he rebuilt the neglected old shrine.
- In 1950 it became a branch of Toyokuni Shrine in Osaka.
- In 1961 it moved to its present location in Itachibori, Osaka.
- In 2005 the Okayama Inner Shrine, damaged by a typhoon, was moved to its present site.

The shrine is also described as having been used as a talisman against arrows and bullets in the Edo period, and as a charm for soldiers' good fortune in wartime.

## Charm rings and omamori

The **protective charm ring** inscribed with the Samuhara characters is widely known. These charms are not given out at the Okayama Inner Shrine and are said to be available only at the **Osaka shrine**. Procedures and quantities can change, so check the official website before you go.

## The Inner Shrine in Okayama (Kamo, Tsuyama)

The Inner Shrine is in Nakahara, Kamo-cho, Tsuyama City, Okayama.

- **By train**: about 35 minutes on foot from Mimasaka-Kamo Station on the JR Inbi Line. Trains are infrequent, so check the timetable in advance.
- **By car**: about 35 minutes from Tsuyama IC on the Chugoku Expressway. Parking is available.
- There are many stone steps, so wear comfortable shoes.
- Part of the stairs leading to the original shrine is damaged and generally closed to visitors.
- It is said to be good to also visit the neighboring Kotohira Shrine.

Hours may change, so check with the Tsuyama tourist association before going.

## Summary

- Samuhara Shrine worships the three creator deities as "Samuhara Okami" and is known as a shrine of protection.
- There is a shrine in Osaka and its roots, the Inner Shrine in Okayama.
- The charm ring is said to be given out at the Osaka shrine.
- Check hours and conditions on the official website before visiting.
