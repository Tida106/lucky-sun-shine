## Introduction

Udo Jingu in Nichinan, Miyazaki, is **a shrine whose main hall stands inside a sea cave** carved by waves into a cliff facing the Pacific. You walk *down* stone steps along the cliff to reach it (a style called "kudari-miya"), and the sight of red-lacquered buildings against the blue sea is considered rare in Japan.

Three things worth knowing before you go:

- **The highlight is "unedama" (luck-ball throwing):** you toss a small clay ball into a hollow on the sacred Turtle Rock in front of the main hall.
- **It is known for relationships, married harmony, conceiving, safe childbirth and child-raising.** It is easy to visit alone, as a couple, or to report a new beginning.
- **Parking is reported to be free (about 400 spaces).** The walk from the car park to the main hall is about 10-15 minutes, and the whole visit takes about 30 minutes to 1 hour.

## The cave shrine and its story

The main deity, Ugayafukiaezu, is said to be the father of Emperor Jimmu, traditionally regarded as Japan's first emperor. He is said to have been born in the birthing hut inside the cave before its roof of cormorant feathers was finished. His mother, the sea princess Toyotama-hime, is said to have left him and returned to the sea, pressing her breasts onto the rock for him; this is the legend of the "Ochichi-iwa" rock.

The cave holding the main hall is described as about 38 m east-west, 29 m north-south and 8.5 m high. The shrine is said to date from the reign of Emperor Sujin, and locals call it "Udo-san."

## What visitors hope to gain (traditionally said)

These are commonly told associations, not guaranteed effects.

- **Relationships and married harmony:** linked to the story of Toyotama-hime and her husband.
- **Safe childbirth, conceiving and child-raising:** people who are expecting or raising children visit because of the Ochichi-iwa legend.
- **A wish you act out:** throwing the luck ball is a hands-on way to put a wish into words.
- **A scene far from daily life:** the approach between cave and sea suits anyone wanting to reset.

## Luck-ball throwing and Ochichi-iwa

In **unedama**, you throw a clay ball into the hollow on the back of the sacred "Turtle Rock" on the shore in front of the hall. If it lands inside, the wish is said to come true. **Men throw with the left hand and women with the right**, as the shrine's guidance is reported. The balls are sold at the amulet office (about 200 yen for five, reportedly). A gentle arc is said to work better than a hard throw.

**Ochichi-iwa** is a rock beside the main hall shaped like a breast, with water dripping slowly down it. A candy made with this water, "ochichi-ame," is a known offering, and people who are expecting or raising children favour it.

## Suggested route and timing

Times are rough estimates.

1. **Walk from the car park to the gate and tower gate** (about 10-15 min)
2. **Cross Tama Bridge, go down the steps and pray at the main hall** (about 10-15 min)
3. **Luck-ball throwing and Ochichi-iwa** (about 10-15 min)
4. **Walk back, enjoying the sea views** (about 10-15 min)

**Allow about 30 minutes to 1 hour.** The return is uphill, so extra time helps.

## Tips

- **Go early in the morning:** hours are reported as 6:00-18:00, and quieter times let you feel the cave's calm.
- **Wear comfortable shoes:** there are many steps and spray can make them wet.
- **Do not climb down onto the rocks:** stay on the approach and avoid the area around Turtle Rock.
- **New Year and holiday weekends are crowded**, and nearby roads can back up; early mornings and weekdays are easier.
- **Amulets and offerings:** buy luck balls and ochichi-ame at the amulet office during opening hours.

## Access

- **By car:** about 40 minutes from Miyazaki Airport and about 50 minutes from Miyazaki IC, reportedly. Parking is about 400 spaces, free.
- **Train and bus:** from JR Aburatsu Station, take a bus (about 20 min) to "Udo Jingu," then walk about 10 minutes. Buses are infrequent; check times in advance.

## Basic etiquette

1. Bow before passing under the torii and gate.
2. Walk near the edge of the approach; the center is said to be for the gods.
3. Bow twice, clap twice, bow once.
4. Wait your turn at luck-ball throwing and throw quietly, away from other visitors.
5. In the cave, do not point flashes or lights at worshippers.

## FAQ

**How long should I allow?** About 30 minutes to 1 hour, a little more if you enjoy luck-ball throwing and photos.

**How many luck balls, and how do I throw?** Five for about 200 yen, reportedly. Men throw left-handed and women right-handed, aiming for the hollow on Turtle Rock.

**Is the car park far?** The walk to the main hall is described as about 10-15 minutes.

**Can I go alone?** Of course. Many visit to report a new start, not only for relationships.
