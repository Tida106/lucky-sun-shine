Hello! This is the Lucky Sun Shine editorial team ☀️

On long autumn nights, temples and shrines across Kyoto hold "special evening openings" with night illumination. Social media is full of beautiful photos, but these events are about more than a pretty picture: **knowing what the light is meant to express can change how you see it.**

This guide covers five places: Kiyomizu-dera, Kodai-ji, Entoku-in, Shoren-in Monzeki and Kifune Shrine. We kept our "meaning of the light" angle, and added what you actually need to plan a visit: **dates, hours, fees and access.**

> **Please note:** Dates are as of October 18, 2026. Where we could not confirm a date on the official website, we say where it was listed. Past years are given as a rough guide, always with the year. Please check each official website before you go.

## 1. Kiyomizu-dera: a single beam of blue light said to represent Kannon's compassion 💙

Kiyomizu-dera holds evening openings with illumination every spring, summer and autumn.
The highlight is **a single beam of blue light said to represent Kannon's compassion**, which is how the temple's own description puts it.

Knowing that the blue light is meant to express compassion can turn "that's pretty" into something quieter.

In our editorial view, this is the place for **days when you have been pushing yourself too hard**. Walking the grounds at night and looking up at the blue light may be a good way to let your shoulders drop.

### Basic information (Kiyomizu-dera)

- **2026 autumn dates:** **Nov 21 (Sat) – Nov 30 (Mon), 2026**, 5:30 pm – 9:00 pm (admission ends 9:00 pm) (per the Kyoto City official tourism site). As in other years, it is timed to the autumn colors.
- **Past results (autumn 2025):** Nov 22 – Dec 7, 2025, 5:30 pm – 9:00 pm (admission ends 9:00 pm). No reservation was required in 2025. Please check the official website to see whether a reservation is needed in 2026.
- **Fee:** 500 JPY for adults, 200 JPY for elementary and junior high students.
- **Address:** 1-294 Kiyomizu, Higashiyama-ku, Kyoto
- **Access:** Please check the official website.
- **Official websites:** [Kiyomizu-dera official (English)](https://www.kiyomizudera.or.jp/en/ "newtab") / [Events (Japanese)](https://www.kiyomizudera.or.jp/event/ "newtab") / [News (Japanese)](https://www.kiyomizudera.or.jp/news "newtab")

## 2. Kodai-ji: light reflected on the water, and a quiet mind reset 🌌

The night highlight at Kodai-ji is **the mirror-like surface of Garyochi Pond (臥龍池)**, where the illuminated scenery reflects on the water.

There is also a projection-mapping show in the garden in front of the Hojo hall (波心庭). The show changes from year to year, so **we have not confirmed what it will be in autumn 2026.**

In our editorial view, looking at the reflections without thinking about anything is a good fit for **times when you want to reset your head**. This is simply our impression.

### Basic information (Kodai-ji)

- **2026 dates:** The autumn evening opening runs **Oct 23 (Fri) – Dec 13 (Sun), 2026** (per the official website).
- **Hours:** 9:00 am to 9:30 pm (last admission), gates close at 10:00 pm. **The lights come on at 5:00 pm.**
- **Fee:** 800 JPY for adults, 400 JPY for junior high and high school students. The fee is the same day and night (per the official admission page).
- **Re-entry:** Entry is not time-slotted, but **once you leave through the exit, you cannot re-enter with the same ticket** (per the official website).
- **Access:** About a 15-minute walk from Keihan Gion-Shijo Station. Or take a city bus to Higashiyama-Yasui and walk about 7 minutes.
- **Reservations:** Please check the official website to see whether a reservation is needed.
- **Address:** 526 Shimokawara-cho, Higashiyama-ku, Kyoto
- **Official websites (Japanese):** [Kodai-ji official](https://www.kodaiji.com/ "newtab") / [Evening opening](https://www.kodaiji.com/saiji.html "newtab") / [Access](https://www.kodaiji.com/houmon.html "newtab") / [Admission](https://www.kodaiji.com/haikan.html "newtab")

## 3. Entoku-in: a sub-temple next to Kodai-ji, linked to Nene 🏯

Entoku-in is a sub-temple (*tatchu*) right next to Kodai-ji. It is known as the place where Kita-no-Mandokoro Nene spent her later years.

Because it is so close to Kodai-ji, it works well as the final stop on a night when you want a little more light.

### Basic information (Entoku-in)

- **2026 autumn dates:** **Oct 23 (Fri) – Dec 13 (Sun), 2026** (listed on a travel information site; please confirm with the official website). This is the same period as Kodai-ji.
- **Past results (autumn 2025):** Oct 24 – Dec 14, 2025, 10:00 am – 9:30 pm (admission ends 9:30 pm), lights on at 5:00 pm.
- **Fee:** A combined ticket for the two temples (Kodai-ji and Entoku-in) is listed at **1,200 JPY for adults** (a 2026 travel-agency listing; we recommend confirming with the temples).
- **Access:** Right next to Kodai-ji.
- **Address:** 530 Kodaiji Shimokawara-cho, Higashiyama-ku, Kyoto
- **Official website (Japanese):** [Entoku-in official](https://www.kodaiji.com/entoku-in/ "newtab")

## 4. Shoren-in Monzeki: an evening lit by a principal deity described as "light itself" 💎

Shoren-in Monzeki's official website says it holds special evening openings with illumination every spring and autumn.

The temple explains why light matters here.
Its principal deity (熾盛光如来) is described as **"light itself."** Its manifestation, Fudo Myoo (不動明王), is also said to be surrounded by flames of light. The temple enshrines the National Treasure known as "Blue Fudo" (青不動).

The illumination uses **about 1,000 lighting fixtures of various sizes** to light the stroll garden and the grounds. The highlights are five camphor trees designated as natural monuments, a bamboo grove lit in blue, and a moss garden (all per the temple's explanation).

In our editorial view, this is a place **for people who would rather soak in the light quietly than join a crowd.**

### Basic information (Shoren-in Monzeki)

- **2026 autumn dates:** **Nov 6 (Fri) – Dec 6 (Sun), 2026**, 6:00 pm – 10:00 pm (last admission 9:30 pm), 1,500 JPY (listed by JR Central's "Sou da, Kyoto, Ikou." campaign). Some information sites give Nov 7 as the start, so please check the [official website](https://www.shorenin.com/night/ "newtab") before you go.
- **For reference (summer 2026):** Jul 17 – Aug 23, 2026 (except Aug 13–16), 7:00 pm – 9:30 pm (admission ends 9:00 pm), 1,500 JPY for adults and 1,000 JPY for elementary students (official).
- **Past results (autumn 2025):** Nov 7 – Dec 7, 2025, the first time in five years (official announcement). Hours were 6:00 pm – 10:00 pm (last admission 9:30 pm), and the fee was 1,000 JPY (travel information site).
- **Access and reservations:** Please check the official website.
- **Address:** Awataguchi Sanjobo-cho, Higashiyama-ku, Kyoto
- **Official websites:** [Shoren-in Monzeki official (English)](https://www.shorenin.com/english/ "newtab") / [Evening opening (Japanese)](https://www.shorenin.com/night/ "newtab")

## 5. Kifune Shrine: a forest at night, in the home shrine of water deities 🌿

Last is Kifune Shrine, in the mountains of Kyoto. It is **the head shrine of water deities**, and is also known for blessings related to relationships and love (*en-musubi*).

In some years, the area has been lit as "Kifune Maple Lantern Illumination" (京の奥座敷・貴船もみじ灯篭), covering the area around Kifune Shrine and the roughly 250 m "maple tunnel" of the Eizan Electric Railway. This is based on past events. A travel information site lists 2026 dates, but **we have not been able to confirm them on the official website.**

In our editorial view, this is a good fit **when you want to regain some energy** in the quiet of the mountains, away from the city.

### Basic information (Kifune Shrine)

- **Main shrine hours:** 6:00 am – 8:00 pm (May 1 – Nov 30), open every day, free to visit (official).
- **Autumn illumination:** A travel information site lists **Nov 7 – 24, 2026** (other details not confirmed). In 2025: Nov 7 – 24, from sunset to about 8:30 pm (travel information site). The best colors are usually early to late November. **We could not confirm this on the official website, so please check [news](https://kifunejinja.jp/news "newtab") before you go.**
- **Access (official):** About a 30-minute walk (2 km) from Kibuneguchi Station on the Eizan Electric Railway. Or change at Kibuneguchi to the Kyoto Bus (No. 33), get off at "Kifune", and walk about 5 minutes.
- **Parking:** Limited (a travel information site lists 18 spaces). Expect heavy crowds in the autumn-color season.
- **Address:** 180 Kuramakifune-cho, Sakyo-ku, Kyoto
- **Official websites:** [Kifune Shrine official (English)](https://kifunejinja.jp/en/ "newtab") / [Visitor information (Japanese)](https://kifunejinja.jp/info/ "newtab") / [Access (Japanese)](https://kifunejinja.jp/access/ "newtab") / [News (Japanese)](https://kifunejinja.jp/news "newtab")

## Sample itinerary: split the nights and take it slowly

We do not recommend trying all five places in one night, because they lie in different directions. We suggest splitting them like this:

- **Night 1 (southern Higashiyama): Kiyomizu-dera → Kodai-ji → Entoku-in**
  These three are in the same part of Higashiyama, so you can link them in one evening. Kodai-ji's lights come on at 5:00 pm, so starting early is one option. However, Kiyomizu-dera's autumn opening is only Nov 21 – 30, so **if you want to link all three in one evening, plan within those dates** (Kodai-ji and Entoku-in run Oct 23 – Dec 13).
- **Another night (northern Higashiyama): Shoren-in Monzeki**
  Shoren-in Monzeki is in northern Higashiyama. Since it is a place to take in the light slowly, we suggest keeping this night for Shoren-in alone.
- **Another day: Kifune Shrine**
  Kifune Shrine is in the mountains, in a different direction. The main shrine is open until 8:00 pm (May 1 – Nov 30), so **plan to arrive early** and leave yourself time. Please also check the bus and train schedules in the official access information in advance.

*We have not included walking times between places, because we could not confirm them.*

## Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Do I need a reservation?

It depends on the place. At Kiyomizu-dera, no reservation was needed in autumn 2025 (a 2025 result). For the other places, please check each official website. The main shrine at Kifune Shrine is free to visit.

### Q2. Can I leave and come back in?

At Kodai-ji, entry is not time-slotted, but **once you leave through the exit, you cannot re-enter with the same ticket** (per the official website). For the other places, please check the official websites.

### Q3. What should I wear?

This is our editorial tip, not official guidance. Some places can feel cooler at night than during the day. A layer you can put on and comfortable shoes are a good idea. If you are heading into the mountains, like Kifune Shrine, please take extra care with your footing.

### Q4. What happens if it rains?

Whether an event is held or changed is announced on each official website, so please check the news there. If you go out in the rain, an umbrella and non-slip shoes are a good idea (our editorial tip).

### Q5. How do I get to Kifune Shrine?

According to the official information: about a 30-minute walk (2 km) from Kibuneguchi Station on the Eizan Electric Railway. Or change at Kibuneguchi to the Kyoto Bus (No. 33), get off at "Kifune", and walk about 5 minutes. Parking is limited, so public transport is the safer choice in the autumn-color season.

### Q6. Where can I check the 2026 autumn dates?

The 2026 autumn dates are out for Kiyomizu-dera (Nov 21 – 30), Kodai-ji (Oct 23 – Dec 13) and Shoren-in Monzeki (Nov 6 – Dec 6). For Entoku-in and Kifune Shrine, the dates come from travel information sites, so please confirm them officially. Dates can change, so check [events](https://www.kiyomizudera.or.jp/event/ "newtab") and [news](https://www.kiyomizudera.or.jp/news "newtab") for Kiyomizu-dera, the [evening opening page](https://www.shorenin.com/night/ "newtab") for Shoren-in Monzeki, and [news](https://kifunejinja.jp/news "newtab") for Kifune Shrine before you go (all in Japanese).
