## 1. 🦊 Fox (Inari Shrine) | The Key to Opening the Door to Abundant Harvests and Good Luck!

The fox, familiar at Inari shrines, is the messenger of the deity of food and agriculture, "Ukanomitama-no-okami" ✨
Since ancient times, foxes have been appreciated beings who came down from the mountains to the villages to exterminate pests that ravaged precious crops. Because their golden tails resemble ripened ears of rice, they have been loved as a symbol of abundance 🌾✨

If you look closely at the foxes (white foxes) at the shrine, you'll see they are holding wonderful items in their mouths or by their paws 🐾 Each one contains amazing good luck power!

  - **Ear of Rice (Inaho)**: A prosperous life without food worries & flourishing business 🌾
  - **Sacred Jewel (Hoju)**: A magical ball that grants any of your wishes ✨
  - **Scroll (Makimono)**: Supreme wisdom and grateful guidance from the gods 📜
  - **Key (Kagi)**: The key that opens the storehouse of abundance! It is also said to be the **"key to attracting wonderful relationships"** 🔑💖

When you visit an Inari shrine, definitely check out what the foxes are holding in their hands with excitement ☀️

## 2. 🦌 Deer (Kasuga Taisha) | The Healing Sacred Deer That Arrived Carrying a Deity

The deer you can meet at shrines like Kasuga Taisha in Nara are cherished as highly revered **"Shinroku" (sacred deer)** ✨

Long ago, there's a legend that when the powerful deity (Takemikazuchi-no-mikoto) at Kashima Jingu in Ibaraki Prefecture headed for Mount Kasuga in Nara, he **"traveled riding on the back of a dazzling white deer"**! You can feel the mystery of great nature, can't you? 💙

Deer are the vehicles of the gods and special beings carrying the gentle hearts of the deities. Carefully protected for over a thousand years, the deer of Nara continue to live on cherished as a genetically distinct group 🐾
Just meeting a deer looking gently at you with its round eyes will give you the ultimate healing power that melts away daily stress ✨ They are the perfect messengers for when you want to heighten your intuition or calm your mind ☀️

## 3. 🕊️ Dove (Hachimangu) | Peaceful Love and Guidance to Victory!

The adorable doves are highly active at Hachimangu shrines nationwide ✨
Surprisingly, there is a legend that when Hachiman-sama first appeared, he transformed into a golden dove and descended ☀️ Since then, they have caused miracles in important scenes, such as a white dove guiding the way when the shrine was being relocated.

When you visit a Hachimangu, please take a close look at the tablet (the framed sign) on the shrine building! There are many shrines (like Iwashimizu Hachimangu and Tsurugaoka Hachimangu) where the character for "Hachi" (八) in "Hachimangu" is **designed as the silhouette of two cute doves facing each other** 🕊️💖

These two doves form a perfectly synchronized "A-un" (Alpha and Omega) pair, warding off evil and watching over peace and family safety. Also, adorable clay dolls of "Mibato" (sacred doves) are popular as charms for the healthy growth of children and to soothe tantrums (warding off "bugs"). They are a highly notable messenger when you want to boost your luck in competitions or restore smiles and peace to your home 💙

## 4. 🦅 Crow (Kumano Sanzan) | The Three-Legged Yatagarasu Illuminating a Bright Future

Many people might be surprised to hear, "Are crows actually the ultimate guides of good luck!?" ✨ Enshrined at Kumano Sanzan (Kumano Hongu Taisha, Kumano Hayatama Taisha, Kumano Nachi Taisha) in Wakayama Prefecture is the **legendary sacred bird with three legs, "Yatagarasu"** ☀️

There remains a myth that when Japan's first emperor (Emperor Jimmu) got lost in the rugged mountains of Kumano, a Yatagarasu sent from heaven guided the way, admirably leading him to victory and safety ✨
The "3 legs" of the Yatagarasu have a very deep meaning 🐾

  - **Heaven (Gods)**
  - **Earth (Nature)**
  - **Humans (Us humanity)**

It represents a world where these three are harmoniously connected like brothers under the sun ☀️ The Yatagarasu is an "incarnation of the sun," and when you are lost in life's choices or taking a step towards a new challenge, it is a reliable lucky partner that will unerringly guide you to the best future ✨

## 5. 🐮 Cow (Tenmangu) | The Soothing "Stroking Cow" and the God of Learning

At Tenmangu shrines, which enshrine Sugawara no Michizane (Tenjin-sama), famous as the god of learning, there are many figures of leisurely lying down "cows (Gagyu)" 🐮✨

Lord Michizane had a very deep connection with cows, and his birth year and month were also the year of the Ox. It is said that when Lord Michizane passed away, the ox carrying his remains suddenly lay down on the spot and refused to move. The people, realizing "This is Lord Michizane's will," built his tomb at that very location (the current Dazaifu Tenmangu), which is how it all began ✨
A characteristic of the cows at Tenmangu is that they are not standing, but all gently lying on the ground. They are a symbol of calming fierce storms and bringing peaceful tranquility to the heart 💙

And the cows in the precincts are hugely popular as **"Nade-ushi" (Stroking Cows)**!

  - **Gently stroking the head**: Grants the power of clever wisdom and academic success ✨
  - **Stroking the same part of the cow as the part of your body that feels unwell**: Grants health and healing ✨

If you feel "I've been accumulating fatigue lately...", definitely try gently stroking the cow to refresh both your mind and body ☀️️
